# 16.1 · The call

A 30-person amuta that accompanies families raising disabled children has asked you to "help us use AI — we have no budget". You are a volunteer: no fee, no contract worth the name, no authority, and 40 hours spread over four weeks. Everything you learned in Stage 1 still applies, but you cannot afford to do any of it at full size. In this lesson you write a one-page charter with the executive director, decide how to spend your hours before you spend any, and read the data room on the first day. At the end you have a signed one-page charter, an hour budget, an urgent-finding rule and a first-read sheet.

## The situation

Sunday 15 November 2026, 09:00, the Open Hands office in Kiryat Ata. Read the [engagement brief](../../labs/module-16/brief.md) first (in the module download). In short: Open Hands accompanies about 440 families across Haifa Bay, in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and Amharic. Thirty staff (about 19 full-time equivalents), about 90 active volunteers, fourteen institutional funders, a ₪6.1M budget, Google Workspace for Nonprofits since 2019, and Yossi, who "does IT" on Tuesdays. On 27 October the board chair came back from a conference, said every nonprofit uses AI, and the board decided: find pro bono help, report on 9 December, no budget.

Mahut Pro Bono matched you with the executive director, Dr. Ronit Azoulay. You have 30 minutes with her before her meeting with the municipality.

You have entered an organization three ways so far: as a consultant with a fee (Northwind, [08.1](../module-08/lesson-01.md)), as an employee who will own what you approve (Lumen, 14.1), and here as a volunteer. IBM's consulting certificate calls the framing of an engagement and its *advisory boundary* the first job of the advisor; [01.1](../module-01/lesson-01.md) turned it into documents. Here every document has to fit on one page, because nobody will read two.

## What to do

### Step 1 — Write down what being a volunteer changes

Fill the entry-role table from [01.1](../module-01/lesson-01.md) for Open Hands before anything else. The rows are the same as at Northwind; the answers are not.

| | What it means here | What you write into the charter |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority** | None, and no money either. Your only currency is being useful in week 1 | "Changes are made by Open Hands staff (Yossi, Hana, Noa) with my help, on Ronit's yes" |
| **The contract** | Mahut's volunteer agreement: confidentiality, "advice, not legal advice". No exits, no change requests: nobody is paying for a phase | One page signed by email; the hour budget instead of a SOW |
| **Your clock** | 40 hours, mostly evenings and two Tuesdays | Every step sized in hours before you start (Step 3) |
| **Who maintains it** | Yossi, one day a week, after 10 December. Nobody else | "Nothing is built that Yossi cannot run in 15 minutes a month" |
| **The people affected** | Children and their families, who never asked for AI and whose information is the most sensitive the organization holds | The urgent-finding rule (Step 2) and a family's voice in discovery |
| **The people you work with** | Overloaded staff, six of them social workers bound by professional confidentiality, and volunteers | Non-attribution, said out loud at the staff meeting |

The row people get wrong is the fourth. A volunteer consultant who builds something clever (a workflow in a tool only they understand, a script, an automation with four connected services) leaves on 10 December and the organization inherits a thing it cannot fix. At a 30-person nonprofit, "can the person who does IT on Tuesdays run this?" is a design constraint as hard as the data boundary.

### Step 2 — Read back the objective, and put "safely" where she wants it

Use the read-back from [01.2](../module-01/lesson-02.md). Ronit's version on the call was "show us how to use the free tools for grants and paperwork, so my social workers spend time with families, not with reports". Read it back as a sentence she can correct. In the worked example she corrected one word order: "safely" first. Keep her correction; it tells you what she will judge you on.

Two objectives exist again, as at Northwind, but this time they do not conflict, they differ in size:

- **Ronit's** (read back): "By 10 December, Open Hands uses the tools it already has for grant writing and paperwork, safely first, and the board has a clear plan."
- **The board's** (minutes): "Help us use AI. We have no budget." Write it as the *stated ask this engagement tests*. Two of its words are claims: "use" (as if nobody does yet) and "no budget" (as if no money is spent).

Then write the **urgent-finding rule**, before you open a single file: if you find family or donor information somewhere it should not be, Ronit hears it the same day, in person or by phone, and Yossi may fix it at once with her yes. At a nonprofit holding children's medical information, the worst outcome of a four-week engagement is finding an exposure in week 1 and reporting it in week 4.

Write "done" as five checkable things: exposures found are closed the same week; two workflows tried by their users on organization accounts, with their verdict; one page of rules; a board item asking for one decision; Yossi and Ronit can keep it running without you.

### Step 3 — Budget the hours before you spend them

This is the step a paid consultant does in a pricing spreadsheet and a volunteer usually skips. Do it in ten minutes. Start from the Stage 1 method and give every step a size:

| Week | Hours | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 12 | First-day read (3), seven 30-minute interviews (4) plus notes (2), staff meeting (1), the same-day note if needed (2) |
| 2 | 10 | The access fix with Yossi (4), diagnosis and the funder-call page (3), the volunteer lawyer (2), offers check (1) |
| 3 | 10 | Build and test two workflows with their users (7), the one-page policy (2), the treasurer (1) |
| 4 | 8 | Board pre-read (3), rehearsal (1), board (1), handover (3) |

Lesson 16.4 is about compression in detail. For now, notice what the table already decided: there is **no time** for a full shadow-AI census, a readiness assessment, a scoring workshop or an ROI model at Harbor & Pine size. There **is** time for every conversation with a person and for every hour with Yossi in the admin console. That is not an accident: those are the steps you cannot shrink.

### Step 4 — Read the data room on the first day

Follow [01.5](../module-01/lesson-05.md). Open the [INDEX](../../labs/module-16/data-room/INDEX.md). Three things about this data room change how you read everything:

- **Nobody exported it from a system.** Yossi pulled it by hand from Drive and the Workspace admin console, and pseudonymised it by hand. Expect gaps and leaks.
- **The time data is one week**, chosen by Ronit "because that is when it hurts". You will need to say what it is a sample of.
- **The census already exists, in a weak form.** Ronit's own "Do you use AI?" form ([01.6](../module-01/lesson-06.md) calls it a survey; treat it as a self-report with non-response). Volunteers were never asked.

Then sort the headline claims. A worked example from Open Hands' files:

| Claim | Where | Sort | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| "We have no budget" | Board minutes, item 3 | **Contradicted** | `finance/card-statement-2026.csv`: about ₪9,190 of software January–October, including a ₪2,072 Microsoft charge and a monthly OpenAI charge coded "Office supplies" |
| "Half my week goes on reports" | Board minutes (Noa) | **Claim to test** | `people/time-log-2026-10.csv` has one week, the week before a deadline |
| "Only the family coordinators and the head of family services have access to family files" | Privacy policy 2019, §3 | **Contradicted** | `it/drive-sharing-audit.csv`: the `Families` drive has 46 accounts |
| "Database registered, no. 700418562" | Privacy policy, §2 | **Unverifiable**, possibly obsolete | Amendment 13 narrowed registration (in force 14 August 2025); a counsel question |
| Three staff pasted family information "with details" | Staff survey | **Reported** | 22 of 30 answered; volunteers were not asked |
| Adler-Haddad report ≈ 90 hours | `fundraising/funders.csv` | **Claim to test** | The log shows 57.8 hours in one week; how many weeks a quarter? |
| "Gemini is not included for nonprofits" | Yossi's IT notes (2023) | **Out of date until checked** | Check the vendor's own page in 16.4 |
| Microsoft licences "from the grant" | Yossi's notes | **Contradicted** | `vendor/microsoft-renewal-notice.md`: the grant ended at the 14 September renewal |

What is missing: a normal week's time log, anything about volunteers' tools and WhatsApp, what was uploaded to which AI tool, any legal opinion. You will not get most of it from files. You will get it by asking people to show you their work.

### Step 5 — Agree it with Ronit, in 30 minutes

Interview Ronit (roleplay: [Dr. Ronit Azoulay](../../labs/module-16/personas/ronit-executive-director.md); no-roleplay path: [the transcript](../../labs/module-16/personas/transcripts/ronit-executive-director.md)). What you want out of the meeting: the objective read back; done; the out-of-scope list with an owner per item; the urgent-finding rule; non-attribution for the staff meeting; introductions to a family (through its coordinator, only if they want) and, later, the largest funder. Ask about families and staff before tools; she will cool if you say "AI strategy". Ask how donor stories are written: it unlocks something the transcript's interviewer never found. Then paste the transcript into the [coach prompt](../../labs/common/coach-prompt.md) with the persona's checklist.

## Watch out

- **Writing the board's ask as your objective.** "Use AI" is satisfied by a demo. Ronit's sentence is satisfied only if staff work differently and nothing bad happens to a family.
- **Skipping the charter because it is pro bono.** Unpaid work without a written scope grows until you quit. One page signed by email protects both of you.
- **Leaving the urgent-finding rule until you find something.** By then you will be tempted to "finish the analysis first".
- **Spending your hours on documents.** Forty hours vanish into a polished readiness assessment nobody at a 30-person nonprofit will read. Spend them with people and with Yossi.
- **Treating the volunteers as outside scope.** A third of the people with access to family files are volunteers on personal accounts.
- **Promising a tool on day one.** Ronit wants something staff can use by January. Promise to find out what is safe first.

## Your turn

### Inputs

- [Engagement brief](../../labs/module-16/brief.md) and the [data room](../../labs/module-16/data-room/INDEX.md) (module download): especially `company/board-minutes-2026-10.md`, `company/budget-2026.md`, `policies/privacy-and-confidentiality-policy-2019.md`, `it/staff-ai-survey-2026-10.csv`, `it/yossi-it-notes.md`, `finance/card-statement-2026.csv`.
- Persona: [Dr. Ronit Azoulay](../../labs/module-16/personas/ronit-executive-director.md) or [her transcript](../../labs/module-16/personas/transcripts/ronit-executive-director.md); [coach prompt](../../labs/common/coach-prompt.md).
- Templates (use them shrunk to one page): [engagement charter](../../templates/engagement-charter.md), [scope boundary](../../templates/scope-boundary.md), [data-room first read](../../templates/data-room-first-read.md), [30-60-90 plan](../../templates/30-60-90-plan.md).

### Steps

1. Fill the entry-role table for a volunteer (six rows).
2. Interview Ronit (or read the transcript and mark what the interviewer found and missed). Read the objective back.
3. Write the one-page charter: objective, the stated ask as a claim, done (five checkable items), out of scope with an owner each, the urgent-finding rule, non-attribution.
4. Write the 40-hour budget by week and step.
5. Do the first read: sort at least eight claims with their evidence; list what is missing.
6. Write the first week day by day, with hours.

### Time

75 minutes: 15 for the interview, 25 for the charter and hour budget, 25 for the first read, 10 for the week plan.

### Done when

- [ ] The objective is Ronit's, read back, with "safely" where she put it; the board's ask appears as the claim tested.
- [ ] "Done" has five items a stranger could check on 10 December.
- [ ] The urgent-finding rule names who hears it, how and when.
- [ ] The hour budget adds up to 40 and puts time with people and with Yossi ahead of documents.
- [ ] At least eight claims are sorted with evidence, including "no budget" and the access rule in the privacy policy.
- [ ] The first week reaches a coordinator, development, IT, a volunteer and (through Ronit) a family.
