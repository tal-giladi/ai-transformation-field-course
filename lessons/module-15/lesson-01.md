# 15.1 · The call

The CEO told the board "we have 40 pilots and nothing in production", the board approved a 16-person AI centre of excellence and ₪11M, and you are its first hire. In this lesson you work out what being a new, senior, powerless head of a CoE in a regulated bank changes, get a mandate from your sponsor that keeps the CEO's headline as a claim to test, read the data room on the first day, and plan three weeks. At the end you have a mandate memo, a first-read sheet, an access request, a three-week plan and a short note to the CEO.

## The situation

Sunday 3 January 2027, 08:15, an empty office on the COO's floor in Ramat Gan. Read the [engagement brief](../../labs/module-15/brief.md) first (in the module download). In short: Crescent Bank is a mid-sized Israeli bank with about 4,000 people, 14 business units and 58 branches. On 24 November the CEO, Avner Golan, said "we have 40 pilots and nothing in production"; the board approved a central CoE of 16 positions under the COO and a ₪11.0M central AI programme for 2027, and Avner promised "at least ten in production by December 2027". You were hired from a fintech. You report to Dalit Ben-Ami, COO, who proposed the design. The Management Committee decides on 15 March; the board revises the budget on 30 March, with the CFO asking for 15% off all AI spend.

This is your seventh entry role. At Northwind ([08.1](../module-08/lesson-01.md)) you were an outside consultant; at Kinneret ([13.1](../module-13/lesson-01.md)) an internal lead with a history. Here you are **inside, senior, new and without line authority**, in an organisation where every route to production runs through a committee you do not chair. IBM's operating-models course names the trap you are walking into: *operating-model drift* — the bank already has an operating model, it just never chose it. Its *CoE-bottleneck test* asks whether a centre ships production outcomes; the board has just approved a centre before anyone asked.

## What to do

### Step 1 — Write down what your entry role changes

Use the entry-role table from [01.1](../module-01/lesson-01.md). At Crescent it reads:

| | What it means here | What goes into the mandate |
|---|---|---|
| **Who gave the ask** | The CEO, at the board, with a number and a design already approved | The design is the starting point; changes go to Dalit first, never around her |
| **Authority** | None over 14 unit heads; you cannot stop a pilot; go-live is the Model Committee's (chaired by the CRO) | Every decision you will need is listed with its owner; you recommend |
| **Money** | ₪11.0M central, committed to a design; about ₪9M sits in the units; a 15% review is open | No new commitment above an agreed line before 15 March |
| **New and senior** | No history, no allies, no blame for the past; people will test you | Use it: ask what insiders can't, and say what you find to the sponsor first |
| **Regulated** | Banking Supervision directives, a survey answer already given to the supervisor | Regulatory readings go to the CRO, the CCO and counsel; your job is that they are asked |
| **You stay** | Whatever you design, you run | Every part has an owner who is not you, by a date |

The fourth row is the one new hires waste. In the first three weeks nobody holds the past against you; after that, everything you saw and did not say becomes yours.

### Step 2 — Get the objective from the sponsor, and keep the headline as a claim

Use [01.2](../module-01/lesson-02.md): the objective in the sponsor's words, read back, then "done". Two statements exist and the mandate holds both without pretending they are the same:

- **The stated ask** (CEO, board minute): "We have 40 pilots and nothing in production", with the remedy already chosen — a central team that "industrialises" the pilots. Record it as *the claim this work tests*. Both halves are testable: is it 40, and is it nothing?
- **The objective** you read back to Dalit: by 15 March, one recommendation for how AI gets from pilot to production at Crescent — which pilots go, in what order, who decides, what it costs — and a 12-month plan the board can follow every quarter.

Expect a correction. Dalit's in the expert version is "*and something Avner can say to the board on 30 March*". Write it in. A sponsor who adds her own constraint has started to own the objective.

Done on 15 March, checkable: a decision in the minutes with an owner and date per part; every pilot counted with an action and its evidence; the route to production agreed with the CRO before it reaches the committee; the CFO's 15% answered with a list; numbers labelled measured, directional or assumed; an inventory behind the board's first quarterly report that internal audit could test.

### Step 3 — Scope: every AI use, not the pilot list

Use the [scope boundary](../../templates/scope-boundary.md) from [01.3](../module-01/lesson-03.md). Three calls at Crescent:

- **The register or the bank?** The bank. The register is what units chose to send in December. The board asked for "the inventory of AI in use" in its first report; [01.7](../module-01/lesson-07.md) taught you that AI arrives in vendor releases, expense claims and side projects. Scope the work to every AI use and let the register be one source.
- **The CoE's design?** In. The board approved it, but the objective is about getting to production; the design is a means. Say so politely in the memo: "the approved design is the starting point".
- **Model development and vendor selection?** Out until 15 March. You will see weak models; your job now is to triage them, not to fix them.

### Step 4 — The advisory boundary in a bank

You will touch model risk, outsourcing, cloud, IT security, privacy and what the bank told its supervisor. You answer none of these yourself. Know the frame well enough to ask the right person:

- **Bank of Israel Proper Conduct of Banking Business Directive 369, Management of Model Risk** (circular 2792-06 of 21 August 2024; in force from 21 August 2025). It applies to AI models, asks for a model inventory, one owner per model, independent validation and effective challenge, and lets the bank apply it in proportion to a model's type, complexity and risk. Existing material models had six months; other existing models eighteen months, to **21 February 2027** — six weeks into your job. The CRO owns it.
- **Directive 310** (risk management), **359A** (outsourcing), **362** (cloud computing) and **364** (IT, information security and cyber risk, in force since 18 May 2026, replacing 357, 361 and 363). The CRO, the CIO and the CISO own them.
- **The inter-ministerial team's final report on AI in the financial sector** (December 2025; Ministry of Justice, Ministry of Finance, the Bank of Israel's Banking Supervision, the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority, the Israel Securities Authority, the Competition Authority): recommendations, not rules — board approval of AI use within risk appetite, an AI officer, validation before and during use, the ability to stop a system. The CCO owns the bank's reading.
- **The Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981 (חוק הגנת הפרטיות)** as amended by Amendment 13 (in force since 14 August 2025): the DPO owns it.

Write the boundary into the memo with names: model risk and the tier of any AI use → Dr. Irina Kogan (CRO); anything to the supervisor → Adv. Lina Nassar (CCO); privacy → Ruth Ben-David (DPO); platform and security → Nir Ashkenazi (CIO); contracts → procurement and counsel; internal audit → Tsega Alemu, who will listen and not advise.

### Step 5 — Read the data room on the first day

Follow [01.5](../module-01/lesson-05.md). Open the [INDEX](../../labs/module-15/data-room/INDEX.md). A worked example from Crescent's files:

| Statement | Where | Sort | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| "40 pilots" | Board minute; `ai/pilot-register-2026-12.csv` | **Claim to test** | 43 rows; Dalit's office compiled them in December and nobody compared them |
| "Nothing in production" | Board minute; survey answer 3.4 | **Claim to test** | Register notes: "Used in 31 branches", "copied into the Kesher note", "Live for 2% of web visitors" |
| "Ten in production by December 2027" | Board minute | **Claim to test** | Depends on a queue you have not measured |
| Every AI system needs full validation | `governance/ai-policy-v1-2025.md` §2 | **Verified** as the rule | The directive allows proportion; the policy does not |
| 3 AI review slots a quarter; full validation = 2 slots | `company/ai-programme-budget-2027.md`, `governance/mrm-workplan-2027.md` | **Verified** | 1.5 AI systems a quarter |
| "A central inventory lists every AI application with its owner" | `governance/bsd-ai-survey-response-2026-extract.md` 3.1 | **Claim to test** | The "inventory" is the pilot register |
| "Public generative AI is blocked" | Policy §6, survey 4.2 | **Unverifiable** today | You have no proxy data yet |
| The central team will build production versions | Programme budget, assumptions | **Claim to test** | Who validates what they build? |
| `depends_on` column | Register | **Verified** empty | 0 of 43 filled: dependencies unknown, not absent |

What is missing goes into the [access request pack](../../templates/access-request-pack.md): the proxy extract for AI hosts (CISO's office), card and expense lines booked to software (finance), AI contracts with renewal dates (procurement), MRM's model inventory (CRO), the Tzofen admin export and the Branch FAQ usage log (IT), IT's architecture notes, the people who work on AI in each unit (HR and unit heads).

### Step 6 — Plan three weeks, and meet the sponsor

Three weeks, not one: in a bank of this size you need the gatekeepers before the units, and the units before the design.

- **Week 1:** Dalit (Monday), the access request, the register against contracts, evals and the queue; a first note to Avner.
- **Week 2:** the gatekeepers and the units with the most at stake — Irina (CRO), Nir (CIO), Karim (Cards), Hagit (Retail).
- **Week 3:** internal audit, compliance, Operations and the Contact Centre, a branch; the findings memo on Thursday 21 January.

And the two dates you cannot miss: the CFO's memo on 12 January (comments by the 31st), and anything in the contracts with a notice date before 15 March.

Meet Dalit (roleplay: [Dalit Ben-Ami](../../labs/module-15/personas/dalit-coo.md); no-roleplay path: [the transcript](../../labs/module-15/personas/transcripts/dalit-coo.md)). What you want: the objective read back and corrected; done and scope agreed; access to every unit; how she wants to hear from you. Ask her who designed the CoE, and why the assistant is on the CFO's list. Afterwards run the transcript through the [coach prompt](../../labs/common/coach-prompt.md) with the persona's checklist.

> [!TIP]
> To the sponsor who wrote the design, "the approved design is our starting point; I'll test it against what I find and bring you anything that changes it before anyone else sees it" is true and keeps her with you. "I'd like to revisit the operating model" on day one sounds like "the board was wrong", and she cannot carry that.

## Watch out

- **Accepting the register as the inventory.** It is what units sent; the board and the supervisor will ask about what is used. Scope to the bank.
- **Promising ten.** You do not know which ten. Promise a date and a format.
- **Answering regulatory questions yourself.** "Is a letter draft a model under 369?" is the CRO's to answer under the bank's policy. You make sure she is asked, with the facts.
- **Criticising the design in week one.** Collect the evidence; take it to Dalit first.
- **Meeting units before the gatekeepers.** Every unit will ask you to fast-track them; you cannot answer until you know what Irina will accept.
- **Missing the dates in the contracts.** An auto-renewal can decide a merge for you before 15 March.

## Your turn

### Inputs

- [Engagement brief](../../labs/module-15/brief.md) and the [data room](../../labs/module-15/data-room/INDEX.md) (module download): `company/` (all), `governance/ai-policy-v1-2025.md`, `governance/bsd-ai-survey-response-2026-extract.md`, `governance/mrm-workplan-2027.md`, `ai/pilot-register-2026-12.csv` (skim).
- Persona: [Dalit Ben-Ami](../../labs/module-15/personas/dalit-coo.md) or her [transcript](../../labs/module-15/personas/transcripts/dalit-coo.md); [coach prompt](../../labs/common/coach-prompt.md).
- Templates: [engagement charter](../../templates/engagement-charter.md) (as the mandate memo), [scope boundary](../../templates/scope-boundary.md), [data-room first read](../../templates/data-room-first-read.md), [access request pack](../../templates/access-request-pack.md), [30-60-90 plan](../../templates/30-60-90-plan.md).

### Steps

1. Fill the entry-role table (six rows).
2. Interview Dalit (or read the transcript and mark what the interviewer found and missed). Read the objective back; agree done, scope, access and the reporting rhythm.
3. Write the mandate memo: objective, the stated ask as a claim, done, the entry-role table, scope with reasons, the advisory boundary with names, the reporting rhythm.
4. Do the first read: every folder with its provenance, at least eight statements sorted with evidence, and what is missing.
5. Write the access request and the three-week plan with the dates you cannot miss.
6. Write a note to Avner of at most six lines.

### Time

90 minutes: 10 for the table, 20 for the interview, 25 for the mandate, 20 for the first read, 15 for the access request, the plan and the note.

### Done when

- [ ] The objective is the sponsor's, read back, with her correction; "40 pilots and nothing in production" appears as the claim tested.
- [ ] Scope is every AI use in the bank, with the register as one source.
- [ ] The advisory boundary names the CRO, the CCO, the DPO, the CIO, counsel and internal audit for the questions you will not answer.
- [ ] At least eight statements are sorted with evidence, including the 40, "nothing in production", the policy's validation rule and the survey answer.
- [ ] The plan reaches the gatekeepers in week 2 and names the CFO's dates and any contract notice before 15 March.
- [ ] The note to the CEO promises a date and a format, not a number of pilots.
