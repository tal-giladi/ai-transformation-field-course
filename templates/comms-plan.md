# Comms plan

The plan and the texts for telling people about an AI change: which promises are true and who approved them, who hears what from whom and when, the announcement, the manager's talking points, the FAQ, and the notice about what is recorded about employees — plus what changes where employees are represented by a works committee or union. Use it for every rollout that changes someone's work. Lesson [06.5](../lessons/module-06/lesson-05.md) shows how; a spreadsheet version of the sequence is `labs/module-06/comms-plan.csv` in the module 6 download ([open](../labs/module-06/comms-plan.csv)).

## How to fill it

- Start with the promise table: every sentence people want to hear, whether it is true, who can promise it, and what the business case says. Get the hardest sentence approved word for word by the person who can keep it.
- Sequence: the manager, the champions and the receiving teams before the team; the team before the company; people who are affected but leaving (temps, contractors) told honestly.
- The FAQ answers the questions people are already asking, including the number everyone heard, in plain words and in their languages.
- The monitoring notice says what is collected, why, who sees it, what it is never used for, how long it is kept, and where to object.
- If there is a works committee or a union: information and consultation before the team hears; monitoring and job-security terms negotiated; labour counsel from the start.

## Template

```markdown
# Comms plan — [change] — v[n], [date]
Owner: [name] · Approved: [sponsor]; [approver of specific sentences]

## Promises and approvals
| Sentence | True? | Approver (date, time) | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|

## Sequence
| When | Audience | Message | Messenger | Channel | Languages | Feedback route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Announcement
[Under 200 words: what, why, what does not change, what happens next, where to ask]

## Manager's talking points
Say: [five points] · Do not say: [four points]

## FAQ
1. [The hardest question] — [approved answer]
2. [The number everyone heard] — [answer]
…

## Monitoring notice
What is recorded · Why · Who sees what · Never · How long · Questions or objections

## Representation
[Works committee / union: yes or no. If yes: who is informed and consulted, before what, about what, by when; counsel]
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine drafts pilot, v1.0, 21 January 2027 (excerpt). Promise: "No one in the Customer Service team will be dismissed or have their role reduced because of this pilot while it runs — until the decision on 30 September 2027", approved word for word by Yoav Ben-Ami and Michal Peretz on 21 January. Sequence: Samir, the champions, logistics and IT on 21 January; the support team at a 30-minute meeting on Sunday 24 January, with the FAQ in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English; the rest of the company by email the same day; the two seasonal temps told by Samir on their last working day. Representation: no works committee; individual rights (a hearing before any dismissal) still apply.
