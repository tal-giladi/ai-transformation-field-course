# Decision record

A short, signed record of one decision at a gate or a committee: what was decided, what else was considered and why not, on which evidence with which label, with what money, under which conditions, who can stop it, what would change it, and who disagreed. Use it for every scale, fix or stop decision, every release of staged money and every retirement, so the next reader (an auditor, a new owner, the board) can see why. Lesson [07.2](../lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md) writes Harbor & Pine's gate-1 record.

## How to fill it

- One decision per record, with a number (DR-YYYY-NN), a date and the forum.
- Decide per segment when segments behave differently (channel, language, category, site).
- If a pre-agreed gate rule was not met and money is released anyway, say so in a sentence, with what the money buys and what is held.
- Conditions of the next gate are yes/no, with the instrument, a date and the consequence of a "no".
- Write dissent: who, what, and why the forum did not follow it.
- Signed by the chair and the budget owner. Next review date at the bottom.

## Template

```markdown
# Decision record DR-[YYYY-NN] — [subject] — [forum], [date]

**Present:** [names and roles]

**Decision.** [Per segment: scale / fix / stop / retire / not now, one line each]

**Gate reading.** [Condition, result per segment, label] · [non-compensable gates: pass / fail]

**Money.** [Spent so far] · [released now, for what] · [held, until when] ·
[Departure from the gate rule, if any: "Gate N was not met (…). The forum departs from the rule to …"]

**Evidence.** [The numbers that decided it, with labels and sources]

**Options considered.** [Option — why not] · [Option — why not] · [Chosen — why]

**Next gate, [date], all yes.** [Condition (instrument)] · … *If yes:* [what happens] · *If no:* [what happens]

**Changed controls.** [Stop rules, flags, change control, monitoring]

**Who can stop it.** [Names, triggers]

**Requests this cycle.** [ID — decision — reason — re-screen date]

**Dissent.** [Who, what, why not adopted]

**Re-forecast.** [Cash banked · cash expected (label) · capacity]

*Signed:* [chair], [budget owner] · *Next review:* [date]
```

## Example

DR-2027-04, Harbor & Pine, 1 April 2027: gate 1 not fully met ((a) and adoption fail on WhatsApp; (d) 153 hours against 320). Scale drafts on email and web form to a service owned by Samir; fix WhatsApp (off since 29 March; test set and style; re-gate 1 July); retire the translation service's Customer Service seats; not now for pre-filled drafts. "The committee departs from the gate rule to release ₪30,000 of stage 2, for the WhatsApp fix only; ₪42,000 is held." Dissent: Yoav asked for pre-filled drafts now; not adopted after an incident with seven ten-second approvals. Re-forecast: ₪17,000 banked; ₪65,000 a year expected if the fix works.
