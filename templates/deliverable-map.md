# Deliverable map

One table of every document an engagement or programme produced: what it is, its version, whether it is living (used and updated), a record (a decision that must stay findable) or retired, who owns it after handover, where it lives, who uses it, when it is next reviewed and what it feeds. Use it before any handover, at the end of an engagement, and as the index of a portfolio piece (lesson 18.5). Lesson [07.9](../lessons/module-07/lesson-09.md) maps Harbor & Pine's Stage 1. Spreadsheet version: `labs/module-07/deliverable-map.csv` (module 7 download).

## How to fill it

- One row per deliverable, including the ones that were retired or superseded.
- Group rows the way people will look for them (mandate, discovery, portfolio, guardrails, service, people, decisions, retired).
- Every living deliverable has an owner who agreed and a next-review date in their calendar. A living document with no owner is a control that has stopped.
- Records have a place and a keeper; they are not edited.
- Mark superseded versions as superseded, with the replacing version.
- "Feeds" says which other deliverable or decision relies on it: that is where the integration check looks.

## Template

```markdown
# Deliverable map — [organization / programme] — [date]
Prepared by: [name] · Accepted by: [receiving owner], [date]

| Group | Deliverable | Template | Version, date | Kind (living / record / retired) | Owner after handover | Lives in | Used by | Next review | Feeds | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Orphans found: [deliverables with no owner, and what was done]
Superseded: [old version → new version]
```
