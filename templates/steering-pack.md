# Steering pack

The papers a steering committee decides from, and the plan for the meeting that decides. Use it whenever a committee is asked to release money, scale, fix or stop something: it puts the decision, who can stop the work and what a "no" means at the top of every document, carries the evidence with its labels, and ends in minutes that can be executed. Lesson [07.1](../lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md) builds the pack; [07.3](../lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md) protects it from softening; [07.4](../lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md) runs the meeting.

## How to fill it

- Write the three-line decision block first, for the pack and for each document in it.
- Body six pages at most, in reader order; everything else is an annex with a page reference.
- Every number carries one label: measured, directional or assumed. Offline results are never called production results.
- Put the weak line on page one, below the expected case.
- Every condition names a person and traces to a cost line.
- Anything that changes what is decided goes on its own agenda line, never fixed quietly.
- In the meeting: ask first, keep five minutes for the decision, read the minute back.

## Template

```markdown
# [Forum] — [date] — [subject]

## Decision block
**Decision this supports:** [what is asked, how much, conditions, by when]
**Who can stop it:** [named people, on which trigger]
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine, 19 January 2027. *Decision:* approve phase 1 of the reply-drafts pilot: ₪48,000 now, ₪72,000 at gate 1 on 1 April 2027 if (a)–(d) are met; send the case to the board on 28 January. *Who can stop it:* Samir or the pilot lead, the same day, on any of six stop rules; the chair at gate 1; the board. *What a "no" means:* no drafts on 1 February; Russian and Arabic agents without a drafting tool through spring; two temps hired next November (₪96,000). *The weak line:* the evidence so far covers one seasonal temp, not two. *Separate decision:* roadmap v1.1 (all four languages from 1 February with native-speaker spot checks). *Minutes, line 3:* Arabic drafts reviewed by Arabic speakers and re-measured before 1 March; out-of-hours support before go-live.
