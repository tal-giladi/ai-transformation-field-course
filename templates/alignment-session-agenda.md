# Alignment session agenda

The plan, pre-read and output page for a 60–90 minute session in which the organization's leaders turn "use AI" into up to three bets, each tied to a business goal with a KPI, a guardrail metric, an owner and a yes/no gate, and park everything else in writing. Use it after the scoring workshop and before the business case. Lesson [03.6](../lessons/module-03/lesson-06.md) shows how; the group roleplay for practice is in the module 3 download (`labs/module-03/group-roleplay.md`).

## How to fill it

- Hold the one-to-ones first: nobody hears bad news about their own idea for the first time in the room.
- The pre-read is one page, sent two days ahead, and asks each reader for the one number they would look at.
- Goals come first, in the leaders' words; bets come only as answers to goals.
- Every baseline is measured (with source and date) or "to be confirmed" (with method and date). No estimates.
- Every KPI has a guardrail that would move the wrong way if the gain were fake.
- Every parked idea has a reason and a dated reopening condition.
- The output page goes out the same day; each owner replies "confirmed".

## Template

```markdown
# Leadership alignment session — [organization] — [date, time, room]

## Before the session
| One-to-one with | Date | The question it must answer |
|---|---|---|
| [CEO / most senior person] | | [their favourite idea: evidence once, options, their choice] |
| [CFO] | | [indicators they already review; how money is staged] |
| [Sponsor] | | [objective still holds; who chairs] |
| [Lead of the team that changes most] | | [what they can absorb, when] |

## Pre-read (one page, sent [date])
1. Goals in the leaders' words: [ ]
2. What discovery found (three lines with numbers): [ ]
3. Candidates from the workshop, each with its condition: [ ]
4. Parked, each with its reason: [ ]
Ask: "Come with the one number you would look at to know each goal moved."

## Agenda
| Minutes | Block | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | The output of the session | Agreed |
| 5–20 | Goals | 2–4 goals in their words |
| 20–35 | Numbers | KPI, guardrail, baseline per goal |
| 35–65 | Bets | Up to three, each against a goal |
| 65–80 | Parked list | Reasons and dated reopening conditions |
| 80–90 | Read-back, money, story | The page; where money lands; one sentence for the board |

## Output page
**Goals:** [ ]

| Bet | Goal | KPI and baseline (source / "to be confirmed by …") | Guardrail | Owner / sponsor | Gate (yes/no) | Where the money lands |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Each bet in one sentence: To [goal], improve [KPI] from [baseline] by changing [workflow step] through
[bet], without [guardrail] getting worse; owner [ ], sponsor [ ]; it starts when [gate].

**Parked:** [idea — reason — reopening condition — date]
**Foundations (gates for the bets):** [ ]
**For the board:** "[one true sentence the most senior person can say]"
**Confirmed by:** [name, date]
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine, 10 December 2026, bet 1: "To have fewer angry customers, cut 'where is my order?' contacts from about 1,650 a month (October, all channels) by telling customers the real delivery date before the promised one passes, through a daily date check and delay emails, without repeat contacts (about 23%) or CSAT (63%) getting worse; owner Michal with Tamar, Yoav to agree the lead-time change with Noa; it goes live when two weeks of dry run show no wrong date." Parked: "Chatbot — would quote today's wrong dates; vendor figure measured elsewhere — re-screen October 2027 after six months of the date fix and WhatsApp in the helpdesk, with a proof of concept on our own data."
