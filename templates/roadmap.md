# Roadmap

A one-page plan of what happens in which order and why: each initiative (bets, foundations and re-screens) in a horizon set by evidence, every gate and dependency written as a yes/no condition with an owner, what each step proves or frees for the next, a quarterly check against review capacity and absorption, and a ledger column for where benefit and residue land. Write it after the ROI model and before the decision-ready case; update it after every gate. Lesson [03.8](../lessons/module-03/lesson-08.md) shows how.

## How to fill it

- List foundations (integrations, data recording, policies) as initiatives with owners, not as assumptions.
- Horizon by evidence: **Now** (measured problem, known fix, few preconditions), **Next** (direction evidenced, depends on Now), **Later** (depends on what Next proves, or on data that does not exist yet).
- Gates are yes/no conditions with an owner and a test date. Never a start date alone.
- Each initiative says what it proves or frees for the next one.
- The quarterly check compares new evaluations with central review capacity, each team's load with what it said it can absorb, and payments with cash timing.
- Scale by segment (language, site, product line); each segment's gate checks only what differs.
- Version and date it; each gate result updates it.

## Example

Harbor & Pine, v1.0, 14 January 2027 (excerpt):

| # | Initiative | Horizon | Gate | Proves or frees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N2 | WhatsApp into the helpdesk | Now: plain integration; precondition for X1 | Business account approved; four numbers migrated off the phones; DPO view on chat history; all written contact arrives as tickets for two weeks | A real baseline for the drafts pilot; customer data off shared phones |
| X1 | Reply drafts for all written contact | Next: depends on N2 and an approved tool | Gate 1, 1 April 2027: 80% of drafts accepted on 400 real messages; median review ≤ 2.5 minutes; wrong replies reaching customers no higher than today | Test set and review routine reused by quote drafts and assembly answers |
| L3 | Chatbot | Later: depends on N1, N2, a test on own data | Re-screen 28 October 2027 | — |

Quarterly check: two new AI evaluations a quarter at most (head of AI, one-person IT, outside counsel); no support go-live from November to January (peak); Q1 payments kept small (weakest cash quarter).
