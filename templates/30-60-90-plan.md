# 30-60-90 plan

A one-page plan for your first 90 days, built from questions and decisions rather than activities, with your first week planned by the half-day. Write it in week one, after the [engagement charter](engagement-charter.md) and [scope boundary](scope-boundary.md), and send it to your sponsor for approval. Lesson [01.4](../lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md) shows how.

## How to fill it

- Write real dates, counted from day one, and put the decision-maker's pre-read at least a week before any board or steering meeting.
- List the constraints first, each with its source.
- Give each block one question. List only the deliverables that answer it.
- Every block ends in a decision by a named person on a date. Name decisions, not solutions.
- Say what you will not do. Keep it to one page.

## Template

```markdown
# 30-60-90 plan — [Organization] — v[1.0], sent [date]

**Dates:** Day 1 [date] · Day 30 [date] · Day 60 [date] · pre-read [date] · [board/steering] [date]

**Constraints (source):**
- [constraint] ([source])

| | Days 1–30 | Days 31–60 | Days 61–90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| The question | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| Deliverables | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| Decisions (who, by when) | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| People | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| Risks and fallback | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| Will not | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |

**Decision points:** By [date], [who] decides [what], from [what evidence].

**Week one:**
| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |

**Rhythm:** weekly check-in [day, time, length] · monthly review [date] · pre-read [date]
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine, sent to Michal Peretz (COO) on 5 November 2026, excerpt:

| | Days 1–30 (2 Nov – 1 Dec) | Days 31–60 (2 – 31 Dec) | Days 61–90 (1 – 28 Jan) |
|---|---|---|---|
| The question | What is really happening in the work, the data and the AI already in use? | What first, and what is it worth? | Does it work on our data; will the board fund six months? |
| Decisions | Michal: interim AI rule by 12 Nov | Michal + Yoav: the first move, 10 Dec | Steering pre-read 21 Jan; board 28 Jan |
| Will not | Meet vendors, promise savings, run workshops | Sign contracts | Train the whole team before February |

Decision point: "By 10 December, Michal and Yoav choose the one thing to have working by 28 January, from a shortlist with measured baselines."
