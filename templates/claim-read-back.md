# Claim read-back

A one-page answer to "can we believe this number?" for a vendor's (or anyone's) claim: the claim as stated, the conditions it was measured under, who chose them, how far they fit yours, a rating, and the evidence that would change the rating — with the decision at the top. Write it before a vendor meeting turns into a negotiation, and apply it to internal claims too. Lesson [04.8](../lessons/module-04/lesson-08.md) shows how.

## How to fill it

- Copy each claim word for word, with where it appears.
- Fill five conditions per claim: sample, definition of success, volume and task mix, oversight, time window. "Not stated" is a finding.
- Trace each figure hop by hop to its method or to a dead end. A second document repeating it is not a second source.
- Compare the claim's conditions with yours (language, channel, integrations, contact mix, definition of success).
- Rate: enough to act on · worth testing further · not yet supportable · contradicted. Say what evidence would change it.
- Add one sentence on counterparty risk and one on the hedge.

## Template

```markdown
# Claim read-back — [vendor / source] — [date]

**Decision:** [one or two sentences: what we do now, what we test, when we decide]

| Claim (as stated, where) | Conditions (sample · success definition · mix · oversight · window) | Who chose them | Fit to our conditions | Rating | What would change it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Hop trace:** [claim] → [footnote / report] → [method or dead end]
**Counterparty:** [age, funding, term, exit, change of control] · **Hedge:** [...]
**Same test applied to our own claims:** [e.g. the internal target this vendor is meant to deliver]
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine — ChatNest, 23 November 2026 (excerpt):

**Decision:** do not sign by 30 November; run a four-week proof of concept on our own data after the delivery-date fix and a DPA, against the drafts pilot's test set; decide in February.

| Claim | Conditions | Fit | Rating | What would change it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "70% of conversations fully resolved without an agent" (slide 2) | "No transfer in the session"; US/Canada; English; Jan–Jun 2026; with order-tracking integration | Language, channel, integration and definition differ; Israeli customer about 38% (said) | Not yet supportable for us | No re-contact on the same order within 14 days and no wrong fact, per language, in the proof of concept |
| Demo "on your own catalogue" (slide 4) | File from marketing, 28 Sep 2026 | Stale 2023 copy: Oslo sofa ₪4,290 (now ₪4,990); Bergen table discontinued Aug 2025 | Contradicted | Rebuild on the live catalogue |
