# Scoring workshop script

The facilitator's script for a 60–90 minute workshop in which the people who own value, money, risk and the work score the screened candidates under criteria they agreed in advance, and leave owning the result. Write it after the criteria are locked and the fast screen has been sent, two days before the session. Lesson [03.5](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md) shows how; the group roleplay for practice is in the module 3 download (`labs/module-03/group-roleplay.md`).

## How to fill it

- Start from the one output. Everything on the agenda produces it; nothing else is decided.
- Put what needs the most senior person before they leave.
- Scores are collected independently, on paper or a private form, before any discussion. Never average; discuss only spreads of 2 or more.
- Write your answers in advance to the pressures you can predict (a bonus for a favourite, a vendor deadline, headcount).
- The read-back names each condition with an owner and a date, and goes out by email the same day.

## Template

```markdown
# Scoring workshop — [organization] — [date, time, room]

**Output (one sentence):** [which candidates go to [next forum], with conditions; what is parked and why]
**Not decided today:** [money / headcount / vendor contracts]
**Present:** [name, role, leaves at …]   ·   **Pre-read sent:** [date] — criteria, screen, one page per candidate

## Agenda
| Minutes | Block | What I say or ask | Output of the block |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Purpose | "One output today: …" | Agreed output |
| 5–12 | Criteria read-back | "[Name], could you state the criteria without the sheet?" | Criteria confirmed, unchanged |
| 12–20 | What the screen stopped | One line per stopped idea; "reopened only with new evidence" | Parking lot |
| 20–30 | Independent scoring | "Three minutes, your own sheet, no discussion" | Score table |
| 30–60 | Divergence | To lowest and highest: "What are you looking at that the others might not be?" | Conditions; changed scores |
| 60–75 | Absorption and sequence | "[Lead], how many hours a month can your team give to anything new until [date]?" | What fits when |
| 75–85 | Read-back | "Let me read it back: … Did I miss anything?" | Result with owners and dates |
| 85–90 | Actions | Who, what, by when | Action list |

## Pressures and my answers
| Likely pressure | From | My answer |
|---|---|---|
| Change a weight or add a bonus after seeing the list | [ ] | "If we change the rules after we've seen the list, …" |
| A vendor deadline or discount | [ ] | [the discount in ₪ against the commitment in ₪] |
| Headcount | [ ] | Close it; ask the team's lead by name |

## Score table (filled in the room)
| Card | [Person 1] V/F/R | [Person 2] | [Person 3] | [Person 4] | Spread ≥ 2? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Read-back (sent [date, time])
1. [Candidate] — condition: [ ] — owner: [ ] — date: [ ]
Parked: [candidate — reason — reopening condition]
Actions: [ ]
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine, 26 November 2026, 10:00–11:30. Output: "Three candidates for the alignment session on 10 December, each with its conditions; what is parked and why." Yoav leaves at 10:40, so the criteria read-back and his scores come first. Pressure from Yoav: "Sign ChatNest before Monday for the discount." Answer: the discount is about US$217 a month, roughly ₪9,700 a year at ₪3.70 per US$; signing commits about ₪64,000 a year in platform fees plus ₪85,000 implementation for the card that scored last. Read-back item: "Reply drafts for all written contact from February, after WhatsApp is routed into the helpdesk; a native speaker samples Arabic and Russian replies weekly — owners Samir (pilot) and Avi (routing, by 14 January)."
