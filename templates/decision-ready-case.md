# Decision-ready case

A one-page front that a board or committee can decide from, with the detail behind it in an appendix: a one-sentence ask, the return with the conservative scenario first, the conditions and gates, the cost of waiting in the same units, and every claim labelled measured, directional or assumed with a source tag. Write it last, from the ROI model, the roadmap and the readiness assessment, after the person whose favourite idea lost has heard it privately. Lesson [03.9](../lessons/module-03/lesson-09.md) shows how; module 7 updates it with pilot evidence for the steering pack.

## How to fill it

- One ask, one sentence: what is decided, who decides, by when, how much. Two "and"s mean two decisions: split them.
- Conservative scenario first. Capacity is never shown as cash.
- Every number has a label (**measured**, **directional**, **assumed**) and a source tag (readiness assessment, ROI model, roadmap, findings memo). No tag, no number.
- The cost of waiting is a two-column comparison in the same units, not a warning.
- Strategy in its own short section, separate from the numbers.
- Report what does not need a decision (already approved, parked) in one line each.
- Two-reader check before sending: finance traces every number in a minute; the sponsor, reading as a director, finds the ask, the return and the consequence of "no" in a minute.

## Template

```markdown
# [Title: the problem, what has been done, one decision] — [forum], [date]

**The ask.** [Forum] is asked on [date] to approve [what] for [₪ amount over n years], [staging].

**What we found** ([label]). [Two or three sentences with numbers.] *([source tags])*

**What we have already done** ([label]). [One or two sentences.] *([source tag])*

**What the proposal does.** [Two sentences, in the business's words.]

**Return, conservative first** ([hurdle]% over [n] years; [model version, signed by]):
low [NPV, payback, what it means] · **expected [NPV, payback, cash a year and its labels; capacity
a year, not cash]** · high [NPV, labelled upside, not relied on].

**Conditions.** [Gate 1, date: yes/no conditions] · [Gate 2, date: …]

**If we wait until [date]:**
| | Decide on [date] | Decide on [later date] |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] | | |

**Not asked today:** [items already approved or parked, one line each, with dates]

---
**Strategy** (separate): [three lines]

**Appendix:** [ROI model vN] · [roadmap vN] · [screen and parked list] · [other]

**Label and source check:**
| Claim on the page | Measured / directional / assumed | Source tag |
|---|---|---|

**Two-reader check:** finance ([name], [date]): [what moved] · sponsor ([name], [date]): [what moved]
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine, board of 28 January 2027: "The board is asked on 28 January 2027 to approve up to ₪267,000 over three years for a reply-drafts pilot in customer service, released in two stages: ₪48,000 now, and ₪72,000 from 1 April only if gate 1 is met." Return: low NPV −₪196,859 (a capacity investment, not a saving); **expected NPV ₪33,717, payback 22.5 months** (₪113,000 a year in cash: ₪96,000 no temps next peak, directional, signed plan; ₪17,000 translation, measured; plus about 109 agent-hours a month at peak, capacity); high NPV ₪195,000 (assumed upside). If we wait until April: gate 2 cannot happen before the next peak and the temps (₪96,000) are hired again. Not asked today: the chatbot, parked with a re-screen on 28 October 2027.
