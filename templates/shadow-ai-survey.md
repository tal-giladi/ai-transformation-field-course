# Shadow-AI survey

A short, anonymous survey that asks staff which AI tools they already use, for what work, with what kind of data, and what they would need from an approved tool. It is one of the five sources of the shadow-AI census, the one that tells you *why*. Send it in the first two weeks, through HR, in every language the staff work in. Lesson [01.6](../lessons/module-01/lesson-06.md) shows how to combine it with logs, extensions, expenses and interviews; the census table itself is `labs/module-01/shadow-ai-census.csv` in the module 1 download ([open](../labs/module-01/shadow-ai-census.csv)).

## How to fill it

- Put the purpose and the amnesty at the top, word for word as the sponsor approved them.
- Keep it to eight questions and five minutes. Ask about tasks and data types, never about colleagues.
- Anonymous: collect department only, and merge any department with fewer than five people into "other".
- Translate it; in Israel that usually means Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English, sometimes Amharic.
- Report results by department and question, alongside the log-based census, never alone.

## Template

```markdown
# Which AI tools help you at work? (5 minutes, anonymous)

[Organization] wants to approve AI tools that help with real work and set sensible rules. We are
asking which tools you already use and for what. Answers are anonymous and reported by department.
**Nobody will be disciplined for using AI tools before the rules are published.**
Questions: [contact name]. Closes: [date].

1. Your department: [list; small departments grouped as "other"]
2. In the last month, which AI tools did you use for work? (tick all)
   [ ] ChatGPT  [ ] Gemini  [ ] Claude  [ ] Microsoft Copilot  [ ] a translation tool (which?) ____
   [ ] a writing tool (which?) ____  [ ] AI features inside a work system (which?) ____
   [ ] other ____  [ ] none
3. How often? [ ] daily [ ] weekly [ ] a few times [ ] never
4. What do you use them for? (tick all)
   [ ] drafting emails or replies  [ ] translating  [ ] summarising documents  [ ] writing marketing text
   [ ] analysing numbers  [ ] images  [ ] coding or formulas  [ ] other ____
5. What kind of information have you put into them? (tick all)
   [ ] general text with no names  [ ] customer names or contact details  [ ] order or claim details
   [ ] employee information  [ ] financial or pricing information  [ ] supplier information
   [ ] I'm not sure
6. How do you access them? [ ] free personal account [ ] paid personal account [ ] company account
   [ ] browser extension [ ] phone app
7. Roughly how much time does AI save you in a normal week? [ ] none [ ] < 1 h [ ] 1–3 h [ ] > 3 h
8. What would an approved tool need to do for you to use it instead? ____
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine, sent by Dana Katz (HR) on 9 November 2026 in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English, closing 16 November. Amnesty line as approved by Michal Peretz on 5 November. Small departments (IT, Facilities, Management) grouped as "other". Question 2 lists LinguaFlow, DeepL, Grammarly and CopyGenie by name, because the proxy log showed them; question 8 is the one the census report quotes ("translate Russian and Arabic replies without retyping the customer's details").
