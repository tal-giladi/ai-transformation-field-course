# Training session plan

The plan for a short, role-based training session on the team's real work: the goal as a behaviour, who is trained in which group and who is not, the exercises built from real cases with one planted error each, the minute-by-minute run sheet, the job aid, and how the session will be measured. Use it for any rollout where people must check or use an AI output in their daily work. Lesson [06.2](../lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md) shows how; the full Harbor & Pine session (script, eight exercise cards, trainer key, job aid, run sheet, exit check) is in `labs/module-06/training/` in the module 6 download ([script](../labs/module-06/training/session-script.md)).

## How to fill it

- Write the goal as something the trainee will do tomorrow, observable in the first week.
- Decide the roster explicitly: every person has a group or a reason why not (leave, contract ended, a different role version).
- Build exercises from real cases in the trainees' languages; each hides one error that one check catches; include one correct output.
- At least 25 of 60 minutes hands-on. The trainer asks "what on the screen tells you?" rather than answering.
- Say who sees the exit-check results before the check. Write the rule that would make you change the session.

## Example

Harbor & Pine, "Your drafts, your call", v1.1, 26 January 2027 (excerpt). Goal: open any written contact, check the AI draft in under three minutes, decide used / edited / discarded, and raise the right flag, on our real messages in your languages. Roster: four language groups 8–11 February with a champion as co-trainer; Samir and Rina in a role version; the two seasonal temps not trained (contracts ended 31 January; session 1 and the job aid become onboarding for any future temp). Exercise card 2: real Russian message EM-32768; the ETA field is blank, so the draft repeats the passed checkout date; the check is "dates". Learning measure: one fresh card, observed; results stay with the trainer and champion; if fewer than 8 of 12 pass, session 2 repeats the cards.
