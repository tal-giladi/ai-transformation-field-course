# Phase-1 plan

The week-by-week plan that turns a board's or committee's yes into work, from the day after the decision to the first gate: the first 72 hours, the people's hours, the money released against deliverables, one goal and one check per week, and the reports. Use it the day after approval; keep it current every Thursday. Lesson [07.8](../lessons/module-07/lesson-08.md) writes Harbor & Pine's. Spreadsheet version: `labs/module-07/phase-1-plan.csv` (module 7 download).

## How to fill it

- Close everything that makes the yes executable within three working days: written decision, money, the team told by its manager, every condition with an owner and a date.
- Write each person's hours per week, from whose budget, agreed with their manager.
- Pay against deliverables someone checks, not against dates.
- Each week: one goal, its deliverables, the owner, hours, money, the gate or condition it checks, the decision needed.
- Label learning weeks and keep them out of gate readings.
- A stage date is the earliest date. If an exit condition is not met, the date moves, or the owner records a departure with a re-check date.

## Template

```markdown
# Phase-1 plan — [initiative] — [decision date] to [gate date]

## First 72 hours
| Item | What exactly | Who | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision confirmed in writing | | | |
| Money released (PO, milestones) | | | |
| Team told by its manager | | | |
| Conditions with owners and dates | | | |
| People's time booked | | | |
| Reporting calendar | | | |

## Staffing (hours a week)
| Person | Weeks [a–b] | Weeks [c–d] | What | Agreed with |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Money against deliverables
| Milestone | Payment | Checked by |
|---|---|---|

## Week by week
| Week | Dates | Goal | Deliverables | Owner | Hours | Money | Check (gate / condition) | Decision needed from | Report |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Weekly report (one page, every [day])
1. Status in one line · 2. Signals (labelled; learning weeks marked) · 3. Decisions needed (who, by when) ·
4. Conditions and risks · 5. Money: committed, spent, next week

## Departures from exit conditions
| Date | Stage | Condition not met | Decided by | Reason | Re-check date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

## Example

Harbor & Pine, 29 January – 1 April 2027. First 72 hours: written decision to Michal and Ronit on Friday; PO for ₪48,000 with milestones (stage 1 live and switch tested ₪16,000; WhatsApp shadow and web-store lookup ₪8,000; logging and monitoring ₪8,000); Samir tells the team on Sunday 31 January that no cuts are in the case. Week 2's check: the WhatsApp shadow exit on Sunday 14 February. Result: Hebrew 78%, Russian 76%, Arabic 71% (41 rated), lookup 62%: not met. The week-2 report gives Samir three options and asks that any departure be recorded with a re-check date.
