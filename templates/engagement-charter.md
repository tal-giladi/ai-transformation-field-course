# Engagement charter

The one document that says why you are here, for whom, with what money and authority, and what "done" means. Write it in your first week, from your first conversation with the sponsor, and get it confirmed in writing (an email reply that says "agreed" is enough). Internal leads use it instead of a contract; consultants write it after the statement of work is signed, and the SOW outline at the end is the skeleton of that contract. Lessons [01.1](../lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md) and [01.2](../lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md) show how to fill it.

## How to fill it

- Write the objective in the sponsor's own words, then read it back to them and record that they confirmed it. Don't polish it into consultant language.
- "Done" is a list of things someone could check on a date, not adjectives.
- Every number (budget, threshold, date) comes from the sponsor or a document; say which.
- Keep it to two pages. Scope details go in the [scope boundary](scope-boundary.md); the plan goes in the [30-60-90 plan](30-60-90-plan.md).
- Re-issue it with a new version number when the objective, money or authority changes.

## Template

```markdown
# Engagement charter — [Organization] — v[1.0], [date]

**Entry role:** [internal lead / consultant / accidental owner] · **Reports to:** [name, role]
**Sponsor:** [name, role] · **Confirmed by sponsor:** [date, how — e.g. "email reply 3 Nov"]

## 1. Why now
[Two or three sentences: what happened that started this, with the source document.]

## 2. The objective, in the sponsor's words
> "[Quote, exactly as said.]"
Read back on [date]; sponsor's correction: [none / what changed].
Is the stated solution the goal or a means? [Answer, in the sponsor's words.]

## 3. What "done" means on [date]
1. [Checkable condition]
2. [Checkable condition]
3. [Checkable condition]

## 4. Money
| Item | Amount (₪) | Who approves | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget the sponsor controls | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| Approval thresholds above it | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |

## 5. Authority and access
- May access directly: [systems, data, people]
- By request only: [departments outside the sponsor's line]
- May not: [e.g. sign vendor contracts, change production systems]

## 6. Constraints
[Season, people's time, promises already made, legal or works-committee limits.]

## 7. Stakeholders to see first
| Who | Why | By when |
|---|---|---|

## 8. Advisory boundary
| Question type | I answer / route to | Named person |
|---|---|---|

## 9. Rhythm and next step
[Weekly check-in: day, length. Next deliverable and date.]

## 10. Open questions
[What you still need to know, and from whom.]

## Appendix — SOW outline (consultants; internal leads keep the headings)
1. Background and objective (sponsor's words)
2. Scope: in, out, change-request route
3. Deliverables, each with its acceptance criterion
4. Timeline and milestones
5. Client responsibilities (access, people's time, decisions by date)
6. Fees and model (fixed fee / time and materials / retainer), expenses, invoicing, VAT
7. Confidentiality and data handling (what, where kept, deleted when)
8. Assumptions and exclusions (no legal advice, no production changes, …)
9. Exit: what the client keeps if it stops after this phase
```

## Example

From Harbor & Pine, after the first meeting with the COO (lesson 01.2):

> **Engagement charter — Harbor & Pine — v1.0, 2 November 2026**
> **Entry role:** internal lead · **Reports to:** Michal Peretz, COO · **Sponsor:** Michal Peretz
> **Confirmed by sponsor:** email reply, 3 November 2026
>
> **2. The objective, in the sponsor's words.** "On 28 January the board sees one thing that already
> works, a plan for the next six months they believe, and numbers Ronit has signed. And my people
> still trust us." Read back 2 November; no corrections. Is the bot the goal? "Fewer angry customers,
> and a support cost that doesn't keep rising. The bot is Yoav's idea of how to get there."
>
> **4. Money.** Operations-improvement budget H1 2027: ₪120,000, approved, controlled by the COO
> (source: Michal). ₪50,000–150,000: CEO; above ₪150,000 over three years: board
> (`company/finance-summary.md`). Before January: no formal budget; small test tools by request.
>
> **5. Authority.** Direct: customer service, logistics, warehouse, purchasing, HR; Priority, Deskly,
> CloudVoice, logistics files. By request: marketing, sales, the web store (report to the CEO).
> May not: sign vendor contracts or change production systems.
