# Module 4 quiz — Guardrails you can ship in week two

Ten scenario questions on the policy pack at Harbor & Pine and organizations like it: writing an acceptable-use policy people can follow, deciding which data may go into which tool, reviewing vendor terms, tiering AI uses and deciding when a DPIA is needed, testing the controls behind a risk register, mapping which law applies with the actor and the date, running governance with an intake register, and checking a vendor's claims before signing. Each question is a decision you would face in a real engagement. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
