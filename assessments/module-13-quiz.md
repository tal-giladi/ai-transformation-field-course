# Module 13 quiz — Kinneret Plastics

Ten scenario questions on the sixth engagement: being the internal lead with a day job, handed "predictive maintenance with AI" by the owner, with your own data under audit; splitting the loss before explaining it; running the same-word test on a KPI two plants report; profiling maintenance records for a named use (outcome labels, completion times, identifiers, clocks, sensors, a stale parts list); escalating safety findings the same day; netting a vendor at its own best claim with the CFO's numbers; writing the readiness bar before the demo and saying "not yet" with criteria, funded work and a date; designing the foundation with a unionised, multilingual front line; building a repeatable readiness audit and a multilingual manual assistant evaluated against gates; and recommending against the owner's idea while giving him something better for the customer. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
