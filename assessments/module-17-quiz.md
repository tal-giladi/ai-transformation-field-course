# Module 17 quiz — Vantage Logistics

Ten scenario questions on the tenth engagement, a rescue: entering an inherited AI programme as an interim lead engaged by a board for 30 days, with two principals and an incident already open; agreeing how findings travel before any exist; testing an incident record, a review step, "four regions live" and a vendor's savings against Vantage's own logs; restating savings and decomposing an overrun; fixing audit rules before opening the list; pressure-testing what the inherited programme promised and planned next; rebuilding trust with the board, operations, a works committee, a customer and a vendor; running a stop/keep audit per system and region; closing the incident with every legal question routed to counsel; taking decisions that cannot wait; and writing the 30-day board brief. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
