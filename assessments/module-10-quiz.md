# Module 10 quiz — Galil Health

Ten scenario questions on the third engagement: entering a clinical network as an outside consultant with decision rights that leave safety, patients' information and the vendor choice to the people who own them; measuring documentation time and the real language mix when the record system's own field misleads; finding AI recording that is already happening without exposing anyone; netting a vendor's time-saving claim as capacity rather than cash; writing an RFP that asks how every claim was measured and makes a proof of concept part of the bid; preparing the privacy, health-regulation and medical-device questions for counsel; reading a small proof of concept with its uncertainty; scoring vendors on evidence beside their demos with must-haves as a gate; designing a clinician pilot with a comparison, an audit, stop rules and consent; and delivering a recommend-against to the CEO who announced the opposite. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
