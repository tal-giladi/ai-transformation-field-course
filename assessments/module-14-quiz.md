# Module 14 quiz — Lumen SaaS

Ten scenario questions on the software-company engagement: joining as the internal owner of AI operations; treating a launch date and a board target as inputs to test; reconstructing what an agent did from its own log and finding who acts under a shared identity; measuring resolution with a quality check beside it; writing an agent permission map action by action, with recoverability, the last reversible moment, a rung on the authority ladder and limits enforced outside the model; identity, audit trail and a kill switch that stops; deciding about a business user's own agent; routing Israeli privacy law and the EU AI Act to counsel; must-refuse tests, red-team scripts and staged autonomy; and taking a per-action decision to the executive staff. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
