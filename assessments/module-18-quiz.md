# Module 18 quiz — Your engagement

Ten scenario questions on the capstone and what comes after it: planning a whole engagement on one page before reading deeply; getting a mandate from a sponsor who has already chosen a solution; sorting an unseen data room without being told what matters; writing hypotheses with their disproving evidence first; keeping one register of facts and running the integration check with its control and timing traces; disclosing a contradiction that changes a decision; choosing the hardest finding and the executive most likely to resist it, and rehearsing it privately; scoring a recorded presentation; running a real engagement in your own workplace with permission, a data plan and colleagues protected; and turning your work into a practice kit and honestly labelled portfolio pieces. The questions do not reveal anything about Tzurim's hidden truth. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
