# Module 12 quiz — Atlas & Rowe

Ten scenario questions on the law-firm engagement: entering a partnership as an outside consultant who is not a lawyer; writing a vendor proposal as the question an engagement answers; finding AI use in logs rather than surveys and research time in narratives rather than codes; protecting the people who tell you what really happens; netting a productivity claim by billing type, keeping capacity apart from cash and pricing the customer's share; reading confidentiality, privilege and the Bar's ethics opinions with the firm's ethics partner; turning the fabricated-citation cases into a citation gate; testing a precedent search and the gate on the firm's own documents; and taking a divisible recommendation to a partners' vote. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
