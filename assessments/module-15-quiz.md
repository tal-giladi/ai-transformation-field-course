# Module 15 quiz — Crescent Bank

Ten scenario questions on the seventh engagement: entering a regulated bank as the new head of an AI centre of excellence with no line authority and a design approved before you arrived; testing "40 pilots and nothing in production" as a claim; testing a pilot register against contracts, evals, usage logs, card lines and the proxy; telling copies, duplicates and shared patterns apart; measuring a review queue in the gatekeeper's own units; escalating a live data exposure the same day; reading usage with the right denominator; choosing between central, federated and hub-and-spoke on the bank's own evidence; writing decision rights and validation depth by risk tier with the tier rule owned by model risk management; specifying the paved roads and the catalogue; triaging 40 builds with rules written first, merging under one owner, finding the dependencies a cut would break; a 12-month plan with gates; and delivering the hardest finding to the CEO. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
