# Module 8 quiz — Northwind Outfitters

Ten scenario questions on the first engagement: entering as an outside consultant with a fixed fee and exit points; testing a CEO's stated ask instead of adopting it; measuring what the client never measured (repeat contacts, handling time like for like, what customers did after the chatbot "resolved" them); netting a chatbot's claim and your own, including the income a fix gives up; drawing the line between what a bot may say alone and what an agent should say with a draft; reading chatbot liability with counsel; building and evaluating an agent-assist pilot where rules decide and the model reads and writes; and delivering a recommend-against to the CEO who asked for the opposite. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
