# Module 11 quiz — Givat Oren municipality

Ten scenario questions on the fourth engagement: being the accidental owner in a municipality, with no budget, no authority over content and every purchase public; getting a mandate from the director-general rather than the mayor's office; measuring what the city never measured (repeat contacts, callbacks actually made, the language residents are answered in, pages that contradict the council); reading decisions residents cannot act on and AI already talking to residents; netting a case where hours are not the point; turning procurement, freedom of information, reasons, accessibility, languages, privacy, workers and government guidance into design rules; designing against the MyCity pattern instead of disclaiming it; evaluating a permit-FAQ assistant in four languages against today and against no AI; and recommending against the mayor's chatbot while giving him something true to say. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
