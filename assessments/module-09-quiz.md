# Module 9 quiz — Meridian Mutual

Ten scenario questions on the Meridian Mutual engagement: reading a sponsor's proposal and a data room on day one as an internal head of AI in a supervised insurer, discovery with a process owner, a frontline handler and an affected claimant, reconciling three cycle-time baselines and finding the real bottleneck, classifying AI and AI-like systems by impact, an outcome test across groups and a pass / flag / block deployment verdict under Israeli law, a document-extraction pilot with a field-level eval and a fairness check, and the readout and decision record. Each question is a decision you would face in an engagement like this one. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
