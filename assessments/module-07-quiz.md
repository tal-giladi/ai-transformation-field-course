# Module 7 quiz — Readouts, decisions and the operating rhythm

Ten scenario questions on the part of the work that follows the pilot: building a steering pack that names the decision, who can stop it and what a "no" means; delivering bad news to a sponsor and a CEO who already committed a number; running the steering meeting; turning a board's yes into weekly work; handling an AI incident; reading a gate per segment and writing the decision record; running the service with an owner, a calendar and triggers; pressure-testing a roadmap someone else wrote; and handing the service over so it survives you. Each question is a decision you would face at an organization like Harbor & Pine. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
