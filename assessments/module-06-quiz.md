# Module 6 quiz — Making people use it

Ten scenario questions on getting an AI pilot used well at Harbor & Pine and organizations like it: redesigning the work so the new way is the easiest way, training people on their own work, running champions and office hours, reading usage data honestly and lawfully, communicating promises you can keep, and turning resistance into information. Each question is a decision you would face in a real rollout. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
