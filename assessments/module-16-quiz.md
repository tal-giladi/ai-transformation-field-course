# Module 16 quiz — Open Hands

Ten scenario questions on the smallest engagement in the course: entering a 30-person amuta as an unpaid volunteer with 40 hours and no authority; running the census and the interviews in a week, including the parent whose child's information is at stake; counting who can see family and donor data; finding that the biggest saving is a conversation with a funder, not a tool, and that "no budget" hides money already being spent; deciding which steps of the method shrink to an hour and which cannot; reading which Israeli privacy rules apply to a small organization and what goes to counsel; verifying free nonprofit offers against the vendors' own pages; building two workflows on free tools with thresholds, traps and a person as the control; writing the whole policy pack on one page; and handing over a 15-minute monthly check. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
