# Module 1 quiz — Walking in

Ten scenario questions on the first weeks of an AI engagement: choosing how you enter and how the work is priced, getting a real mandate from a sponsor, holding a scope boundary, planning 90 days around decisions, reading a data room, running a shadow-AI census and building the first AI inventory. Each question is a decision you would face at an organization like Harbor & Pine. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10).
