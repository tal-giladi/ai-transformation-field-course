## What it adds over the first certificate

The first certificate is a leader's judgment course. This one is the consultant's version of the
same arc: framing and scoping an engagement, reading a data room on day one, due diligence on vendor
claims, stricter screening and scoring, readiness as a written deliverable, agent/workflow
opportunity framing, pressure-testing a roadmap, a fuller ROI model, a decision-ready case and
delivering bad news upward. Its role-plays happen inside a client (the first certificate's were job
interviews). Its capstone — company brief, partial data room, one integrated advisory package,
"recommending against is rewarded" — is the same model as our engagements, which confirms the design.

It still leaves the same core gaps as the first one: nobody interviews real people, touches real
data, builds or evaluates a pilot, writes a policy pack, trains a team, or runs anything after the
decision; everything is one large manufacturer; regulation is EU-only.
