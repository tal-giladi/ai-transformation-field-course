## What the public page says

- **Seat:** AI transformation *advisor* (outside consultant) for a multinational manufacturer — the
  same manufacturer used throughout courses 4–6 (inspection pilots, maintenance scheduling, supplier
  documents, warranty claims, plants on different record formats).
- **Inputs:** a company brief and a **partial data room**. A video titled "Reading a Data Room on the
  First Day" (9 min) opens the capstone.
- **Module 1 — Assemble the advisory foundation (2h):** for each candidate initiative, what it would
  deliver, where its benefits land, what must be true for it to work, and what the evidence shows;
  separate the organization's readiness *claims* from what can be verified. Output: opportunity
  assessment, prioritized portfolio, readiness summary.
- **Module 2 — Build the core advisory work (3h):** roadmap, ROI business case, governance and risk
  plan; tie analysis to decisions, including recommending **against**, de-scoping or redesigning an
  initiative ("recommending against is a valid, rewarded outcome").
- **Module 3 — Integrate, document, present (2h):** one coherent package + an executive presentation
  built around a **single recommendation**; documented reasoning that stands alone as a portfolio
  piece; plan how to present the **hardest finding to the executive most likely to resist it**.
- **Grading:** rubric on framework application, advisory reasoning, integration across components,
  executive communication. Single graded deliverable at the end. Each stage has ~80–100 min of
  materials (likely PDFs like the first certificate's capstone).

## New vs first certificate

- **Data room on day one** with partial information and readiness *claims* to verify — closer to
  our engagement design than anything in the first certificate.
- **Integration across separately built components** and a single recommendation (course 6 module 4
  taught the method).
- **Rehearsing the hardest finding** for the most resistant executive.
- Recommending against is explicitly rewarded (the first cert said "defer/retire" was allowed;
  this one grades it).
