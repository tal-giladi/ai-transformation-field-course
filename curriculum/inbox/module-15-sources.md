## Changes for `references/laws-and-guidance.md`

- Add **Bank of Israel Directive 369, Management of Model Risk** (circular 2792-06, 21 Aug 2024; in force 21 Aug 2025; phase-in to 21 Feb 2026 for existing material models and 21 Feb 2027 for other existing models; applies to AI models).
- Add **Directive 364** (IT, information security and cyber risk; circular C-06-2799, 18 Nov 2024; in force 18 May 2026; replaces 357, 361, 363) and note the **Directive 362 update** (circular 2849-06, 17 June 2026).
- Add the **Bank of Israel's May 2026 release** on the AI-governance maturity survey of banks.
- The inter-ministerial report's issuers can now be marked verified (p. 6).
- US line: **SR 11-7 was superseded by SR 26-2 on 17 April 2026**; generative and agentic AI are outside SR 26-2's scope. Any lesson that cites SR 11-7 as current should be checked.
