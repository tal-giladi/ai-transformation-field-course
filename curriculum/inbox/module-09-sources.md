## Not found

- No CMISA circular or guidance specifically on AI in claims or automated decisions was found (searched
  in English and Hebrew on 2026-11-02). The authority has issued cyber guidance on AI-related threats
  (reported in the press, not opened) and took part in the inter-ministerial report. Lessons say the
  report is recommendations and that the binding layer is the existing circulars.
