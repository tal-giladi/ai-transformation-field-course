## Suggested additions to `references/laws-and-guidance.md`

- Israel table: **Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981 (חוק הגנת הצרכן)**: distance-sale cancellation (§14C, §14C1 four months for 65+/disability/new immigrants after a conversation), refund and fee cap (§14E), misleading (§2), punitive damages (§31A); enforced by the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority. Status: in force (2026-11-02).
- Israel table: **Consumer Protection (Cancellation of Transaction) Regulations, 5771-2010**: exceptions and in-store cancellation rules (via Kol Zchut; ⚠️ open the regulation text before citing details).
- Israel table: **Class Actions Law, 5766-2006**: consumer claims against a dealer may be brought as class actions (second schedule, item 1).
