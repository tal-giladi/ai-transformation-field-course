## One-line summary

Walk into ten different organizations and run the whole AI transformation yourself — discovery, portfolio, guardrails, a tested pilot, adoption and the steering-committee decision.

## Who it is for

New heads of AI, consultants and managers handed the AI mandate who know the strategy and governance ideas but need to start implementing on Monday.

## What you will learn

- Get a real mandate, sponsor and data access in your first week, and write a 30-60-90 plan.
- Find the real work through interviews, process walks, task inventories and a shadow-AI census.
- Build a short, evidence-backed portfolio with measured baselines and an ROI model finance accepts.
- Ship a minimum policy pack in week two: acceptable use, approved tools, vendor review, AI inventory, DPIA, risk register.
- Build and evaluate a pilot on real-shaped data, with or without code, and design human review that works.
- Run training that changes the work, and measure adoption honestly.
- Bring a scale, fix or stop decision to a steering committee and defend it.
- Handle the hard cases: regulated deployment, vendor selection, public sector, agents, "not ready", and rescuing a failing program.
