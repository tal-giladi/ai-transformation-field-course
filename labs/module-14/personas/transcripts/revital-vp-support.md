# Transcript — Revital Azoulay (VP Support)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 14.1. Sunday 29 November 2026, 09:00, Revital's office, 45 minutes. Read it as if
you were sitting in: note what the interviewer found out, what they missed, and which questions worked.*

---

**Interviewer:** Thanks for the welcome. Before anything else, can I read back what I understood the job to be,
and you correct me?

**Revital:** Go.

**Interviewer:** "Make Lumen Assist safe to ship and worth shipping: decide what it may do, prove it refuses what
it must, and own every AI agent that touches a customer account." That's your offer letter.

**Revital:** That's it. The last part matters. I'm not sure we know what's out there.

**Interviewer:** What would "done" look like on 27 January?

**Revital:** One page Yaron can read in two minutes: what the agent may do at Lumen Live, what it may not, and why.
And numbers I can defend.

**Interviewer:** Numbers on what?

**Revital:** How much it resolves. Maya's dashboard says about sixty-three percent. I don't fully trust the
definition, but the handoff queue is real: my team gets about fifteen a day from the widget.

**Interviewer:** What can I decide on my own, and what needs someone else?

**Revital:** You can pause the agent for a safety reason, with me. Roadmap is Maya's. Tokens are Andrei's.
Contracts are Amir's and Nurit's.

**Interviewer:** And if I think it shouldn't ship on the ninth of February at all?

**Revital:** Then bring me something that ships instead. "No" without a plan, Yaron won't hear.

**Interviewer:** Fair. Who approves things today when the agent can't, credits above the limit, for example?

**Revital:** Support can credit up to five hundred per case; above that the account manager. For the agent,
approvals would go to my shift leads, Boris and Hiba. They each have maybe half an hour a shift for approvals
and escalations. Not more.

**Interviewer:** So roughly an hour a day in total.

**Revital:** On a good day.

**Interviewer:** Has anything gone wrong in the beta so far?

**Revital:** (pause) Yes. On Sunday the twenty-second a help-centre article on downgrades was replaced by a
marketing campaign draft. The agent read it and gave customers credits for unused annual months. For about ten
days, we think. Yonatan found it from a customer's email. I switched it off at twenty past one and back on at
four, after the article was fixed. Billing reversed what wasn't invoiced yet.

**Interviewer:** How much money?

**Revital:** A few thousand, I think. Fatma has the number.

**Interviewer:** Is there a write-up?

**Revital:** No. I said "a content issue, handled" at exec staff. I didn't want to give Maya's critics a weapon two
months before Lumen Live. I know. Help me write it properly.

**Interviewer:** I'll start there this week. Who should I talk to first?

**Revital:** Maya tomorrow, Andrei and Yonatan Tuesday, Nurit Wednesday. The rest when you're ready.

**Interviewer:** One request: before anything I find goes to exec staff, you see it first, privately.

**Revital:** Please. No surprises for me in a room with Yaron.

**Interviewer:** And the ninety-day plan by the tenth of December.

**Revital:** Yes. Short.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you want me to stay away from in the first month?

**Revital:** Pricing. And the support headcount discussion. Not yours.

*What a reader might note:* the read-back and "done" were settled early; asking plainly "has anything gone
wrong?" surfaced the 22 November incident, and the interviewer asked for the money and the write-up. The
approval capacity came out as a number. The interviewer never asked what the 50% target means for support's own
2027 plan, or whether other teams run their own bots, so both stayed unasked.
