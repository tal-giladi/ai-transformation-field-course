# Expert deliverables — Lumen SaaS

What an experienced head of AI operations produced in this engagement, one file per stage. Use them in lesson
14.6 to compare with your own work, after you have written yours: reading them first turns the engagement into
copying. They are one good answer, not the only one; the rubric in the module project says what any strong
answer needs. All people and companies are fictional; nothing here is legal advice.
