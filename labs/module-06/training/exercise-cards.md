# Exercise cards — "Your drafts, your call" (session 1, lesson 06.2)

Eight cards for the hands-on block of the 60-minute session. Each card is one real customer message
from Harbor & Pine's helpdesk export (`support/emails.csv`, May–September 2026), with the facts the
panel shows beside it and a draft as the AI assist panel produced it. Cards 7 and 8 are written for
this session in the style of the data room: the export has no safety or legal-threat message, and the
WhatsApp tally has no message text.

Print the cards for the trainees' languages (each group gets its own two languages plus one Hebrew
card and card 8). Trainees decide, for each card: **used as is / edited / discarded**, the reason
(wrong fact, wrong language or register, tone, promises something, missing information, other), any
flag, and what they would send. The answers are in `trainer-answer-key.md`; do not hand it out.

All customers and people are fictional. Dates are last year's: work with the facts shown on the card.

## Card 1 — Hebrew · where is my order? (EM-32160, 23 May 2026)

**Customer message**

> שלום, הזמנתי ספה תלת מושבית Arbel לבן לפני כמה שבועות. רק רציתי לוודא שהמשלוח עדיין בזמן ל-31/5? יש לנו אירוע משפחתי. תודה, רותי גולן

*Gloss: I ordered the Arbel 3-seat sofa in white a few weeks ago. Just checking the delivery is still on time for 31/5 — we have a family event.*

**Beside the draft:** order SO26004946 (web store HP-117302) · container MSKU7712045, port arrival 21/5 · **ETA to customer: 26–28/5 (updated 18/5 by logistics)** · promised date at checkout: 31/5.

**Draft**

> שלום רותי,
> תודה שבדקת איתנו. הספה שלך הגיעה לנמל ב-21/5, ולפי העדכון האחרון של מחלקת הלוגיסטיקה המשלוח אליך צפוי בין 26 ל-28 במאי, כלומר לפני האירוע. נשלח לך SMS יום לפני עם חלון ההגעה.

*Gloss: Thanks for checking. Your sofa reached the port on 21/5, and according to logistics' latest update delivery is expected between 26 and 28 May, before the event. We will send an SMS the day before with the arrival window. Regards, Harbor & Pine Customer Service.*
