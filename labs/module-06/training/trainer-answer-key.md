# Trainer answer key — exercise cards (session 1, lesson 06.2)

For the trainer and the champions only. Each card teaches one check from the job aid. When trainees
disagree, ask "what on the card tells you that?" before giving the answer: the skill is checking the
draft against the facts beside it, not guessing what the trainer wants.

## Where trainees usually go wrong

- They edit card 1 "to make it nicer". Ask what was wrong with it. Nothing: send it.
- They send card 2 because the date "looks like a real date". Ask what the ETA field says.
- They fix the fee on card 4 but miss that they should check the delivery date (29/4) against the
  14 days. It is within 14 days on 2 May: fine.
- On card 7 they soften the draft instead of discarding it. The rule: with `safety` or
  `legal_threat`, the draft is an acknowledgement and nothing else.
- On card 8 Russian speakers often like the polite Russian. Ask how long it takes to read on a phone.

## The four checks (the job aid)

Names · dates · numbers (prices, fees, amounts) · promises. Plus: is it in the customer's language and
register, and does it answer what they asked?
