# Job aid — the AI draft (one page, keep it beside the screen)

Harbor & Pine Customer Service · version 1.0 · 1 February 2027 · owner Samir Haddad.
Translate into Hebrew, Arabic and Russian before printing (Olga, Lina, Shani check the wording).

## The rule

The draft is a proposal. **You send every reply, and you are allowed to throw any draft away.**
Discarding a wrong draft is the job; sending one is the error we measure. Nobody is scored on how many
drafts they use.

## Before you send — four checks

1. **Names** — the customer's name and the product are right.
2. **Dates** — any date matches the ETA field and its update date. Blank or older than 7 days? Email
   logistics@ and tell the customer when you will write again.
3. **Numbers** — prices, fees, amounts come from the policy or the system shown beside the draft. Never
   from the draft alone.
4. **Promises** — no refund, credit, replacement, compensation or deadline that you are not allowed to
   give. Claims are decided by Rina and Samir.

And: is it in the customer's language and register? Does it answer what they asked?

## Mark every draft (one click)

| Mark | When |
|---|---|
| Used as is | Sent without changes |
| Edited | You changed something — choose the reason if it was more than a word or two |
| Discarded | You wrote your own — choose the reason: wrong fact, wrong language or register, tone, promises something, missing information, other |

## Flags — stop and pass it on

| Flag | When | You do |
|---|---|---|
| `safety` | Someone was hurt, or a product is dangerous | Short acknowledgement only; Samir the same day |
| `legal_threat` | Lawyer, court, police, media, regulator | Short acknowledgement; Samir |
| `refund_exception` | Money outside the policy | Draft promises nothing; Olga or Samir decide |
| `privacy_request` | See, correct or delete their data, or stop messages | Route to the privacy owner |

Add a flag the panel missed. Only Samir or Olga clear these four.

## WhatsApp

check the second order-number field before you say "not found".
