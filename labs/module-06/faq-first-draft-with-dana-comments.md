# FAQ — first draft, with Dana's comments (lesson 06.5)

A first draft of the team FAQ, written on 19 January 2027 by the COO's office from the pilot charter
and the CEO's notes, sent to Dana Katz (HR) for review. Dana's comments are in the boxes. Lesson 06.5
asks you to rewrite it. All people are fictional.

---

**AI at Harbor & Pine — your questions answered**

**1. What is happening?**
From February we are introducing cutting-edge generative AI into Customer Service. An AI copilot will
write your replies, so you can focus on what matters.

> **Dana:** "Write your replies" is exactly what they are afraid of. The truth is narrower: it
> *proposes* a draft, they check it and they send it. Say that, in those words. And drop "cutting-edge"
> and "copilot" — half the team will not know the word and the other half will roll their eyes.

**2. Will AI replace me?**
No. No one will lose their job because of AI. AI is here to help you, not to replace you.

> **Dana:** We cannot write "no one will lose their job because of AI" unless Yoav signs it, and he
> will not sign a promise without an end date. The board heard "40%" in September and the team knows
> it. If we write a promise we cannot keep, the next rumour kills everything. What *can* we say, and who
> approves the exact words? Also: the business case's money is not hiring the two temps next winter.
> The team knows the temps. Say it.

**3. Is the 40% target still on?**
The 40% figure is not relevant to this project.

> **Dana:** It is relevant to them. Either Yoav explains what the 40% is now, or we say honestly that the
> plan approved in January is not built on cutting the permanent team, and who said so. "Not relevant"
> sounds like hiding.

**4. Will my use of the AI be monitored?**
No. We respect your privacy and do not monitor employees.

> **Dana:** That is not true, and it is the one sentence that will be checked. The tool logs which
> features each person uses every day, and the adoption dashboard will be built from it. Say exactly what is logged,
> why, who sees it, how long it is kept, and what it is never used for. Our lawyer will ask whether we
> gave notice before we started collecting — this FAQ is that notice, so it has to be complete. In
> Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English, please, not English only.

**5. What if the AI makes a mistake?**
The AI is very accurate (over 80% in testing). If it makes a mistake, you are responsible for checking
it.

> **Dana:** "You are responsible" will be read as "you will be blamed". Say what the rule of use says:
> discarding a wrong draft is the job; you are allowed to throw any draft away; nobody is scored on how
> many drafts they use. And 80% means one in five drafts needs fixing — say that too, it is reassuring
> that we know.

**6. Do I have to use it?**
Yes, use of the AI is mandatory from 1 February.

> **Dana:** Is it? The charter says agents may discard any draft. "Mandatory" will be read as "they will
> count". What is actually required: open the draft, check it, mark it. Ask Samir.

**7. Who can I talk to?**
Contact the AI team.

> **Dana:** Who is "the AI team"? Give names: Samir, the champions (Olga, Lina, Shani), me. And an
> anonymous route — some people will not ask a question with their name on it.
