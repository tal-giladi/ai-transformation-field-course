# Transcript — Larisa Gurevich (head of Moked 106)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 11.2. Tuesday 1 December 2026, 14:00, Larisa's office overlooking the
106 floor, 50 minutes. The interviewer is the Head of Digital Services. Translated from Hebrew.*

---

**Larisa:** Before you start: 106 is not the problem. The departments are.

**Interviewer:** That's what I want to understand. I'm not here about a chatbot; I'm here about how 106
works. Can you walk me through what happens when a resident calls about a parking permit?

**Larisa:** They choose the language in the menu. An agent answers. If it's general, the agent answers
from the website. If it's their file, we can't see Rishuyit properly, so we open a task for the permits
desk to call back.

**Interviewer:** How long does a callback take?

**Larisa:** The SLA is two working days. We schedule them within SLA 96% of the time.

**Interviewer:** Scheduled. And done?

**Larisa:** *(pause)* That's the department's side.

**Interviewer:** I'll say this once: nothing from today will be quoted with your name. I'm trying to
understand the numbers, not to blame anyone. How is first-contact resolution counted?

**Larisa:** It's closed at first contact. That includes when we transfer it, or open a ticket for the
department, or schedule a callback. My people did their part.

**Interviewer:** So a resident whose callback never comes is counted as resolved.

**Larisa:** In my report, yes. I defined it in 2019 so my team wasn't blamed for other people's work.
Is it wrong? From where I sit, no.

**Interviewer:** That makes sense from your seat. Do you know how many callbacks are actually made?

**Larisa:** Not in my report. The callback module has a "completed" date. Nobody asked me for it.

**Interviewer:** And residents who call again about the same thing?

**Larisa:** A new call. We don't link them.

**Interviewer:** Which languages can your agents answer in?

**Larisa:** Three Arabic, four Russian, one Amharic, two English. I lost one Arabic speaker in March and
a Russian speaker in June. Not replaced.

**Interviewer:** What happens to an Arabic caller at 16:00 when the three are busy?

**Larisa:** After 90 seconds the system sends them to the general queue. A Hebrew speaker answers.
Some manage; some hang up.

**Interviewer:** Do you have numbers on that?

**Larisa:** The export has the IVR language and the agent. You can count.

**Interviewer:** What would help your team most?

**Larisa:** Departments that call back. Two more language speakers. And if you build anything, build it
for my agents first, not for the residents.

**Interviewer:** Why for the agents?

**Larisa:** Because my agents know when an answer smells wrong. A resident doesn't.
