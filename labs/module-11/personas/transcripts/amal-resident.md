# Transcript — Amal Nassar (resident)

*Pre-run phone call for lesson 11.2. Tuesday 8 December 2026, 18:30, 20 minutes, arranged by the
Resident Service Division with Amal's agreement; the division head agreed in advance that her
application's outcome would not be discussed. The interviewer is the Head of Digital Services.
Translated from Arabic (an Arabic-speaking colleague, Omar Jaber, joined to interpret).*

---

**Interviewer:** Good evening, Mrs Nassar. Thank you for agreeing to talk. I work on the city's website
and services. I can't decide anything about your application today; I'm here to understand what
happened, so that letters and answers become clearer. Is now a good time?

**Amal:** I hope this is not another "we'll call you back".

**Interviewer:** It isn't. Could you tell me what happened, from the beginning, in order?

**Amal:** My husband got a new job in July with a leasing car. Our street has resident parking. I
applied online on the sixteenth of August and attached the letter from his employer.

**Interviewer:** And then?

**Amal:** On the twenty-fifth a letter came. In Hebrew. "Rejected. Does not meet the criteria, section 3
of the procedure. For enquiries, Moked 106." What is section 3? I don't know.

**Interviewer:** What did you do?

**Amal:** I called 106. I pressed Arabic but a Hebrew speaker answered. She said leasing cars are not
eligible. Then I wrote on WhatsApp on the first of September. They said the department will call me in
two working days. Nobody called. On the tenth I called again and got an Arab agent. He said, "I think
the rule changed, send it again". Send what again? The same letter?

**Interviewer:** Three contacts, three different answers.

**Amal:** Three answers and no reason.

**Interviewer:** What would have made the decision acceptable to you, even if it was a no?

**Amal:** If the letter said which condition, and which paper I need, and how to object. In Arabic. If
it's no because of a rule, I accept a rule. I don't accept "section 3".

**Interviewer:** Did you know you could object?

**Amal:** No. Can I?

**Interviewer:** There is an objection route; I'll make sure the department explains it to you. I can't
tell you more than that today.

**Amal:** Write it down, please.

**Interviewer:** I have. Would you use an automatic answer on the website for questions like this?

**Amal:** For "how much" and "which papers", yes, if it answers in Arabic and shows where it found it.
For my file, no. I want a person who can see my file.

**Interviewer:** That's very clear. The Resident Service Division will contact you this week about the
objection route. Thank you.

**Amal:** Thank you for listening. Write "section 3" in your report.

*[end of transcript]*
