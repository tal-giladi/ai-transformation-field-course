# Transcript — Eyal Ben-Haim (chief of staff)

*Pre-run conversation for lesson 11.1. Sunday 29 November 2026, 08:40, Eyal's office in the mayor's
suite, 15 minutes ("I have a meeting at nine"). The interviewer is the Head of Digital Services.
Translated from Hebrew. Read it as if you were sitting in: note what was found out, what was missed,
and which questions worked.*

---

**Interviewer:** Thanks for making time. Before I send any plan, I'd like to understand what the mayor
needs. What happened that made this urgent?

**Eyal:** You saw the WhatsApp storm in al-Zahra? Hundreds of messages. People waiting weeks on parking
permits, nobody calls back. Then the local site ran "106: the number that doesn't answer". The mayor
read it twice.

**Interviewer:** So the problem he wants to solve is residents not getting answers.

**Eyal:** Exactly. And a chatbot answers 24/7, every language. Other cities have it. It's modern.

**Interviewer:** What does the mayor need to be able to say on 4 February?

**Eyal:** *(pause)* That we heard them. That something changed. A chatbot is the obvious thing to show.

**Interviewer:** If it weren't a chatbot, what else would count for him?

**Eyal:** Honestly? Anything people can feel. But it has to be real on the night, not "a committee".

**Interviewer:** How many people at the conference?

**Eyal:** About 600. He speaks at 19:30. I want a live demo on the screen.

**Interviewer:** There's a ₪600,000 line in the draft budget. Where did it come from?

**Eyal:** I asked the treasurer on 12 November. The council votes on the 22nd.

**Interviewer:** Has the director-general seen the plan for the chatbot?

**Eyal:** Ronit knows the mayor wants it. You'll talk to her, no? She's… thorough.

**Interviewer:** I'm meeting her tomorrow. I'd like to bring her a mandate she can sign, so it's hers
and not only the mayor's office's. Is that all right with you?

**Eyal:** Fine, as long as it doesn't become a year-long process.

**Interviewer:** You mentioned in the email a company that offered a free pilot.

**Eyal:** CivicTalk. I met them in Eilat in October. They work with 30 authorities. Very professional.

**Interviewer:** Did you see their product working somewhere in Israel?

**Eyal:** A demo on a laptop. It answered everything.

**Interviewer:** What I'd like to do is find out, with our own questions, whether any assistant can
answer our residents correctly in Hebrew, Arabic and Russian. I'll have something you can tell the mayor
tonight: that we're testing it on real residents' questions and he'll have a concrete announcement on
the 4th.

**Eyal:** "Concrete" meaning a chatbot?

**Interviewer:** Meaning something true that residents will feel. I'll bring you options by 17 December.

**Eyal:** OK. But I want to see something before the conference. The mayor hates surprises.
