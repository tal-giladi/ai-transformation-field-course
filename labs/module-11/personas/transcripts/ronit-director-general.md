# Transcript — Dr. Ronit Shemesh (director-general)

**Interviewer:** A draft: "Within ten weeks, find out why residents don't get correct answers about

**Interviewer:** I'll check that first. Anything else that could stop this?

**Interviewer:** I'll ask to meet him before anything is decided.

**Ronit:** Good. One page every two weeks. Thursday.
