*Pre-run desk visit for lesson 11.2. Wednesday 2 December 2026, 09:00–10:00, the parking-permits desk at
the Resident Service Centre. The interviewer is the Head of Digital Services; three residents came to

**Tsega:** Are you from IT or from the mayor?

Fix the letters before you build a bot.
