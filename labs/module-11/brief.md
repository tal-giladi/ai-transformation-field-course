# Engagement brief: Givat Oren municipality

All people, organizations and data are fictional.

## How you were brought in

You are the **Head of Digital Services** of the Givat Oren municipality: a city of about 221,000
residents in central Israel whose residents speak Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, Amharic and English at home.
You have worked here for four years. Your unit (you, two content editors, a UX designer and half a
data analyst) runs the website, the online forms and the app's content, inside the Information Systems
Division (CIO: Dmitri Kogan). Nobody has ever asked you about AI.

On **Thursday 26 November 2026 at 21:48** the mayor's chief of staff, Eyal Ben-Haim, emailed you
(`data-room/city/mayor-office-email-2026-11-26.md`):

> "The mayor wants a citizen chatbot like other cities have. Residents are fed up with waiting on
> 106 … He wants to launch it at the residents' conference on 4 February: 'Givat Oren answers you,
> 24/7, in your language'. You're our digital person, so it's yours. … Can you send me a plan by
> Sunday?"

That is the whole mandate. You are the **accidental owner**: handed the subject because of your job
title, with no budget, no authority over the departments that own the content, and no team for it.

## The stated ask

"A citizen chatbot like other cities have", live on the website for the residents' conference on
**Thursday 4 February 2027**.

## Money and authority

| Spending authority | None. A purchase is a requisition to the treasurer; a commitment binds the city only when the mayor and the treasurer sign it; above the regulatory thresholds it needs a tender or a recorded exemption (`data-room/procurement/procurement-procedure-extract.md`) |

| Your time | Your day job continues. Your CIO agrees you may spend about half your time on this until February |

## Who you report to

Formally to Dmitri Kogan (CIO). The ask came from the mayor's office. The first thing you need is a
mandate from the person who can give one: the director-general.

## Constraints you know about

- Everything you write may be requested under the Freedom of Information Law, 5758-1998, unless an
  exemption applies. Write as if it will be.
- The website must meet the accessibility regulations; anything you add to it must too.
- Moked 106 agents are municipal employees represented by the works committee; a 2019 local agreement
  covers changes to their work.
- Municipal elections are in 2028. The mayor wants something to announce on 4 February.
