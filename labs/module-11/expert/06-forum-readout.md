# 06 · Readout to the management forum, decision record and handover

Management forum, Tuesday 2 February 2027, 08:30–09:30. Chair: Dr. Ronit Shemesh. Present: Eyal
Ben-Haim, Adv. Samer Haddad, Orna Ziv, Larisa Gurevich, Eli Saban, Dmitri Kogan, Yaakov Mizrahi
(treasurer); Moshe Azulay for the item on 106. Pre-read sent Thursday 28 January.

## Front page

**One sentence.** Residents are not missing a chatbot: they are missing one correct answer in their
language and a reason they can use; we have fixed the content, the letters and the callback measure,
and an assistant for 106 agents passed our test in four languages, so it goes to agents first and to
residents only when it passes again on their real questions.

**Recommendation (one, in five parts).**
1. Publish knowledge pack v1 as the website's permits-and-fees section in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and
   English by 1 March; each page shows its owner and effective date; the 2025 fee table removed; any
   council or division change updates the pack within five working days (owners named).
2. New decision letters from 1 March: the condition not met, the document that would meet it, the
   objection route; in the applicant's chosen language. The 33 leased-car applications refused since
   1 July re-examined under procedure PRK-4 by 28 February.
3. Moked 106 reports callbacks completed by the promised date and permits-family repeat contacts
   monthly, from February; the Q3 definitions kept beside them for one year.
4. The permits-and-fees assistant goes to 106 agents in shadow mode on 15 February and live for agents
   on 15 March if it holds the gates; the register entry is published on 1 March.
5. Budget line 1611-780 (₪600,000, re-labelled by the finance committee on 22 December): two
   Arabic-speaking agents for 106 (≈ ₪492,000 a year loaded) and the translation and content work
   (≈ ₪108,000); no chatbot licence in 2027 H1.

**Recommend-against.** **Against a public chatbot on the website on 4 February, and against
CivicTalk's "free pilot" in any form.** On our own questions, an assistant reading today's website is
right 52% of the time and carries an outdated fee or rule in 17 of 93 answers (illustrative), because 9
of 24 permits pages are wrong, mostly in Arabic, Russian and English (measured); the free pilot is a
₪522,000 commitment nobody authorised and a head start before a tender (legal adviser).

**What the mayor says on 4 February** (agreed with Eyal on 31 January): "From 1 March, every answer from
the city in your language, every refusal with its reason, and every callback counted until it is made;
we publish the numbers every month. Our 106 team is testing an assistant in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and
English. It goes to residents when it passes."

**What a "no" means.** The site keeps the 2025 rules in three languages; refusals keep saying "section
3"; any chatbot launched later inherits both.

## Integration check (three contradictions found and fixed before the pre-read)

1. The case (17 Dec) said content published "by 15 February"; the charter assumed 14 January approval
   and translation took six weeks: fixed to 1 March everywhere.
2. The 58 net hours of the assistant appeared in a draft of item 5 as "₪7,500 a month saved": removed;
   capacity, not cash, and not the reason.
3. The register entry promised "updated when a rule changes" but no one owned the trigger: owners named
   in item 1, with a five-day rule, and the council secretary added to the distribution of fee decisions.

## Rehearsal of the hardest finding (with Eyal, Sunday 31 January)

Hardest finding for Eyal: *no chatbot for residents on 4 February, and his October email to CivicTalk is
the procurement problem.* First attempt opened with the legal adviser's note; Eyal heard "you broke the
rules" and said he would ask CivicTalk to go ahead directly. Second attempt opened with his question
from November ("what can the mayor say on the night?"), gave him the sentence, then the screenshots of
the Arabic parking page and the outdated answers, then "Samer has written to CivicTalk; you don't need to
do anything more". He asked for a slide with the monthly numbers for the mayor: that was the moment he
owned it.
