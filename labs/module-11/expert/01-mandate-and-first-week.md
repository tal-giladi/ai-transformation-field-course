## 2. Mandate (signed by the director-general)

**Objective (Ronit's words, read back 30 November):** "Within ten weeks, find out why residents don't
get correct answers about permits and fees, and test whether **any change, including** an AI assistant
can answer them correctly in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English; bring the management forum on 2
February a recommendation and something true the mayor can announce." *(Change by the DG: "any change,
including".)*

**The stated ask, recorded as the claim this work tests:** "A citizen chatbot like other cities have",
live for the residents' conference on 4 February 2027 (chief of staff, 26 November).

**Done means:** a minuted decision at the 2 February forum; every number labelled measured, directional
or assumed; a named owner in the administration for each part of the decision; any assistant tested on
the city's own questions against gates fixed before the run; nothing in front of residents without the
legal adviser's sign-off.

**Scope.** In: permits and fees information (parking, disabled parking, signs, cafés, events, skips,
building archive, bulky waste, business licence first steps, small works, reasons and objections);
Moked 106 as far as it handles them; decision letters as far as they drive contact. Out: arnona,
enforcement, welfare (reason: different owners, different law; revisit after 2 February); any
purchase (reason: I have no authority; I prepare, the treasurer and the legal adviser decide); legal
opinions (routed to the legal adviser).

## 3. What being the accidental owner changes

| | What it means here | What goes into the mandate |
|---|---|---|
| Who gave the ask | The mayor's office, which does not run the divisions | The mandate comes from the director-general; the mayor's office is informed every two weeks |
| Budget | None of my own; the ₪600,000 line is a draft and not mine | No commitment by me; small purchases through the treasurer; the line's use is a recommendation on 17 December |
| Authority over content | None: each division owns its pages | Owners named by the DG's email, one per division, by 4 December |
| Every purchase is public | Tender or recorded exemption; signatures of the mayor and treasurer (Ordinance s.197, s.203) | No vendor receives data or a promise; vendor contact is minuted |
| Everything is public | FOI Law: write as if it will be read | Notes are factual, no adjectives, no names of junior staff |
| I stay | I will own what I launch for years | Every component has a named owner who is not me |
