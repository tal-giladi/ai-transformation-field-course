## The answer first

Residents do not lack a chatbot. They lack **one correct answer, in their language, and a reason they
can use**. About a third of Moked 106's work is permits-family questions, a third of those are the
same resident coming back within two weeks, and many come back because the answer they got was out of
date, in the wrong language, or a callback that never came. An assistant that reads today's website
would repeat the outdated answers.

## Findings

1. **The website contradicts the council's 2026 decision in the languages residents use (M).** Of 24
   permits pages, 9 are out of date: Arabic parking (₪90 and "leased cars not eligible"), Arabic bulky
   waste (₪120), Arabic events (14 days), Russian parking, Russian signs (₪245), English parking and
   events, and the Hebrew skip (₪60) and archive (₪60) pages. Six pages still link the 2025 fee table.
   Pages changed before 2024: Arabic 70.8%, Russian 73.3%, English 80.0%, Hebrew 2.5%. 40.7% of the
   1,240 pages have no named owner.
2. **Agents give the old answer where the rule changed (M).** The quality unit's 120 mystery-shopper
   calls: right on 74.4% of questions whose rule did not change in 2026 (n = 39), 42.3% where it did
   (n = 52); 21 answers stated an outdated figure. Larisa: agents answer from the website and
   "experience" (R).
3. **"First-contact resolution 83%" hides the callbacks (M, R).** The definition counts transfers,
   tickets and scheduled callbacks as resolved. Of 943 callbacks, 47.3% were made on time, 19.3% late,
   33.4% never; permits family 42.4% / 18.4% / 39.2% (n = 451). The report's "96% within SLA" means
   scheduled.
4. **Repeat contacts were never measured (M).** Same caller, same topic, within 14 days: 35.0% of
   permits-family contacts, 12.2% of other topics. Of permits-family contacts the report counts as
   resolved, 34.3% were followed by a repeat.
5. **Arabic callers are mostly answered in Hebrew (M).** Served in their chosen language: Arabic
   39.7% (n = 748), Russian 75.9%, Amharic 26.7%, English 89.6%. Arabic callers moved to a Hebrew agent
   wait a median 134 seconds against 74 for Hebrew callers. 3 of 24 agents speak Arabic; two language
   speakers left this year and were not replaced (M, R). In the mystery-shopper calls, 10 of 30 Arabic
   callers were answered in Arabic (M).
6. **Decision letters give no usable reason (M, R).** All 2,300 letters this year were in Hebrew. Half
   the parking-permit rejections (151 of 293) carry R-07 "does not meet the criteria (section 3 of the
   procedure)". Rejections with R-07 bring 2.36 linked 106 contacts each, other codes about 1.05,
   approvals 0.16.
7. **The parking desk applies a rule that was abolished on 1 July (M, R).** The desk checklist is
   version 3 (2024): "leased / company car: not eligible". Since 1 July, 63 leased or company cars came
   with the employer letter procedure v4 asks for; of the 55 decided, 33 were rejected (28 with R-07).
   Two of five clerks follow v4 (R, Tsega). One of them is Amal Nassar's application, 2026-PRK-0945.
8. **Event applicants who read the Arabic page are refused as late (M).** Rejections for late
   submission (R-08) are 48.8% of decided Arabic-speakers' event applications, 9.2% of Hebrew
   speakers'. The Arabic and English pages still say 14 days.
9. **A chatbot already talks to residents (M).** "Ruti", built by a business-licensing clerk on a free
   no-code platform with a private account, is on three pages since 10 March: 640 conversations; every
   reply in Hebrew, including to 187 questions in Arabic, Russian or English; 433 replies state an
   outdated or invented figure (e.g. sign ₪245 in 83, café ₪160 in 62, "leased cars not eligible" in 53,
   an invented ₪1,200 restaurant fee in 58); 46 messages contain a nine-digit number and 28 a mobile
   number. It breaches procedure IS-07 (third-party script, external service, private account). The
   clerk built it in good faith (R).
10. **The FOI answer of 14 September is wrong (M).** It said the municipality uses no AI. Ruti, the
    enforcement vendor's plate-reading cameras and the translation vendor's machine translation were not
    counted (R, Dmitri).
11. **Skip permits were charged at the 2025 rate until 15 March (M).** 87 permits, ₪10,920 charged
    against ₪13,650 at the 2026 rate.

## What it means

The fastest improvement for residents is not a new channel: it is the same answer everywhere, owned and
dated, and a reason in every refusal. An AI assistant can help agents give that answer in four
languages, but only from content someone owns; and it should be tested on our own questions first.
