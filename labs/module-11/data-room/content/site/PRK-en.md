# Resident parking permit

Who is eligible: a Givat Oren resident whose address in the Population Register is in the city, for a car registered in their name. Leased and company cars are not eligible for a resident parking permit.

Fee: the permit for the first car in the household is free. A permit for a second car costs ₪90 a year.

Documents: a copy of your ID card with the appendix (Givat Oren address) and the vehicle licence.

How to apply: the online form on the website or the Resident Service Centre, 4 HaMeyasdim Street.

Processing time: up to 21 working days from a complete application.

Validity: until 31 March; renew from 1 February each year.
