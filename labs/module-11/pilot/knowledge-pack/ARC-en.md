# Copy of a building file from the archive

Who may ask: the property owner, or someone with a power of attorney or the owner's written consent.

Fee: a digital copy by email ₪40 per file. A printed copy ₪40 plus ₪3 per page.

Processing time: up to 10 working days.

Apply with the online form on the website.
