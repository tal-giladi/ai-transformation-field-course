# Resident parking permit

Who is eligible: a Givat Oren resident whose address in the Population Register is in the city, for a car registered in their name. A leased or company car that the resident uses regularly is eligible with a letter from the employer or the leasing company.

Fee: the permit for the first car in the household is free. A permit for a second car costs ₪120 a year.

Documents: a copy of your ID card with the appendix (Givat Oren address) and the vehicle licence. For a leased or company car: a letter from the employer or the leasing company naming the driver.

How to apply: the online form on the website or the Resident Service Centre, 4 HaMeyasdim Street.

Processing time: up to 10 working days from a complete application.

Validity: until 31 March; renew from 1 February each year.
