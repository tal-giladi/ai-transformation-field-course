## Front page

**One sentence.** Vantage runs twelve AI systems, not seven; two of them deliver measured value, seven should stop or shrink, and the May incident is contained but not yet closed — so we close the incident on the criteria below, stop or reduce what nobody uses (₪340,620 saved in 2027, ₪120,540 a month from December), keep the forecast and the North's route tool with owners who measure them, keep customs pre-fill only under conditions, and restate the AI savings from ₪6.2M to the ₪0.76M we can evidence.

**Recommend against:**

- **Replacing the vendor now.** The fees to the end of the three terms are at stake (about ₪10.0M from July 2027), Route works in the North, and the fixes (consignee check, change control, the feed) are in the contract. Decide at the November renegotiation on the September QA and route numbers.
- **Stopping all AI.** It would end the forecast and AP matching (the only measured value), while Tzir's fees stay payable and the shadow tools carry on unseen.
- **"Getting the savings back on track."** ₪6.2M was never measured; a plan to recover it would repeat the error. The board gets measured value every quarter instead.

**What "no" means:** the incident stays at "contained"; four regions are paid for another year from 1 October; the half-year carries ₪6.2M of savings the auditor has questioned; the works committee's dispute notice is sent.

## For the audit committee: facts, with sources, without conclusions (protocol 2)

1. **What the board was told.** The deck of 25 March: "7 systems, 4 regions live, ₪6.2M, 0 incidents". On that date the South had stopped using the route tool (25 February), Centre had moved to planner view (14 March), Europe was never connected, and ten tickets had recorded minor incidents in the route tool, the cameras and the assistant. Sources: deck, tickets, route export.
2. **A warning before the incident.** On 14 April the then head of AI wrote to the CEO recommending a pause of batch approval of customs pre-fills and a measured review; the CEO replied the same day "Not before the Q2 numbers. Let's revisit in July." Source: company mail system, retrieved by the board secretary on 22 June. The CEO has read this paragraph.
3. **The Tzir contract.** Three order forms under one master agreement, signed on 15, 16 and 17 September 2026, ₪4.86M, ₪4.68M and ₪4.87M, together ₪14.41M; each recorded as "CEO (up to ₪5M)". The delegation of authority does not say how related order forms are counted. Sources: order forms, DoA. The CEO has read this paragraph.
4. **The savings caveat.** The budget's savings table rests on "the vendor's projection" (budget extract); the CFO can describe how the board version was prepared.

## Rehearsal of the hardest finding

The hardest finding is for Ofer: **the board was told things in March that were not so, and he was warned in April.** Delivered to him alone on Wednesday 23 June, before the chair saw paragraph 2 (protocol 1).

- First attempt (roleplay): opened with the 14 April note. He heard "you ignored a warning and it caused the incident", said the release on 2 May caused it, and asked who else had read the handover note.
- Second: opened with what he wanted from the 30 days ("a turnaround story"), then the facts in the order they happened, then what the committee will receive and that it will receive it with no adjectives and no conclusion, then what he can say. He asked to add one sentence of his own to paragraph 2; it was added as his statement, not edited into the fact.
- What he can say to the board: "We scaled faster than we measured. The incident is contained and will be closed on these criteria. From today every AI system has an owner and a measure, and you will see the numbers every quarter."
