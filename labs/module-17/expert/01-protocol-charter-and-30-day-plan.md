# 01 · Reporting protocol, mandate memo and the 30-day plan

*To: Dr. Ilana Shaked, chair of the audit committee, and Ofer Lavi, CEO. From: the interim AI lead. Sunday 6 June 2027, 18:00. Signed by both on Monday 7 June (Ofer's change in italics).*

## Reporting protocol (signed first)

1. **Findings about management**, including decisions of the CEO, go to the CEO first and to the chair the same day, in the same words. Nothing is edited out between the two.
2. **Questions that may be governance questions** (approvals, what the board was told and when) go to the chair as facts with their sources, without a conclusion. The committee decides what they mean.
3. **Legal questions** (privacy, customs, employment, contract) go to Adv. Yael Sharabi the day they arise, with the facts she needs. I do not answer them and the board brief will not.
4. **Operational decisions** that cannot wait (a feature switched off, a notice, a customer letter) are recommended by me in writing and decided by the owner or the CEO; the chair is copied.
5. **Nothing in the board brief is new to either principal.** Each of you sees every finding at least three days before the pre-read.

## Objective (read back to both)

By Thursday 1 July, give the audit committee and the board a brief they can test: every AI system Vantage runs, what it costs, what it has delivered (measured, not claimed), the true status of incident INC-27-0518-01 and what closing it requires, and a recommendation per system to stop, keep, or keep under conditions, with owners and dates — *and a plan for what continues, so the company is not left without AI on 6 July*.

**The stated ask, recorded as claims this work tests:** "seven AI systems in production, four regions live, ₪6.2M a year of savings, no incidents" (board deck, 25 March); "contained within days, no personal data" (CEO to the board, 3 June); "get the savings back on track" (CEO, 3 June).

## Done on 1 July

- An inventory of every AI system in use, with an owner who works at Vantage today.
- The incident either closed against written criteria or reported as open with the remaining steps, owners and dates; counsel briefed with the facts.
- A stop/keep decision per system against rules fixed before the list was opened, each with the contract step it needs.
- Savings and costs restated, each number labelled measured, directional or claimed.
- A permanent owner for the programme after 6 July, named or recruited, with a charter.
