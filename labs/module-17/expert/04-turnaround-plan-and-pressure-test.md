# 04 · The turnaround plan, the audit rules and the pressure-test of the inherited programme

*To: Ofer Lavi and Dr. Ilana Shaked. Thursday 24 June 2027, agreed in the meeting with both (changes in italics). The audit rules were fixed and sent to both on Sunday 13 June, before the inventory was complete.*

## What a turnaround is here

Not a new strategy. Three things in order: **stop the harm** (the incident, the review that exists only on paper, the monitoring nobody agreed), **stop paying for what nobody uses**, and **keep and prove what works** with an owner who measures it. Trust comes back from the order, not from a message: each audience sees one promise kept before it is asked for anything.

## The audit rules (fixed Sunday 13 June)

| Rule | When it applies | Action |
|---|---|---|
| R1 Harm open | An open incident, or harm to customers or people not yet bounded | Suspend the part that causes it until written conditions are met |
| R2 No basis | People monitored without the consultation clause 14 requires, or data sent where no agreement covers it | Stop, or bring in if the work is needed |
| R3 Not used | Active below 10% of billed for 60 days | Stop, or reduce to active users plus 10% |
| R4 Value measured | A comparison exists and the risk is controlled | Keep, with a named owner |
| R5 Value plausible | Unmeasured, risk controlled | Keep under test: 90 days, a measure agreed now |
| R6 Outside the register | Found in licences, cards, proxy, tickets | Register, then apply R1–R5 |

Every action carries an owner, a date and the contract step it needs. The rules are applied by system and region, never to "the programme".
