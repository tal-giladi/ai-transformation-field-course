## The one-page case (as sent)

**Ask.** Agree on 24 June a turnaround plan that closes the incident on facts before 1 July, stops seven things, reduces two, keeps three with owners, brings three in from outside the register, and gives the board on 5 July numbers it can test.

**Why now.** The incident is open: 7 mismatches and 6 cross-customer opens after the fix, 2,462 records of private individuals in opened manifests, 9 customs queries past their requested dates. The Tzir region notice must go by 1 July or four regions are paid for another year. The external auditor returns to the booked savings in August.

**What it costs.** Nothing new. It saves ₪340,620 inside 2027 and ₪120,540 a month once in effect; the 2027 forecast falls from ₪7.51M to ₪7.17M. The overrun against ₪5.2M remains and is explained, not hidden.

**What it does not do.** It does not decide reporting, customs corrections, discipline or the approval questions; those go to counsel, the licensed customs agent, HR and the audit committee.

**Labels.** Every number here is measured from Vantage's own data except where marked.
