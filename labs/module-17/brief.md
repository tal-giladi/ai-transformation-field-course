# Engagement brief: Vantage Logistics

All people, organizations and data are fictional.

## How you were brought in

You are the **interim AI lead** (ראש/ת תוכנית ה-AI בפועל) at Vantage Logistics (ואנטג' לוגיסטיקה), an Israeli logistics and freight-forwarding group listed on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange: about 1,200 people, trucking, warehousing and customs brokerage in four regions — North, Centre, South and Europe (a forwarding office and a small trucking unit in Rotterdam). You have run AI programmes before, inside and outside companies; you have never worked for Vantage.

On Thursday 27 May 2027 Vantage's head of AI & innovation, Itai Barkan, resigned with immediate effect, nine days after an incident in the customs module of the company's AI platform. On Thursday 3 June the board met in special session (`data-room/company/board-minutes-2027-06-03-extract.md`). Its resolution reads:

> "The last AI lead left; the board wants answers in 30 days. An interim AI lead will be engaged for 30 days, on the audit committee's proposal, reporting to the chair of the audit committee and to the CEO."

Dr. Ilana Shaked, the external director who chairs the audit committee, called you on Friday 4 June. You start on **Sunday 6 June 2027**.

## The stated ask

"The last AI lead left; the board wants answers in 30 days." The board's questions, in the minute: what AI systems are running, what they cost, what they have delivered, what happened in the May incident and whether it is closed, and whether and how to continue. The CEO added: "and a plan to get the savings back on track". Pre-read to the audit committee on **Thursday 1 July**; the board on **Monday 5 July 2027**. Your engagement ends on Tuesday 6 July.

## Constraints you know about

- **Privacy.** The Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981 (חוק הגנת הפרטיות, התשמ"א-1981), as amended by Amendment 13 (in force since 14 August 2025), and the Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017, apply to the personal data in Vantage's systems; whether an event must be reported, to whom and when, is for the general counsel and outside counsel to decide. Vantage Europe's staff and contacts are also covered by the GDPR.
- **Customs.** Vantage's customs agents are licensed under the Customs Agents Law, 5725-1964 (חוק סוכני המכס, התשכ"ה-1964), and file declarations for importers with the customs houses. What a wrong declaration means, and how it is corrected, is for the head of the customs brokerage (a licensed agent) and counsel.
- **People.** Drivers, warehouse and customs staff are covered by a collective agreement; a works committee represents them (`data-room/people/`). Monitoring of employees is governed by the agreement, by Privacy Protection Authority guidance and by labour case law; HR and counsel own the reading.
- **Listed company.** The board's audit committee has its own duties under the Companies Law; what it does with your facts is its decision, not yours.
- **Languages.** Staff speak Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, Amharic and English; Vantage Europe Dutch and English; supplier documents arrive in English and Chinese.
