# Transcript — Tigist Wondimu (shift supervisor, production, Plant K)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 13.2. Night of Tuesday 19 January 2027, 00:45–01:10, the supervisors' booth on the Plant K floor. The interviewer is the operations-excellence manager. Translated from Hebrew.*

---

**Tigist:** Twenty minutes, then I go around. You built my reason codes, so be nice.

**Interviewer:** I'll try. How does a stop get recorded at night?

**Tigist:** The operator stops, chooses a reason on the terminal. MECH, ELEC, HYD, MOULD, MAT, WAIT_TECH. You know them.

**Interviewer:** When do operators choose WAIT_TECH rather than the real cause?

**Tigist:** When nobody came for a long time. From the operator's side that is the truth. The technician is with another machine. So WAIT_TECH at night hides what really broke.

**Interviewer:** And when does the stop end in ProdTrack?

**Tigist:** When the machine is producing again and the first parts are approved.

**Interviewer:** You keep a log of night calls to Vladi.

**Tigist:** Since March. Because machines were waiting while they talked. Yaron and Muhammad joined. About two calls a week.

**Interviewer:** What are they about?

**Tigist:** Codes nobody knows, the hot runner, "can we continue with a cavity closed", where a part is. Sometimes the lockout with the accumulator.

**Interviewer:** Can I have a copy of the log?

**Tigist:** It's on the shared drive, under supervisors. Take it.

**Interviewer:** Which stops are the longest at night?

**Tigist:** Moulds. A hot runner or a water leak at one in the morning, and the mould waits for tooling at half past seven. The machine is fine. We move the operator to another machine, if there is one.

**Interviewer:** How does language work on your shift?

**Tigist:** Half Arabic, then Hebrew, Russian, some Amharic. I explain the risk sheets orally. The Hebrew sheet, most don't read.

**Interviewer:** And contract workers?

**Tigist:** Same, but they change often. The agency trains them. Sort of.

**Interviewer:** What would you want from this project?

**Tigist:** A technician who can answer at night without phoning Vladi. A rule for moulds at night. And training in their languages.
