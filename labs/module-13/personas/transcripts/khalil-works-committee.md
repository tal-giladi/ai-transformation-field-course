# Transcript — Khalil Mansour (chair of the works committee)

*Pre-run meeting for lesson 13.4. Tuesday 23 February 2027, 14:00, the committee room at Plant K, 50 minutes. Present: Khalil, the operations-excellence manager, Michal Aharon (HR). Translated from Hebrew and Arabic.*

---

**Khalil:** I wrote a letter on the twelfth of January. I am waiting for an answer in writing.

**Interviewer:** You'll have one. Michal is here so that what we agree can be written. I came before anything is decided. Can I tell you what we are looking at, and then ask you what workers would need?

**Khalil:** Go on.

**Interviewer:** Three things. Recording breakdowns properly: what failed and what was done, at the machine. A list of the company's machines and moulds that everyone uses. And an assistant on a tablet that answers technicians' questions from the manuals and procedures, in Arabic, Hebrew, Russian and English.

**Khalil:** And the cameras?

**Interviewer:** The vendor offers them. I am not recommending them.

**Khalil:** Not recommending, or no?

**Interviewer:** I can only say what I recommend. Management decides. I'll recommend that it is written: no cameras.

**Khalil:** In 2025 you put tablets on the machines with a personal code. Nobody asked us.

**Interviewer:** I know. I was part of that project. It was a mistake not to come to you first.

**Khalil:** *(pause)* Good that you say it.

**Interviewer:** The recording will have the technician's id, because a work order needs it. What would workers need for that to be acceptable?

**Khalil:** That it is not used to measure who is slow. No report per technician. No discipline from it. Written.

**Interviewer:** What else?

**Khalil:** That they can write in Arabic. That training is paid, in work hours, in Arabic. That the joint committee from clause 14 meets before it starts, not after.

**Interviewer:** And the assistant? Will people use it?

**Khalil:** Young ones, yes. If it's correct. If it says something dangerous, I will be the first to stop it.

**Interviewer:** Then I'd like a technician from the committee in the group that tests it.

**Khalil:** I'll think about who.

**Interviewer:** Michal, can we send Khalil a draft protocol in Hebrew and Arabic by next week?

**Michal:** By Thursday.

**Khalil:** Then we talk again Thursday.
