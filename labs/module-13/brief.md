# Engagement brief: Kinneret Plastics

All people, organizations and data are fictional.

## How you were brought in

You are the **operations-excellence manager** (מנהל/ת מצוינות תפעולית) of Kinneret Plastics: a family-owned injection-moulding company in the Galilee with about 600 people, two plants and three shifts. Plant K, near Tiberias, is the original factory (26 machines); Plant G, near Karmiel, was bought in January 2025 (14 machines). You have been here three years. You ran the OEE programme, you designed the downtime reason codes in the MES in 2025, and you ran the mould-change (SMED) project. You report to Dalia Mizrahi, VP operations and Plant K manager. Your team is you and a half-time analyst.

On **Thursday 7 January 2027**, at the annual management meeting, the CEO and owner, Amnon Shaked, said (`data-room/company/management-meeting-2027-01-07-minutes.md`):

> "2027 is the year of AI. We start with predictive maintenance. I saw at the conference in December what Predictiva does: the machines tell you before they break. [You] lead it, with Dalia."

That is the whole mandate. You are an **internal lead with a day job**: you know the plant and the people know you, which helps and which is also the problem. Some of the data you are about to judge is yours.

## The stated ask

"Predictive maintenance with AI", starting with a vendor's platform on all 40 machines. First recommendation to management on **Tuesday 23 March 2027**.
