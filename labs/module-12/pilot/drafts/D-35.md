**Question:** Does a liability cap also cover gross negligence?

**Answer**

1. A consequential-damages exclusion removes any claim for lost profits. [ARM-2020-455 ¶2]
2. A clause excluding consequential damages excludes every claim for lost profits. [ARP-2022-285 ¶3]
3. Without express words, a cap does not cover intentional breach or gross negligence. [ARP-2022-083 ¶4]
4. Capping liability at twelve months of fees is a reasonable allocation in a supply contract with a large customer. [FCR-2023-077 ¶3]
