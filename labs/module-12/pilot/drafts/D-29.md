**Question:** Must a party prove its damage to collect agreed compensation?

**Answer**

1. A claimant under an agreed-compensation clause need not prove its actual loss. [ARM-2023-381 ¶2]
2. Late-delivery compensation above one percent of the price per day has been reduced as excessive. [ARP-2024-286 ¶3]
3. Some actual damage must be proven before agreed compensation is awarded. [ARM-2021-296 ¶2]
4. Excessive agreed compensation can be cut down by the court, not struck out entirely. [ARM-2018-466 ¶3]
