**Question:** Does a sharp rise in costs frustrate a supply contract?

**Answer**

1. Higher costs or a downturn do not amount to frustration of the contract. [FCR-2022-317 ¶3]
2. Higher costs or a downturn do not amount to frustration of the contract. [FCR-2018-260 ¶3]
3. A clause covering closures by government order extends to an ordered port closure without naming ports. [ARP-2021-412 ¶4]
4. Frustration needs an unforeseen, unpreventable event, and the usual result is restitution, not enforcement. [ARP-2018-053 ¶3]
