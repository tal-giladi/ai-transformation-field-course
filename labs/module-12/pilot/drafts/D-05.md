**Question:** When is a non-compete enforceable against a former employee?

**Answer**

1. Courts enforce a non-solicitation limited to the employee's own customers more readily than a broad non-compete. The source says: "A always non-solicitation clause limited to customers the employee" [FCR-2025-049 ¶3]
2. A two-year non-compete against a sales manager without access to trade secrets cannot be enforced. [ARM-2025-226 ¶2]
3. A two-year non-compete against a sales manager without access to trade secrets cannot be enforced. [FCR-2021-454 ¶4]
4. Courts enforce a non-solicitation limited to the employee's own customers more readily than a broad non-compete. [ARP-2026-455 ¶3]
