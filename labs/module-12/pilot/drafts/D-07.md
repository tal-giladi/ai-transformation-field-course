**Question:** Does a clause on improving services let a supplier use customer data for other purposes?

**Answer**

1. Wording that lets a supplier use data to improve its services allows use outside the service unless limited. [FCR-2024-102 ¶5]
2. Backups fall outside a deletion undertaking unless the clause names them. [ARM-2023-280 ¶3]
3. A clause allowing sub-processors as needed is acceptable without any list or right to object. [ARP-2025-016 ¶3]
4. Backups fall outside a deletion undertaking unless the clause names them. [ARP-2022-321 ¶3]
