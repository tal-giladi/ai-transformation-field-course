**Question:** Can a party recover the lost profit of a deal that was never signed?

**Answer**

1. A non-binding term sheet does not force a closing, yet good-faith duties still apply to the negotiation. The source says: "does always not force a closing, yet good-faith duties still apply" [ARM-2024-378 ¶3]
2. The lost profit of an unsigned deal is recoverable only exceptionally, where signing was certain. [ARP-2021-455 ¶7]
3. Walking away from advanced negotiations without good reason can create liability for reliance damages. [FCR-2023-148 ¶5]
4. Walking away from advanced negotiations without good reason can create liability for reliance damages. [FCR-2022-047 ¶3]
