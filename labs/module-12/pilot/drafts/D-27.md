**Question:** Is a cap of twelve months' fees reasonable in a supply contract?

**Answer**

1. Capping liability at twelve months of fees is a reasonable allocation in a supply contract with a large customer. [ARM-2023-189 ¶2]
2. A consequential-damages exclusion removes any claim for lost profits. The source says: "damages and are excluded by a clause that excludes consequential" [FCR-2019-333 ¶3]
3. Courts enforce a liability cap negotiated between companies as written, so long as the wording is clear. [ARM-2023-033 ¶2]
4. Lost profits that follow directly from the breach survive a clause excluding consequential damages. [ARP-2022-285 ¶4]
