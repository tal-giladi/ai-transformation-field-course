**Question:** Does paying the employee for a non-compete make it enforceable?

**Answer**

1. Special payment for a non-compete helps enforcement but is not enough by itself. [ARM-2022-267 ¶5]
2. A two-year non-compete against a sales manager without access to trade secrets cannot be enforced. [ARP-2022-117 ¶3]
3. Special payment for a non-compete helps enforcement but is not enough by itself. [FCR-2021-124 ¶5]
4. A two-year non-compete binds a sales manager who signed it knowingly. [FCR-2020-460 ¶3]
