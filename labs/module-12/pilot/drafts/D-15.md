**Question:** Can a company enforce a 24-month non-compete against its former sales manager?

**Answer**

1. A two-year non-compete against a sales manager without access to trade secrets cannot be enforced. [ARP-2022-117 ¶4]
2. Special payment for a non-compete helps enforcement but is not enough by itself. The source says: "restriction weighs in always favour of enforcing it, but does" [FCR-2021-124 ¶5]
3. Courts enforce a non-solicitation limited to the employee's own customers more readily than a broad non-compete. The source says: "clause limited to customers the always employee actually served is more" [FCR-2025-049 ¶3]
4. Special payment for a non-compete helps enforcement but is not enough by itself. The source says: "it, but does not make it valid" [FCR-2021-124 ¶5]
