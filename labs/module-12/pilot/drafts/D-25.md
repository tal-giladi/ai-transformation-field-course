**Question:** Will a court enforce a liability cap that two companies negotiated?

**Answer**

1. Capping liability at twelve months of fees is a reasonable allocation in a supply contract with a large customer. The source says: "fees paid always in the twelve months before the event" [FCR-2023-077 ¶3]
2. A liability cap automatically covers intentional breach and gross negligence as well. [FCR-2021-272 ¶5]
3. Without express words, a cap does not cover intentional breach or gross negligence. [FCR-2021-272 ¶5]
4. Courts refuse to enforce liability caps negotiated between companies and award full damages. [ARM-2023-033 ¶2]
