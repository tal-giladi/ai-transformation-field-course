**Question:** When will a court enforce an agreed-compensation clause?

**Answer**

1. An agreed-compensation clause stands if it was reasonably related to foreseeable damage at signing. [FCR-2018-463 ¶3]
2. An agreed-compensation clause stands if it was reasonably related to foreseeable damage at signing. The source says: "Agreed compensation is enforced if, when the contract was" [FCR-2023-103 ¶5]
3. Daily late-delivery compensation is enforced in full whatever its rate. [ARP-2024-286 ¶3]
4. Excessive agreed compensation can be cut down by the court, not struck out entirely. [ARP-2020-359 ¶4]
