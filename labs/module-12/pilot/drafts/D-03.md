**Question:** What happens if force majeure notice is given late?

**Answer**

1. Late force majeure notice can cost the party the defence. [ARM-2024-270 ¶2]
2. Only events expressly listed are covered, so an unlisted port closure falls outside the clause. [FCR-2019-214 ¶3]
3. A clause covering closures by government order extends to an ordered port closure without naming ports. [FCR-2024-069 ¶3]
4. A clause covering closures by government order extends to an ordered port closure without naming ports. [ARP-2021-412 ¶7]
