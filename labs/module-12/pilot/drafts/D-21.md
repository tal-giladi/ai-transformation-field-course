**Question:** What are the conditions for frustration of a contract?

**Answer**

1. Frustration needs an unforeseen, unpreventable event, and the usual result is restitution, not enforcement. [FCR-2018-260 ¶8]
2. Frustration needs an unforeseen, unpreventable event, and the usual result is restitution, not enforcement. [ARM-2024-270 ¶2]
3. Higher costs or a downturn do not amount to frustration of the contract. [FCR-2024-294 ¶5]
4. A clause covering closures by government order extends to an ordered port closure without naming ports. [ARM-2021-057 ¶2]
