**Question:** Is it bad faith to negotiate in parallel with another buyer?

**Answer**

1. Parallel talks with another party are allowed, but hiding them when asked directly can be bad faith. [FCR-2022-279 ¶3]
2. The lost profit of an unsigned deal is recoverable only exceptionally, where signing was certain. [ARP-2021-455 ¶4]
3. A non-binding term sheet does not force a closing, yet good-faith duties still apply to the negotiation. [ARP-2023-277 ¶4]
4. Any parallel negotiation with a third party is in itself bad faith. [ARM-2021-454 ¶2]
