# Engagement brief: Atlas & Rowe

All people, firms, clients and vendors are fictional.

## How you were brought in

On Tuesday 3 November 2026 Adv. Yoav Dagan, managing partner of Atlas & Rowe, called you. A partner at
another firm had heard about your work at Northwind and Galil. You met him the same afternoon in the
firm's offices on Menachem Begin Road, Tel Aviv. His words, from your notes:

> "Everyone wants Harvey or CoCounsel, or now this Israeli one, Parshan. Noa says we are the last firm
> in Tel Aviv without it; Shmuel says it invents cases and will cost us our clients. The partners are
> split down the middle and they vote on 14 January. I need someone who isn't a partner to tell the
> partnership what to buy, with our own numbers. I can't be the one, I have to chair the vote."

You are an **independent consultant**, not a lawyer of the firm and not its legal adviser. You sent a
proposal on Wednesday; Yoav signed it on Thursday 5 November.

## The stated ask

"Everyone wants Harvey/CoCounsel; partners are split." Concretely: whether to sign Parshan AI's offer
(90 seats for 24 months; 20% discount if signed by 31 December 2026), as proposed by Adv. Noa
Friedman at the partners' meeting of 22 October. Yoav's version in the signed proposal: "Advise the
partnership, on the firm's own data, how Atlas & Rowe should use AI in its legal work from 2027, what
to buy, if anything, and on what terms; test it on our own material where possible."

## Who you report to

Yoav Dagan, managing partner (the sponsor). The management committee meets with you twice (3 December,
12 January): Yoav Dagan, Adv. Shmuel Atlas (founding partner, litigation), Adv. Noa Friedman (partner,
Tech & IP), Adv. Orit Levinson (general counsel and ethics partner), Eli Ben-Hamo (finance director,
non-voting). The decision belongs to the partners' meeting (26 partners, one vote each).

## What you have on day one

- A signed confidentiality undertaking. You may not see client files or privileged documents; the firm
  gives you extracts with client names replaced by codes. Carmel Retail Group, the firm's
  fourth-largest client, has agreed to be named and to meet you.
- The data room (`data-room/`, start with `INDEX.md`): HR and billing extracts, a quarter of time
  entries for three groups, IT's proxy and DLP logs, an AI-use survey, policies, the vendor's documents,
  the precedent bank's index, and the 78 research documents the head of knowledge management exported
  for the vendor's proof of concept.
- Meetings Yoav has booked for week 1: Eli Ben-Hamo (Monday), Tamar Golan, head of KM (Tuesday), Orit
  Levinson (Wednesday). Noa Friedman and Shmuel Atlas "when they have time".
- Not yet: associates (Yoav: "the partners will want to know who you're talking to"), the vendor,
  the Carmel GC (Yoav wants to "prepare the ground" first), the PoC outputs.

## Constraints you know about

- Parshan's discount expires on 31 December 2026. Noa will push to sign before the vote.
- Hanukkah is 4–12 December; the end of December is the billing rush.
- Carmel's panel review closes on 31 January 2027.
- You are not a lawyer. Questions of professional ethics, privilege and client consent go to Orit
  Levinson and, through her, to outside ethics counsel. This course is not legal advice.
