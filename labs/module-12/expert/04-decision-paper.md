## 3. Citations: the design rule

A research answer may go to a partner only after a **citation gate**: the document exists in the
corpus; the paragraph exists; any quote is verbatim; the document is current after curation; a reader
(a person, helped by a model) confirms the paragraph says what the sentence claims. The first four are
rules; the fifth is judgement. The vendor's "zero hallucinations" fails on the fourth and fifth in its
own PoC (10 superseded, 12 misstated of 80).

## 4. The billable hour

- On hourly matters (52% of fees) a research hour saved removes ≈ ₪814 of fees; there is no backlog to
  absorb freed hours. On fixed fees (19%) it is margin; Tech & IP's packages overrun by 38%.
- Clients take their share: the NDA history shows both the loss on hourly billing and the recovery
  under a fixed price (₪1,422 per hour vs ₪860).
- **Recommendation:** price repeat work at fixed prices (Carmel's twelve contract types first; a
  quarterly subscription for routine advice), keep hourly for disputes and deals, and say in the
  engagement letter how AI affects fees. Reject the draft policy's "bill the time a task would normally
  take" pending outside ethics counsel (it bills time not spent).
- Partner compensation rewards personal billed hours; the committee should know it shapes the vote.
  The consultant proposes no scheme.
