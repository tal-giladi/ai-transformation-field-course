# 06 · Partners' paper, readout, decision record and handover — Atlas & Rowe

Paper circulated Sunday 10 January 2027; management committee Tuesday 12 January; partners' meeting
Thursday 14 January 2027.

## Front page

**One sentence.** Our lawyers already use AI on client work without a rule, our knowledge base is
mostly unchecked, and on half our fees time saved is fees lost: put a rule and one safe tool in place
now, check our knowledge, price repeat work so we keep part of the gain, and choose a platform in June
on a test of our own documents.

**Recommendation (one, in five parts).**

1. The interim AI rule (Orit's text after outside ethics counsel) from 1 February; one enterprise
   assistant for all lawyers; consumer accounts on firm work end; consent letters to the four clients
   with AI terms by 15 February.
2. Knowledge: the 78 research documents are curated (done); the 300 most-used precedents by 31 March
   (Tamar with two associates at half time, a signing partner per group); librarian post from April.
3. Precedent-search pilot with the citation gate in Tech & IP and litigation research, 17 January –
   30 April; rule of use and stop rules as chartered.
4. Pricing: Carmel's repeat work at fixed prices for twelve contract types and a quarterly advice
   subscription, offered by 20 January; Tech & IP packages repriced on measured hours in March.
5. Platform: an RFP in April with a proof of concept on the curated corpus in Hebrew and English;
   decision at the June partners' meeting for up to 30 seats.

**Recommend-against.** Signing Parshan for 90 seats and 24 months before 31 December was declined on
17 December; the paper recommends against any whole-DMS platform contract before June: its index would
not follow our ethical walls; its own proof of concept failed a basic check on 28 of 80 citations (10
superseded, 12 misstated); its 6.2 hours were measured at other firms by self-report; and with billing
unchanged our model shows −₪3.06M of cash against doing nothing in 2027.

**What a "no" means.** Consumer tools stay on client documents; the insurer's AI questions (due 15
January) are answered without a rule; Carmel's renewal is negotiated on a 15% discount, not on a price
model.

**Cost in 2027.** Tools ≈ ₪108,000; curation and librarian ≈ ₪420,000; pricing work in house; Carmel
package ≈ 10% below 2026 spend on that work. Year 1 roughly break-even against doing nothing if Tech &
IP sells half the hours it stops losing (assumed).

## Integration check (contradictions found and fixed)

1. The decision paper said curation of the top 300 "by end of February"; the pilot charter assumed it
   by January. Fixed: the pilot uses only the 78 curated research documents; top 300 by 31 March.
2. The eval report's 1.4 minutes per citation appeared in a draft budget line as "₪1.1M of time
   saved". Removed: directional, capacity, not cash.
3. Consent letters were in the rule but had no owner or date. Added: Orit, 15 February.
4. The insurer's questionnaire was due before the vote. Orit answered it on 13 January from the facts
   in the findings memo, including the near miss, unattributed.

## Hardest finding, rehearsed

For Shmuel (privately, Monday 11 January): *a research memo with an invented case and a reversed
ruling was approved for a client in his group in October; the firm's own controls did not catch it; a
person did.* First attempt opened with the memo; he defended the associate and the firm ("we caught
it"). Second attempt opened with his own words from 1 December ("nobody checks at midnight"), then the
gate's results on the test set, then the memo as the example of why the gate must exist before any
tool, not after. He asked, "so the gate applies to everyone, with or without a tool?" — yes. He agreed
to co-sign part 3 with Noa.

2027. Dissent recorded: Noa asked that "a platform decision not slip past June"; recorded as a stop
rule on the RFP timetable.
