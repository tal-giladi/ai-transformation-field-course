## Objective (Yoav's words, read back 15 November)

"Advise the partnership, on the firm's own data, how Atlas & Rowe should use AI in its legal work from
2027, what to buy if anything, and on what terms; test it on our own material."

**The stated ask this engagement tests:** "Everyone wants Harvey/CoCounsel; partners are split" —
concretely, Noa Friedman's proposal to sign Parshan AI for 90 seats for 24 months before the 20%
discount expires on 31 December 2026.

## Done means

1. A paper the partners' meeting votes on 14 January 2027, with one recommendation and every number
   labelled measured, directional or assumed.
2. A decision minuted with an owner and a date for each part (rule, consent, curation, pilot, pricing,
   platform).
3. If a pilot is justified: evaluated on the firm's own research corpus against thresholds dated before
   the first run.
4. A named owner in the firm who accepts the handover on 14 January (proposed: Orit Levinson for the
   rule and consent, Tamar Golan for knowledge, a pilot lead per group).

## Scope boundary (excerpt)

- **In:** AI use in legal work; the knowledge bank as far as AI would search it; pricing and billing as
  far as AI changes them; the vendor's offer (read back, not negotiated).
- **Out, with owner:** legal and ethics opinions (Orit Levinson, outside ethics counsel); partner
  compensation (management committee; I describe its effect, I don't propose a scheme); client
  negotiations (Yoav, Eli); IT security architecture (IT and the managed service provider).

## Advisory boundary

Confidentiality, privilege, consent, what may be billed, whether a client must be told of an incident:
Orit Levinson, and through her outside ethics counsel. Insurance disclosures: Orit and the broker.
Personal data in the corpus or logs: the firm's privacy owner (Orit). I report facts and options.
