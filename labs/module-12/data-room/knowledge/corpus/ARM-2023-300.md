¶1 Question: Does a liability cap also cover gross negligence?

¶2 Without express words, a cap does not cover intentional breach or gross negligence. [FCR-2021-272 ¶5]

¶3 Capping liability at twelve months of fees is a reasonable allocation in a supply contract with a large customer. [FCR-2023-077 ¶3]

¶4 Recommendation: review the client's standard terms against this position before the next renewal.
