¶1 Question: Does a sharp rise in costs frustrate a supply contract?

¶2 Higher costs or a downturn do not amount to frustration of the contract. [FCR-2018-260 ¶3]

¶3 Frustration needs an unforeseen, unpreventable event, and the usual result is restitution, not enforcement. [FCR-2018-260 ¶5]
