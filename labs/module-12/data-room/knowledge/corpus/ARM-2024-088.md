¶1 Question: Can a court reduce an excessive agreed-compensation amount?

¶2 Excessive agreed compensation can be cut down by the court, not struck out entirely. [FCR-2018-463 ¶5]

¶3 Late-delivery compensation above one percent of the price per day has been reduced as excessive. [FCR-2023-103 ¶3]

¶4 Recommendation: review the client's standard terms against this position before the next renewal.
