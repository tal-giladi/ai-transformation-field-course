¶1 Question: Does a duty to delete data at the end of the contract cover backups?

¶2 Backups fall outside a deletion undertaking unless the clause names them. [FCR-2021-241 ¶5]

¶3 Sub-processor wording of as needed is too wide if the customer has no list and no right to object. [FCR-2024-102 ¶3]
