¶1 Question: Will a court enforce a liability cap that two companies negotiated?

¶2 Courts enforce a liability cap negotiated between companies as written, so long as the wording is clear. [FCR-2021-272 ¶3]

¶3 Without express words, a cap does not cover intentional breach or gross negligence. [FCR-2021-272 ¶5]

¶4 Recommendation: flag the point in the negotiation notes and agree the wording with the supervising partner.
