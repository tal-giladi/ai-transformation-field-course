# Engagement brief — Meridian Mutual

*Module 9. Fictional organization; synthetic data. Read this first, then the data room's `INDEX.md`.*

## How you came in

Meridian Mutual Insurance Company Ltd. is a mid-size Israeli insurer: motor, home, private health and
travel, about 1,050 employees, about 36,000 non-bodily-injury claims a year, ₪3.4bn of gross
premiums. It is supervised by the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority.

In October the board approved, in principle, an AI programme budget for 2027 and created a new role:
**Head of AI Transformation**. You were hired from outside and started on **Sunday 1 November 2026**.
You report to **Ilana Bar-Lev, COO**. The board's **risk management committee** (chair: Prof. Hanna
Sela) oversees AI risk under the AI policy it recommended in June; the Chief Risk Officer, Michael
Ohana, is its secretary. You are an employee, not a consultant: you will live with what you
recommend.

## The call

Wednesday 4 November 2026, 08:30, Ilana's office. She hands you draft 2 of her proposal
(`data-room/plans/pilot-proposal-2026-11.md`) and says:

> "I want us to automate claims intake and spot fraud. We already have the tools inside Kalanit since
> September. I want a three-month pilot from 1 December, in time for the winter, and the risk
> committee on 15 December should just have to say yes. I present the proposal at the management
> meeting on Tuesday the 10th. Tell me by Sunday how you'll run it."

## Stated ask

"Automate claims intake and spot fraud."

## Budget

- 2027 AI programme: ₪1.6M approved in principle, released in stages against approved cases.
- Ilana's pilot ask: ₪240,000 from the programme, plus the Fraud Insight Pro licence (₪380,000 a year,
  three-year term) proposed separately.
- Approval levels: up to ₪250,000 the COO with the CFO; up to ₪1M the CEO; above ₪1M over the
  contract's life, the board. Anything that touches customer decisions also needs the risk
  committee (AI policy, rule 3.2).
