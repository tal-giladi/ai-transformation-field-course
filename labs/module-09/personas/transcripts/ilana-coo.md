# Transcript — Ilana Bar-Lev (COO)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 09.1. Wednesday 4 November 2026, 08:30, Ilana's office, 35 minutes. The
interviewer is the new Head of AI Transformation, on day four. Read it as if you were sitting in: note
what the interviewer found out, what they missed, and which questions worked.*

---

**Ilana:** Welcome again. You've read the proposal? Good. I want us to automate claims intake and
spot fraud, and I want to start on 1 December.

**Interviewer:** I read it last night. Before I say anything about how, can I check I understand what

**Interviewer:** That helps. So the outcome is: claimants paid sooner and fewer chasing calls,

**Ilana:** Yes. And fraud.

**Interviewer:** On fraud, what would you accept as proof that it worked?

**Interviewer:** I'll want to see how both numbers are built before I put them in front of the

**What to notice.** The interviewer got the objective in Ilana's words and read it back, separated
the decision rights (her budget, the board for Pro, the committee for anything touching claims), set
a reporting rhythm and a rule for bad news, and asked for a line to the CRO. They also turned "is
the committee aware?" into a fact: it is not.

**Questions nobody asked.** Who decided, in August, to leave the 9.4 modules switched on, and what did the June minutes say would happen first? What have the CEO and the board already been told about fraud savings? Try them in your own interview.
