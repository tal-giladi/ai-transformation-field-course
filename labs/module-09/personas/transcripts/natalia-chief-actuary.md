# Transcript — Natalia Gurevich (Chief Actuary)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 09.3. Monday 23 November 2026, 14:00, Natalia's office, 30 minutes.
The interviewer is the new Head of AI Transformation, building the baselines and the case. Read it as
if you were sitting in.*

---

**Natalia:** What do you want to know?

**Interviewer:** I have three different numbers for how long a claim takes, and I want to use the one
you'd defend. The dashboard says 10.5 days. Kalanit, from registration to closure, gives a median of 41.
From the reported date to the last payment I get 32. Which do you use?

**Natalia:** The last one. For reserving, a claim is open from the day it is reported until we stop
paying. My median for this year is about a month. Your 32 sounds right.

**Interviewer:** Why is the dashboard so different?

**Natalia:** It starts the clock at "file complete" and leaves out the SIU. It measures the part of the
process that was already fast. Closure in Kalanit is administration; nobody waits for it.

**Interviewer:** So for the claimant's wait I'll use reported to final payment, and keep the
dashboard's number as the regulatory clock it really is. Would you sign that definition?

**Natalia:** Yes, if you write it down with the source and the sample size.

**Interviewer:** It's a 1-in-40 sample of the register, 696 claims paid in 2026. Second topic: fraud.
The proposal says ₪20 million a year. How would you estimate what we lose to fraud?

**Natalia:** Confirmed fraud is about 0.4% of claims by count. What we don't detect, nobody knows.
My own range is 1 to 2% of claims paid. On ₪281 million that is ₪3 to 6 million, and I would not
defend the upper end.

**Interviewer:** And the ₪20 million?

**Natalia:** Ten percent of claims paid, times two-thirds. Ten percent is from American marketing decks.
I have a folder for numbers like that.

**Interviewer:** The SIU reports ₪6.77 million "prevented" this year. How should I read it?

**Natalia:** As activity, not savings. It is claimed minus paid on every closed SIU case: deductibles,
partial payments, people who gave up. Eran counts only confirmed fraud. So should you.

**Interviewer:** If I split it by outcome from the SIU's log, confirmed fraud is about ₪2.3 million of
the 6.77. Does that sound plausible?

**Natalia:** Plausible. Then 2.3 is your measured number and the rest is not a benefit.

**Interviewer:** Does a long cycle cost us anything you can measure?

**Natalia:** Case reserves stay open longer, and the year-end estimate is less certain. And people
who wait call. Rinat's contact centre can tell you how many calls are "where is my claim".

**Interviewer:** Last one. If I bring a case to the committee, which numbers will you challenge?

**Natalia:** Anything without a source and a date. Label what you measured, what you estimated, and
what someone told you. I will read the labels first.

**Interviewer:** [end interview]

**Natalia:** — end of interview —

---

**What to notice.** The interviewer came prepared with their own three numbers and asked which one
she would defend, then asked her to sign a definition: that is how a baseline becomes agreed. They
split "fraud prevented" by outcome before the meeting and let her confirm it.

**Questions nobody asked.** Has anyone validated the vendor's fraud numbers? What does "fraud-flagged" mean in Kalanit's back-test? Try them in your own interview.
