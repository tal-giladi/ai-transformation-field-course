# Transcript — Samira Abu-Ahmad (Claimant)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 09.2. Thursday 12 November 2026, 17:00, phone call arranged by the
complaints officer, 25 minutes, in Hebrew at Samira's choice. The interviewer is the new Head of AI
Transformation. Read it as if you were sitting in.*

---

**Samira:** Good evening. The complaints officer said you wanted to talk about my claim.

**Interviewer:** Good evening, and thank you for agreeing. I work at Meridian on how we handle claims.
I'm not calling to defend anything; I'd like to hear what happened, in order, in your words. Is that
all right?

**Samira:** Yes.

**Interviewer:** Where did it start?

**Samira:** On the fourteenth of September, a car hit mine while it was parked outside the school, at
about five. I called the next morning. I asked for letters in Arabic. They came in Hebrew.

**Interviewer:** What happened after the call?

**Samira:** About the twentieth, a list: the form, the licences, the estimate from the garage, photos.
I sent everything in three days.

**Interviewer:** And then?

**Samira:** At the beginning of October they asked for a police confirmation. Nobody said so at the
start. The car was parked, nobody was hurt. I had to go to the station to file a report just to get
the paper. I took a day off work. And they asked for the photos again.

**Interviewer:** That's a whole day for a document nobody had asked for. What came next?

**Samira:** On the twelfth, they asked again for the garage estimate, "unreadable", and a written
statement. It was the garage's normal invoice. It's in Arabic, like every invoice in my town.

**Interviewer:** Did anyone explain why?

**Samira:** I called that day. A young man said: "The system flagged your file, it's with
investigations, I can't tell you more." I filed a complaint that evening.

**Interviewer:** I'm sorry you were told that, and nothing else. What happened after the complaint?

**Samira:** Nothing for ten days. Then they paid the garage at the end of October. Nobody explained
anything. Not why it was investigated, not what they found.

**Interviewer:** What would a good explanation have looked like for you?

**Samira:** One sentence. Why my file. What they checked. What they found. In Arabic, if possible.
And that it doesn't happen to the next person.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything else about the claim I should know?

**Samira:** That's the story.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. I can't promise an outcome tonight, but I'll make sure the complaints
officer writes to you with an answer about the investigation by the end of the month, and I'll tell
her you asked for Arabic.

**Samira:** We'll see.

**Interviewer:** [end interview]

**Samira:** — end of interview —

---

**What to notice.** The interviewer said who they are and why they called, asked for the story in
order and let her tell it, acknowledged the cost (a day off work), asked what a usable explanation
would be, and promised only what they could deliver with a date.

**Questions nobody asked.** What did the claim cost her beyond the repair? Did she ever think of giving up? What does she think the reason for the flag was? Try them in your own interview.
