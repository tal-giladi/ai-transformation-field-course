# Claims manual (excerpt)

*Meridian Mutual, Claims division. Version 7.2, approved by the VP Claims on 3 March 2024. Chapters
3 (intake and documents) and 6 (special investigations). Translated from Hebrew for the AI programme.*

## 3. Intake and documents

**3.1 First notice.** A claim can be notified by the agent, by phone, through the website or app,
or at a branch. The intake clerk registers it in Kalanit the same working day.

**3.2 The documents list.** At first notice, the claimant (or the agent who notified the claim)
receives **the complete list of documents for the claim type, the same day**, with the claims rules
and the claims process document. The standard lists:

| Claim type | Standard documents |
|---|---|
| Motor own damage | claim form, driving licence, vehicle licence, garage estimate, photos |
| Motor third-party property | claim form, driving licence, third party's details, garage estimate |
| Motor theft | claim form, police confirmation, keys declaration, vehicle licence |
| Windscreen | claim form, glass invoice |
| Home water damage | claim form, plumber's report, contractor's quote, photos |
| Home storm | claim form, contractor's quote, photos |
| Home burglary | claim form, police confirmation, purchase receipts, photos |
| Home contents (other) | claim form, purchase receipts, photos |
| Health: surgery | claim form, medical summary, invoice/receipt |
| Health: specialist | claim form, invoice/receipt, referral |
| Health: medications | claim form, prescription, invoice/receipt |
| Travel: medical abroad | claim form, medical report, invoice/receipt, boarding passes |
| Travel: baggage | claim form, airline property irregularity report, purchase receipts |
| Travel: cancellation | claim form, proof of cancellation, medical report |

**3.3 Receipt.** When a document arrives, the claimant is told in writing what arrived and what is
still missing.

**3.4 Additional documents.** Ask for additional information or documents only when they are needed
for this claim, and no later than 14 business days from the day the need became clear (Supervisor's
circular 2016-9-9, section 8(a)). Do not ask for documents a reasonable claimant cannot obtain.

**3.5 File complete.** When every requested document has arrived, the handler sets the status
"file complete" (תיק הושלם). The 30-day period for a decision notice runs from that day.

**3.6 Language.** Letters go out in the claimant's language of correspondence (Hebrew, Arabic,
Russian or English templates). When a claimant asks to be called in another language, set the
*interpreter* flag in Kalanit and book the phone interpreter service.

## 6. Special investigations (SIU)

**6.1 Referral.** A handler refers a claim to the SIU when there are concrete grounds for suspicion
(examples: late notification without explanation, inconsistent accounts, documents that appear
altered, a pattern across claims). The team lead approves; the reasons are written in the referral.

**6.2 What the claimant is told.** Referral is not a decision. If more time is needed, the claimant
receives a continued-clarification notice with the reasons, at least every 90 days.

**6.3 Decisions based on an investigation.** If a decision relies on an investigation report, the
claimant is told the main findings that support it (circular 2016-9-9, annex). Investigators are
never paid by result.

**6.4 Reporting.** The SIU reports monthly: cases opened and closed, outcomes, and "fraud
prevented" (amount claimed minus amount paid on closed cases).
