# Policy on the use of artificial intelligence

*Meridian Mutual. Approved by the board of directors on 22 June 2026 on the recommendation of the
risk management committee. Owner: Chief Risk Officer. Review: annually.*

## 1. Purpose

To let Meridian use AI where it helps customers and the business, while managing the risks to
customers, to the company and to compliance with the law and the Supervisor's instructions.

## 2. Definitions

- **AI system:** a machine-based system that, for explicit or implicit objectives, infers from the
  input it receives how to generate outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations or
  decisions (following the OECD definition).
- **High-impact use:** use of an AI system that makes, or materially supports, a decision about a
  customer's claim, cover, price or eligibility, or that communicates with customers without a
  person checking.

## 3. Rules

1. Every AI system is entered in the **AI inventory** before use, with a business owner.
2. A **high-impact use** requires, before it is used in production: a risk assessment, a privacy
   impact assessment with the DPO, a test of accuracy and of outcomes across customer groups, a
   way to explain to the customer the main reasons for a decision that affects them, a named human
   oversight design, and the **approval of the risk management committee**.
3. AI features delivered inside existing software are new AI systems for this policy.
4. Customer data goes only into tools approved by the CIO and the DPO.
5. Outsourcing an AI system does not transfer Meridian's responsibility for it.
6. Any incident or complaint involving an AI system is reported to the CRO within two working days.

## 4. Roles

Business owner (accountable for the use); CIO (approved tools, security); DPO (privacy); CRO
(risk assessment, inventory, reporting to the committee); Internal Audit (independent assurance).

## 5. Reporting

The CRO reports to the risk management committee each quarter: the inventory, high-impact uses
approved and pending, incidents and complaints.
