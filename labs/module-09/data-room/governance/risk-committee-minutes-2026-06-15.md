*Meeting 3/2026, Monday 15 June 2026, 14:00–16:30, Ramat Gan. Present: Prof. Hanna Sela (chair),
two directors; invited: CEO Avner Shamir, COO Ilana Bar-Lev, CFO Eran Paz, Chief Actuary Natalia

## Item 4: Policy on the use of artificial intelligence

The CRO presented the draft policy (attached). Discussion: the definition follows the OECD; the
committee asked whether "materially supports" is clear enough. The CRO answered that the inventory
owner decides and the CRO reviews. The chair asked for the inventory to be presented at every
quarterly meeting.

**Management statement (recorded at the chair's request):** as at 15 June 2026, Meridian uses no
AI system that makes or materially supports decisions on claims, cover, price or eligibility. AI in
use is listed in the attached inventory (4 entries).

**Decision:** the committee recommends that the board approve the policy. (Approved by the board on
22 June 2026.)

## Item 6: IT change portfolio, H2 2026

The CIO presented the planned changes. Among them, the upgrade of Kalanit Claims to release 9.4
(regulatory reporting fields, new intake screens, performance), scheduled for September.

The COO noted that the release "includes optional AI modules" and that she will bring them to the
committee if Meridian decides to use them.

**Decision:** noted.
