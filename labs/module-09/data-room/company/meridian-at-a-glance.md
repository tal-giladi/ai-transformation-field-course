# Meridian Mutual — at a glance

*Welcome pack for new managers, HR and Corporate Communications, version October 2026.*

**Meridian Mutual Insurance Company Ltd.** (מרידיאן הדדית חברה לביטוח בע"מ) is a mid-size Israeli
insurer. It started in 1957 as a mutual aid fund of a transport workers' cooperative, became an
insurance company in 1979 and kept "Mutual" in its name. It is a subsidiary of Meridian Holdings
Ltd. and is supervised by the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority (רשות שוק ההון,
ביטוח וחיסכון).

## Business

| Line | What we sell | Gross premiums 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | Comprehensive (מקיף), third-party property (צד ג'), compulsory (חובה) | ₪1.45bn |
| Home | Structure (מבנה) and contents (תכולה) | ₪0.52bn |
| Health | Private supplementary health: surgery, specialists, medications | ₪1.10bn |
| Travel | Medical abroad, baggage, cancellation | ₪0.18bn |
| Other | Small business packages | ₪0.15bn |

About 70% of policies are sold through independent insurance agents (סוכני ביטוח); the rest
direct, by phone, website and app. About 1,050 employees.

## Claims

Non-bodily-injury claims (motor property, home, health, travel): about 36,000 a year. Bodily
injury and compulsory motor claims are handled by a separate litigation unit and are not part of
the claims division's dashboard.

- **Claims division** (VP Claims, Yusuf Darawshe): 212 people. Four claims centres: Ramat Gan
  (Center and Tel Aviv motor and home, and all health and travel claims), Haifa (North), Jerusalem,
  Beer Sheva (South). Intake clerks, claims handlers, team leads, the special investigations unit
  (SIU, 7 investigators plus two external investigation firms), claims quality (QA).
- **How a claim arrives:** through the agent (email or the agents' portal), by phone to the contact
  centre, through the website form or the app. Documents arrive by email, app upload, fax, or on
  paper at a branch and are scanned. Correspondence templates exist in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and
  English.
- **Seasons:** December to February is the busy season: winter storms bring water and storm damage
  to homes, and wet roads bring more motor claims. Travel claims peak after the summer holidays,
  Passover and Sukkot.
