## Cost of the claims division

- 212 people, about ₪66M a year fully loaded.
- Planning rate for a claims handler hour: **₪145** (fully loaded). Intake clerk: **₪110**.
- External investigation firms: ₪2.9M in 2025.
- Kalanit Claims maintenance and hosting: ₪3.1M a year (includes new releases).

## How we judge a proposal

- Business cases in the finance template: one-off and running costs by year, benefits by year,
  three scenarios, NPV at a **10%** discount rate, payback.
- Approval levels: up to ₪250,000 the COO with me; up to ₪1M the CEO; above ₪1M over the
  contract's life, the board. Any new system that touches customer decisions also goes to the
  risk committee (AI policy, June 2026).
- Freed handler time is capacity, not cash, until there is a decision about the roles (hiring
  freeze, temporary staff not renewed, redeployment).
- **Fraud:** I count only amounts we did not pay because fraud was confirmed. The SIU's
  "fraud prevented" figure is useful for the SIU; it is not a saving in my books.
