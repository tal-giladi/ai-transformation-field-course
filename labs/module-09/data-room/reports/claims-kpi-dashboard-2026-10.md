## Definitions (BI data dictionary, v2.3)

- *Handling time*: calendar days from status "file complete" to status "decision", claims decided in the month. Claims under special handling (SIU) are reported separately and are not included.
- *Within 30 days*: share of the same claims with handling time of 30 days or less.
- *Fraud prevented*: for SIU cases closed in the month, the amount claimed minus the amount paid.
- Bodily-injury and compulsory motor (חובה) claims are out of scope of this dashboard.
