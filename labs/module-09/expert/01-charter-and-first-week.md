# Engagement charter — AI in claims — Meridian Mutual

*Head of AI Transformation, v1.0, Sunday 8 November 2026. For Ilana Bar-Lev (COO). Template:
[engagement charter](../../../templates/engagement-charter.md), adapted for an internal lead.*

## 1. Objective (Ilana's words, read back on 4 November)

"Claimants paid sooner and fewer chasing calls, without more cost, shown before the spring campaign;
and catch fraud we don't catch today." Read back and agreed: *the outcome is the claimant's wait and
the cost of handling, measured end to end; fraud counts when it is confirmed.*

## 2. The ask, and what this charter commits to

The stated ask is "automate claims intake and spot fraud" through a winter pilot (1 Dec – 28 Feb)
of Smart Intake and Fraud Insight. This charter commits to **evidence for a decision**, not to that
pilot: by 15 December a deployment verdict on what is already live and on what is proposed; by late
January a tested pilot and a recommendation. The pilot's shape may change; Ilana hears first.

## 3. Scope

| In | Out (for now) and why |
|---|---|
| Non-bodily-injury claims: motor property, home, health, travel | Bodily injury and compulsory motor: litigation unit, different process |
| Intake, documents, handling time, SIU referral | Underwriting and pricing: not asked; would bring other rules (and EU AI Act Annex III 5(c) if ever sold in the EU) |
| The two live 9.4 modules (Smart Intake, Fraud Insight) and the proposal | Contact-centre chatbot and transcription (in the inventory already) |
| An offline extraction pilot on the data room | Anything live during December–February |

## 5. How I work

- Reporting to Ilana weekly (Thursday, 30 minutes) and **the same day** for anything urgent; never
  first in front of the CEO.
- **Dotted line to the CRO on AI risk** (agreed 4 November): the committee hears from someone who is
  not only the project's owner.
- Data: pseudonymised extracts through the DPO; no production access; every extract logged.
