*Pre-run meeting. Wednesday 10 February 2027, 14:00, President's office, 40 minutes. Translated from Hebrew.*

**Hanna:** So, have you come to tell us we are behind?

**Interviewer:** I've come to understand what the academic side needs from anything we recommend.

**Hanna:** That is a better start. What do you think we need?

**Interviewer:** The board minutes say you want the academic side involved in anything that touches how students are assessed or contacted. What does involved mean to you?

**Interviewer:** Which questions here are academic?

**Interviewer:** Later meaning?

**Hanna:** Meaning later.

**Interviewer:** What would make an early-warning system acceptable to you?

**Hanna:** That nobody is judged by it. That students know about it. That it is not wrong more often for some students than for others. Can you promise me that?

**Interviewer:** Not today. I can promise to test it before anything is recommended.

**Hanna:** Then we may get along.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything about students' rights I should read before I go further?

**Hanna:** The Student Rights Law, and our regulations. And ask counsel, not me, about the law.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. I'd like to come back with the evidence in March.

**Hanna:** Come back. Bring the files, not the slides.
