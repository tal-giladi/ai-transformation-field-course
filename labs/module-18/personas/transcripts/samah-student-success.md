# Transcript — Samah Khoury (Head of Student Success)

*Pre-run meeting. Tuesday 9 February 2027, 11:30, Student Success unit, 45 minutes. Translated from Hebrew. The interviewer said at the start that nothing would be attributed to her by name without her agreement.*

---

**Samah:** Are you here to tell me the advisers are failing?

**Interviewer:** No. I'm here to understand how a normal week goes for them.

**Samah:** A normal week? There's no normal week. Five advisers working, one on leave, almost seven thousand students.

**Interviewer:** How do they decide whom to call?

**Samah:** Since March last year, mostly from Tavla's red list. Before that, students came to us, or lecturers referred them. Now we have a list. Finally.

**Interviewer:** What's on the list?

**Samah:** A score, a colour and up to three reasons. Low activity, late submissions, things like that.

**Interviewer:** How many can the advisers really reach in a week?

**Samah:** A real conversation, with notes, is 30–40 minutes. In a peak week we manage maybe 150, with everything else.

**Interviewer:** And what happens on the call?

**Samah:** Many don't answer. Some are fine. Some are angry: "why am I on a list?" Some are blocked by finance, and we send them to finance.

**Interviewer:** Can an adviser lift a block?

**Samah:** No. We can only send an e-mail to the desk and hope.

**Interviewer:** There's a procedure for early contact after semester A. How does that work in practice?

**Samah:** SS-03. First-years who failed or missed two exams. We do it, partially. I reported partial coverage to the Dean.

**Interviewer:** Partial meaning roughly what share?

**Samah:** I'd have to check the log. Less than I'd like.

**Interviewer:** Does anyone outside the unit see the red list?

**Samah:** The lecturers get a sheet for their courses, so they can tell us if they're worried about a student.

**Interviewer:** That's useful. Could I see the contact log?

**Samah:** Tamar has the export. Please don't use the advisers' names.

**Interviewer:** I won't. The log will appear as adviser codes.
