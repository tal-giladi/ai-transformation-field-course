# Transcript — Rawan Haj-Yahya (student, union welfare officer)

*Pre-run meeting. Wednesday 17 February 2027, 12:00, student union room, 30 minutes. Translated from Hebrew.*

---

**Rawan:** What will you do with what I tell you?

**Interviewer:** I'll use it in a report to the CEO and the board, without your name unless you agree. I can't promise any change; I can promise they'll hear it.

**Rawan:** Fine. Ask.

**Interviewer:** As welfare officer, what do students come to you with most?

**Rawan:** Money and bureaucracy. Every January and every June: "I'm blocked from exams, but I don't owe anything."

**Interviewer:** Why would they be blocked if they don't owe anything?

**Rawan:** Because their scholarship hasn't gone in yet. Or the reservist grant. The system sees a balance.

**Interviewer:** What do they do then?

**Rawan:** They write an inquiry. Then they wait. Registration closes after a week.

**Interviewer:** How long do they wait?

**Rawan:** It depends. Two weeks is normal. If you write in Arabic, longer, and the answer comes in Hebrew.

**Interviewer:** How do students feel about the idea of a system that predicts who will leave?

**Rawan:** Nobody told us anything about any system. What would it predict? That I'm blocked? They know that already.

**Interviewer:** Has the college ever contacted you about your studies?

**Rawan:** Once. An adviser called last spring.

**Interviewer:** How was it?

**Rawan:** Short. She asked if I was OK. I said yes.

**Interviewer:** What would you change first, if you could?

**Rawan:** No block for a scholarship that's approved. Answers in Arabic for students who write in Arabic. And tell us what you do with our data.

**Interviewer:** I've written those down in your words.

*[end of transcript]*
