# Transcript — Avi Golan (VP Finance)

*Pre-run meeting. Thursday 11 February 2027, 10:00, VP Finance's office, 45 minutes. Translated from Hebrew.*

---

**Avi:** Show me what you think you know.

**Interviewer:** Very little yet. I want to know how finance values a student who stays.

**Avi:** Good question. Not tuition. About 70% of tuition is contribution. Teaching is fixed per section; one more student costs little.

**Interviewer:** So for a first-year in business, roughly 70% of ₪29,400 a year for the years they stay?

**Avi:** Roughly. Measured on last year's sections. Directional if you use it for next year.

**Interviewer:** Persevia's sheet says ₪10.6M a year.

**Avi:** Gross tuition, three years, 120 students, and all of them would have left without Persevia. I've read it.

**Interviewer:** What's your hurdle for line 6800-110?

**Avi:** Payback in three academic years. Anything over 250 thousand or over twelve months to the board. And I don't sign automatic renewals.

**Interviewer:** The budget mentions the exam-registration block. How does it work?

**Avi:** Since 2023 the ERP blocks exam registration automatically if the balance is above 1,500 on the check date, two weeks before exams. Bad debt went from 3.1 million to 1.9. It works.

**Interviewer:** How many students are blocked each semester?

**Avi:** In January, around twelve hundred of the students you're looking at. Most pay or sort it out.

**Interviewer:** How quickly is a block lifted after they pay?

**Avi:** Same day for card payments. Arrangements go through the desk.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you'd want protected in whatever I recommend?

**Avi:** The block. Don't come to me with "remove the block". Students who owe money must pay.

**Interviewer:** Understood. I'll bring numbers labelled measured, directional or assumed.

**Avi:** Then we'll talk again.
