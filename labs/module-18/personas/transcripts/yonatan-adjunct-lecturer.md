# Transcript — Dr. Yonatan Mekonen (adjunct lecturer, chair of the adjunct committee)

*Pre-run meeting. Monday 15 February 2027, 13:00, cafeteria, 30 minutes. Translated from Hebrew.*

---

**Yonatan:** So who sent you — management?

**Interviewer:** The CEO hired me; the board asked for it. I read your committee's letter, and I'd rather hear it from you than guess.

**Yonatan:** Then you're the first. Ask.

**Interviewer:** The letter says lecturers use AI to keep up. With what?

**Yonatan:** Feedback, mostly. A first-year course has 160 assignments. Without a tool you give everyone "good, but expand".

**Interviewer:** Which tools?

**Yonatan:** ChatGPT, Gemini, whatever people have. On personal accounts. The college never gave us anything.

**Interviewer:** What would the committee need before new rules?

**Yonatan:** To be in the room. Paid training — two hours, not a webinar at midnight. And a promise that nobody uses AI to judge our teaching. We heard course evaluations are summarised by a machine now.

**Interviewer:** I've seen that the survey tool has a summary feature. I'll check how it's used.

**Yonatan:** Thank you. And detectors.

**Interviewer:** Detectors?

**Yonatan:** Some colleagues run assignments through free AI detectors and send students to the committee on the percentage. A student who writes Hebrew as a second language gets 90%. It's wrong.

**Interviewer:** Do you know how many cases?

**Yonatan:** Ask the President's office. She chairs appeals.

**Interviewer:** Would adjuncts use an approved tool if the college provided one?

**Yonatan:** Most would. If it's on the college's account and nobody watches who uses it for what.

**Interviewer:** That's a fair condition to write down. Can I quote the committee's position, not you personally?

**Yonatan:** The committee's position, yes.
