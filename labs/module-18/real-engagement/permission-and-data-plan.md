# Permission letter and data-handling plan (optional real engagement, lesson 18.4)

Two short documents you write **before** you open any real data in your own workplace. Fill the brackets; delete what does not apply. This is not legal advice: your organization's legal adviser and data protection officer (DPO) decide what is lawful; you ask them, in writing, and keep their answer.

## Part 1 — Permission letter (one page)

**To:** [Name, role: the manager who can authorise work across the units you will touch]
**Copy:** [DPO or privacy lead] · [information security officer] · [HR, if people's work or data about employees is involved] · [workers' committee chair, if the work could change how people work]
**From:** [You, role]
**Date:** [date]

**1. What I am asking to do.** Run a [6]-week review of [process / unit] to find where AI could help and where it should not, as part of a training programme. Output: [findings memo, readiness assessment, a short recommendation]. Nothing is built or changed in production without a separate decision.

**2. Why.** [One sentence in the organization's own terms: the problem the manager cares about.]

**3. What I will look at.** [Systems and exports, by name] · [documents] · interviews with [roles, about how many].

**4. What I will not do.** No data leaves the organization's systems or approved tools. No personal data goes into any AI tool not approved for that data. No recording of interviews without consent. No individual performance conclusions about any employee. No contact with customers or students without [name]'s approval.

**5. Who sees what I write.** [Manager] and the people named above. Interviewees are not named or quotable without their agreement.

**6. What I take out.** Nothing that identifies the organization, its people, its customers or its numbers. For my training portfolio I will write a **synthetic or anonymised** version, which [name] will review before it leaves.

**7. Stop.** You or the DPO can stop the work at any point. If I find an exposure of personal data or a safety issue, I report it to [name] and [DPO] the same day and stop that line of work.

Approved by: ______________ Date: ________ Conditions: ______________________
