## Objective (read back to Yaron, 8 February; his correction in italics)

"By 4 May, find out why first-year students leave Tzurim and what would make fewer of them leave, test whether an AI early-warning system such as Persevia would do that better than other options, and bring the board one recommendation with costs, risks and dates *that the board can track every semester*."

**The stated ask, recorded as the claim this work tests:** "Dropout rose from 12% to almost 20%; an AI early-warning system (Persevia) that predicts who will leave will bring first-year dropout to 12% by 2029."

## Done (checkable on 4 May)

1. One recommendation in the board's minutes, with an owner and a date for each part.
2. Every number labelled measured, directional or assumed; the dropout figure's definition stated and signed by its owner.
3. Any AI option judged against criteria written before the vendor's evidence is reviewed (by 1 March).
4. No student data shared with any vendor before the DPO's written review (board decision 2).
5. Any exposure of personal data found during the work reported to the DPO and the VP Administration the same day, by phone, then in writing.
