*Expert deliverable. Pre-read sent to the board on Tuesday 20 April 2027; integration check run 15–19 April; rehearsal with the CEO on Sunday 2 May; board meeting Tuesday 4 May, 17:00–18:30.*

## Decision-ready case — front page

**The ask, in one sentence.** Approve ₪540,000 from line 6800-110 to stop blocking students from exams for grants the college has approved but not posted, to answer students in their own language with a tested assistant, and to put rules on the AI the college already uses — and adopt a corrected retention measure with a new target.

**What we found.** First-year leaving has been flat at 11–13% for three cohorts (measured); the 19.5% and 17.7% in the December report count reservist freezes and internal transfers. 45% of first-year leavers in 2025-26 had missed exam registration because of a block (measured); 60% of blocks were grants approved but not yet posted (measured). An AI risk score has been running since March 2026, flags reservists and Haredi-track students five to eight times more often without higher leaving (measured), and its list was shared through an open link (closed 7 February; the DPO is assessing).

**Recommendation.**

|---|---|---|---|
| Restated measure: "left the college" by cohort, freezes and transfers apart; target first-year leaving ≤ 10% for cohort 2027 | Registrar; CEO | Signed 20 Apr; board 4 May | ₪30k dashboard |
| Block fast lane before the June check | VP Finance | Live 30 May (gate G1) | ₪150k |
| Inquiry assistant: shadow, then routing, then drafts by language | Registrar | 9 May shadow; G2 4 Jul; G3 5 Sep | ₪135k |
| Policy pack, DPIA practice, approved staff tool, paid training | VP Administration; President for academic rules | 18 May; 25 May; Oct | ₪195k |
| Contingency | VP Finance | — | ₪30k |

**What we recommend against.** No Persevia pilot or contract in 2027: its evidence comes from one US college in a year with a statewide free-tuition programme; its terms send three years of student data incl. health information to the US with a licence to train on it; and it cannot see or lift a block (45% of first-year leaving). Re-decide in March 2028 on a proof of concept under our conditions. Tavla Engagement Insights stays paused for outreach until the DPO's conditions are met.

**Labels.** Measured: the flat leaving rate, the blocks, the inquiry times, the score's flag rates. Directional: ≈ ₪1.4M a year of contribution from the fast lane (range ₪0.7–2.8M); ≈ 510 staff hours of capacity. Assumed: costs pending quotes; that removing timing blocks removes most missed registrations.

**Cost of waiting.** The June 2027 check runs on 6 June. Without the fast lane, about 640 students are blocked again, most of them for the college's own timing; the first chance to measure the effect moves to January 2028.

**What a "no" means.** The June check blocks as before; the red-list practice has no rule; the board's 12% target is met on paper by correcting the measure and missed in substance.

**Rehearsal (roleplay with the Yaron persona, two runs, coach after each).** Run 1 opened with the BI-07 table; he heard "you misled the board" and said "then I'll ask Persevia to show the board their list". He asked "so I tell the board the number was wrong?" — answer: "the registrar tells them the number now counts what it should; you tell them what you did about it." He agreed to present the corrected measure himself.
