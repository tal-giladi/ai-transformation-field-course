# 07 · Adoption plan

*Expert deliverable. Version 1.0, 15 April 2027. Three groups change their work: the service centre and collections (the fast lane and the inquiry assistant), the advisers (a rule-based alert instead of the red list), and lecturers (an approved tool and rules). Consultation with the adjunct committee on 23 March and 13 April; the administrative staff's representatives informed on 13 April.*

## 1. Workflow redesign

| Step | Before | After | Who absorbs what |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grant approved | Credited in a monthly batch; may land after the check | Approval feeds the ERP nightly; an approved grant counts as paid at the check | Reservist desk enters approvals the same day (≈ 10 minutes a day) |
| Block check | Blocks every balance over ₪1,500 | Blocks real arrears only; SMS to the student with the link for an arrangement | Collections: fewer blocks, same rule |
| Arrangement request | Up to two to three weeks in January | Decided in 3 working days; two temporary staff in the two block windows | Collections desk (+2 temporary, 3 weeks twice a year) |
| Block inquiry | Student picks "exams"; reassigned after days | Routed to collections on day 0, urgent flag | Exams office loses ≈ 300 misrouted inquiries a year |
| Any inquiry | Read, route, write in Hebrew | Assistant routes and drafts in the student's language; staff edit and send | Service centre: reviewing replaces writing; Arabic speakers review Arabic drafts (≈ 3 hours a week each, from their current translation work) |
| Adviser outreach | Red list from the LMS | Alert: first-year blocked at the check date, or missed two semester A exams (SS-03) | Advisers: about 800 students a year (SS-03 plus blocked first-years), about 100 a week in the four weeks after each check, inside the 150 a week at peak |
| Lecturer feedback with AI | Personal accounts, sometimes with names | Approved tool, names removed, students told in the syllabus | Lecturers: two paid hours of training |

## 2. Training

| Group | Session | Length | Exercises | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Service centre and collections (≈ 40) | Working with the assistant: route, edit, send, override, report | 60 minutes, by role | Ten real inquiries from the test set's dev split, incl. one negative test and one Arabic draft | 2–6 May, before shadow |
| Advisers (6) | The new alert, what to say, referrals that work (collections direct line) | 45 minutes | Three role-plays: blocked first-year; a student who asks "why did you call me?"; a reservist | 16 May |
| Lecturers (328 adjuncts + senior staff on request) | The approved tool and the rules: what never goes in, feedback without names, detectors are not evidence | Two paid hours | Rewrite feedback on two anonymised assignments; decide three "may I?" cases | September–October, in Hebrew and with Arabic and Amharic speakers among the trainers |

## 3. Champions

Two champions in the service centre (one Arabic-speaking), one in collections, one adviser; four lecturers named by the adjunct committee. Office hours: Tuesdays 12:00–13:00 in the service centre in May–July; online for lecturers in October. Champions bring back every override reason and every "may I?" question to the monthly review.

## 4. Adoption dashboard (spec)

| Measure | Definition | Source | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified use (service centre) | Share of inquiries closed with an assistant draft that was edited or accepted, by staff member's unit | Portal log | ≥ 70% by October |
| Override rate | Route changed by staff / routed | Portal log | Watched, not targeted; reasons reviewed |
| Arabic replies in Arabic | Replies in Arabic / inquiries written in Arabic | Portal | ≥ 80% by October (8% in 2025-26) |
| Reopen rate by language | Reopened / closed | Portal | Arabic ≤ 15% |
| Blocks at check, missed registrations | Count, and share of blocked students unblocked after the close | ERP, SIS | ≤ 300 blocks; misses −50% |
| Licence use (office assistant) | Active days in 30 per licence | Admin console | Unused licences reallocated quarterly |

**Privacy of usage data.** Usage is reported by unit, never by person, and never used to evaluate lecturers or staff (written commitment to the adjunct committee, 13 April). The DPO approved the dashboard's fields on 14 April.

**Quiet non-adoption check (monthly).** For each unit: who stopped using drafts, when, and back to what — asked of the unit head in three questions: what do people do instead, what does the draft get wrong, what would make it worth using.
