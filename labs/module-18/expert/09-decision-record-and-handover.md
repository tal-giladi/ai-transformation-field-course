## Decision record — DR-2027-05

**Decision asked.** Approve the retention plan and ₪540,000 from line 6800-110; adopt the corrected measure; decline the Persevia pilot for 2027.

**Decided.**

1. The board adopts "first-year leaving" (statuses 51, 52, 58 on 1 October after the first year) as the retention measure, with freezes and transfers reported separately. Cohort 2025: 11.5%. Target for cohort 2027: ≤ 10%. The 12% target of 20 January is withdrawn. Owner: registrar; reported every November.
2. ₪540,000 released in two stages: ₪330,000 now (fast lane ₪150k, inquiry pilot ₪135k, measure ₪30k, contingency ₪15k); ₪210,000 on 1 September if gates G1, G-June and G2 are met (staff tool and training, governance, remaining contingency). Owners as in the case.
3. **Stop rule:** if the June 2027 check blocks more than 400 students, or the bad-debt provision rises above ₪2.1M, the fast lane returns to the board in July.
4. Persevia: no pilot and no contract in 2027. The CEO will tell Persevia the college's conditions for a proof of concept (de-identified data, Israel or EU region, no training licence, no automatic conversion, compared with the college's own rule). **Re-decision: March 2028**, against the readiness bar of 1 March 2027.
5. Tavla Engagement Insights remains paused for outreach; the DPO reports in December 2027.
6. The policy pack is noted; management adopts it on 18 May; the academic council decides the evidence rule on 25 May.

**Dissent, in the director's words.** "We are a private college competing on innovation. Saying no to the only AI product in this plan is a message to the market I don't like. I accept the conditions, not the timing." (Director, finance committee.) Recorded; the CEO will brief the board in November on the 2028 proof-of-concept conditions.

**What a "no" would have meant (as presented).** The June check blocks as before; no rule on lists of students; the 12% target met on paper and missed in substance.
