# Engagement brief: Tzurim Academic College

All people, organizations, products and data are fictional.

## How you were brought in

You run a small, two-person AI transformation practice. On Thursday 28 January 2027 Dr. Eitan Rozen, chair of the board of **Tzurim Academic College** and an acquaintance from a previous engagement, called you: the board had just asked the CEO to bring in an outside consultant on student retention and AI. The CEO, Yaron Bar-Lev, called you the next day. You signed a statement of work on Wednesday 3 February.

Tzurim is a private, non-budgeted academic college in the southern Shfela: about 6,840 students, 712 staff (including 328 adjunct lecturers), six first-degree programmes and an MBA, a regular track, an evening track and a Haredi track. Tuition pays for almost everything.

## The stated ask

From the board minutes of 20 January 2027 (`data-room/company/board-minutes-2027-01-20-extract.md`) and the CEO's call:

> "First-year dropout jumped from 12% to almost 20%. Every point costs us two million shekels a year. I want an AI early-warning system that tells our advisers who is going to leave, so they can save them. Persevia does exactly this and offers a free pilot from March. Target: 12% by 2029."

The board asked for **one recommendation** on **Tuesday 4 May 2027**, with costs, risks and dates.
