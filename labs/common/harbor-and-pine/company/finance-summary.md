## How we judge a project

- **Template.** Every project above ₪50,000 comes in the finance team's business-case template:
  costs by year (one-off and running), benefits by year, three scenarios (low / expected / high),
  net present value and payback. Benefits must say whose budget they land in.
- **Hurdle rate.** 12% a year (our cost of capital plus a margin for risk). A project with a negative
  NPV at 12% in the expected scenario does not go forward.
- **Payback.** We prefer payback within 24 months.
- **Approval.** Up to ₪50,000: the department head and the CFO. ₪50,000–150,000: the CEO. **Above
  ₪150,000 (total over three years): the board.**
- **Headcount savings** count only when there is a named plan for the roles (attrition, redeployment
  or a decision by the CEO), with timing. "Capacity freed" is not cash until then.
- **Staged money.** We release budget in stages against conditions written in advance.

## Things to know

- The write-off of capitalised software in FY2023 (₪860,000) is in the table for completeness.
- Seasonal cash: Q4 is the strongest quarter for cash in, Q1 the weakest after the winter sale. Large
  payments in February–March are hard.
- Currency: suppliers are paid in US$ and €; we hedge three months ahead. For planning use
  ₪3.70 per US$ (finance planning rate, October 2026). Deliberately conservative: it covers currency
  risk on dollar-priced imports and is above the market rate.
