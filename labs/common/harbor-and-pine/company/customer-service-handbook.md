# Customer Service Handbook

*Harbor & Pine Ltd. · Version 3.2 · Owner: Samir Haddad, Customer Service Manager · Approved by Michal
Peretz, COO · March 2024. Addendum A added July 2026.*

## 1. Who we are

The Customer Service team answers every customer of Harbor & Pine, before and after they buy, in
Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English. We own the customer's question until it is answered.

## 3. Service levels

- **First response:** within **2 business hours** for email.
- **Phone:** answer 85% of calls; average speed of answer under 2 minutes.
- **Damage claims:** decision within 5 business days of receiving photos.
- **Refunds:** paid within 14 business days of the item reaching the warehouse.

## 4. Working a ticket in Deskly

1. Take the oldest ticket in your language view. Assign it to yourself.
2. Find the order in Priority (search by order number, phone or email).
3. Set the **category** (see section 5). If nothing fits, use "Other/כללי".
4. Use a saved reply (macro) where one exists and personalise the first line.
5. Send the reply and **set the status to Resolved**. If the customer writes again, Deskly opens a new
   ticket (setting changed in 2025 so that resolved tickets stay closed in the reports).
6. Use the timer if you have it switched on.

Do not leave tickets in "Pending" over the weekend: resolve them and the customer can write again.

Macros exist in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English. Do not change the macro wording without Samir's
approval.

## 7. Languages

Answer in the customer's language. If you do not speak it, assign the ticket to a colleague who
does (Russian: Olga, Yana, Sergei; Arabic: Lina, Rami, Ahmad; English: Daniel). If nobody is free,
answer in Hebrew.

## 8. Damage claims

1. The agent sends the "Damage – photos request" macro.
2. When photos arrive, the claims coordinator (Rina) opens a claim number DC-YY-NNN in the claims log.
3. **Every claim is reviewed by QA within 48 hours.** QA checks the photos against the delivery note
   and the supplier's packing standard and approves or rejects the claim.
4. The claims coordinator informs the customer of the decision and arranges replacement, repair or
   credit.
5. Approved claims caused by the supplier are charged back to the supplier by Purchasing.

## 9. Escalation

- Abusive customers, threats to go to the media or to court, credit-card disputes: to Samir.
- B2B customers: copy the account manager.
- Anything about delivery dates: check Priority. Logistics should not be contacted directly by agents
  except through the "logistics@" mailbox.

## 10. Customer data

Customer details stay in Deskly and Priority. Do not send customer details to private email or
WhatsApp. Do not print order lists.

## Addendum A — Category change (July 2026)

From **1 July 2026** the category "Delivery" is replaced by two categories:

- **Delivery status** — questions about when an order will arrive.
- **Delivery damage** — items that arrived damaged.

Old tickets keep the category "Delivery". Reports that compare months before and after July must take
this into account.
