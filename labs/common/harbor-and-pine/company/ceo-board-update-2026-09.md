# CEO update to the board — September 2026

*Excerpt from the minutes of the board meeting of 24 September 2026, item 4, and the CEO's slide notes.
Circulated to the management team on 27 September 2026.*

## Item 4 — "AI at Harbor & Pine" (presented by Yoav Ben-Ami)

The CEO reported on the retail technology conference he attended in Amsterdam (8–10 September). Key
points from his presentation:

- "Every serious retailer we met already runs an AI agent for customer service. Several told us their
  bot handles most customer questions without a human."
- "Our customer service costs us ₪3.4 million a year and our customers still complain they can't get
  through. This is exactly what AI is for."
- "A vendor we met, ChatNest, showed a demo built on our own catalogue in two days."

**The CEO committed to the board that, within 90 days:**

1. Harbor & Pine will have an **AI customer-service bot** live on the website;
2. management will present an **AI strategy** for the company;
3. the program will **cut customer support cost by 40%**.

Discussion. One independent director (Orna Sela) asked whether 40% was "a target or a forecast" and
what the 40% was based on. The CEO answered that it was "conservative compared to what the vendors
showed us" and that the new Head of AI and Operations Improvement, starting in November, would
"bring the detailed plan". The minority investor's representative asked that the plan come with a
business case in the company's standard format.

**Resolution:** Management to present the AI strategy, the bot plan and the business case at the next
board meeting on **28 January 2027**. Any spend above ₪150,000 to come to the board for approval as
usual.
