# Harbor & Pine Ltd. — company profile

*From the welcome pack HR gives new managers. Last updated August 2026.*

**Harbor & Pine Ltd.** (הארבור אנד פיין בע"מ) imports and sells furniture and home goods. Founded in
2009 by Shlomo Ben-Ami as a single showroom in Jaffa; run since 2021 by his son, Yoav Ben-Ami (CEO).
Privately held; the board has five members (two family, two independent, one from the minority
investor that came in in 2022).

| Employees | about 120 (see `org-chart.md`) |

| Suppliers | 10 main factories in Vietnam, China, Turkey and Italy |

| Customers | B2C: web store and two showrooms; B2B: hotels, offices, clinics |

## How the business works

1. **Buying.** Purchasing (Eyal Navon's team) orders from factories months ahead. Most products are
   made to order or shipped in regular containers: Vietnam and China weekly, Turkey and Italy every
   two weeks. A freight forwarder (Gal-Ship Forwarding) books the containers and sends updates by email
   and through its portal.
2. **Selling.** Customers buy on the web store (StoreHub) or in the showrooms in Tel Aviv and Haifa.
   When they order, the web store shows and emails a promised delivery date. B2B customers buy through
   account managers (Yaniv Carmi's team) and are invoiced on terms.
3. **Receiving.** Containers land, clear customs, and reach the warehouse in Rishon LeZion. Logistics
   (Tamar Shalev's team) allocates stock to orders and books deliveries with customers.
4. **Delivering.** An outside delivery company brings the goods to the customer; our own technicians
   assemble where the customer paid for assembly.
5. **Service.** Customer Service (Samir Haddad's team, 14 people) answers email, the web contact form
   and the phone in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English, handles damage claims and returns.

## Seasons

- **Peak:** November to January (Black Friday, winter sale, the end-of-year B2B budget rush), and the
  four weeks before Rosh Hashana and Pesach.
- **Quiet:** May–June and the summer weeks after the holidays.

## Sites

- Head office and warehouse: Rishon LeZion (about 85 people).
- Showroom Tel Aviv (14 people) and showroom Haifa (9 people).
