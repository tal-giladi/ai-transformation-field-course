## Highlights

### NEW: AI Candidate Match ✨

Hiring just got faster. AI Candidate Match reads every application and CV you receive and ranks
candidates against the requirements of the job, so your recruiters see the best fits first.

- A **Match score (0–100)** appears next to every applicant in the Recruiting pipeline.
- Candidates are sorted by Match score by default.
- "Smart filter" hides applicants with a Match score under 40 (you can show them with one click).
- Works in Hebrew, Arabic, English and Russian CVs.

**Availability:** included at no extra cost in the Business and Enterprise plans. **Enabled by default**
for all existing jobs and new jobs. Admins can switch it off under *Settings → Recruiting → AI
features*.

**How it works:** CV text and the job description are sent to our AI service, which compares skills,
experience and keywords. The model was trained on anonymised hiring data from PeopleWise customers.
Match scores are suggestions; hiring decisions remain with you.
