# Information Technology Use Policy

*Harbor & Pine Ltd. · Approved by management 14 June 2021 · Owner: IT (Avi Mizrahi) · Next review:
June 2023*

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how employees may use the company's computers, network, email and software, so
that company and customer information stays safe and our systems keep working.

## 2. Who it applies to

All employees, temporary workers and contractors who use company equipment or accounts.

## 3. Company equipment

- Company laptops and PCs are for work. Reasonable personal use (e.g. checking personal email at
  lunch) is allowed.
- Do not install software on company computers without IT approval. IT installs approved software
  through the management service provider.
- Lock your screen when you leave your desk.

## 4. Accounts and passwords

- Use your own account. Never share your password, including with IT.
- Passwords must be at least 10 characters. Two-step verification is mandatory for email.

## 5. Email and internet

- Company email is for company business. Do not forward company emails to private addresses.
- Internet use is logged and filtered by the company's web proxy for security reasons.
- Do not download files from unknown sources or open unexpected attachments.

## 6. Company and customer information

- Customer information (names, phone numbers, addresses, orders, payments) may only be used for
  serving the customer and only in company systems (Priority, Deskly, StoreHub, Google Workspace).
- Files must be saved on the company shared drive, not on personal cloud storage.
- Do not take customer lists out of the company.

## 7. Cloud services

Only cloud services approved by IT and management may be used for company work. Requests go to IT by
email.

## 8. Mobile phones

Company email on a personal phone is allowed with the Google device policy installed.

## 9. Breaches

Report a lost device or a suspected leak to IT immediately. Breaches of this policy may lead to
disciplinary action.

## 10. Review

IT reviews this policy every two years.
