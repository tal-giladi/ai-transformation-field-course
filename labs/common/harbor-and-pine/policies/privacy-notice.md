# Privacy Notice — Harbor & Pine

*Published on harborpine.co.il/privacy. Last updated: 1 January 2022. Fictional company document; not
legal advice and not a template.*

Harbor & Pine Ltd. ("we") respects your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we
collect when you buy from us or contact us, and what we do with it.

## 1. What we collect

- **Details you give us:** name, phone number, email, delivery address, floor and access details,
  company name and number for business customers.
- **Order details:** products, prices, delivery and assembly appointments, returns and claims.
- **Payment details:** processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
- **Messages:** emails, web-form messages, phone calls (calls may be recorded for quality and
  training), and photos you send us (for example of a damaged item).
- **Website data:** cookies and similar technologies, as described in our cookie notice.

## 2. Why we use it

- To process and deliver your order, arrange assembly and handle returns and claims.
- To answer your questions and complaints.
- To send you invoices and service messages.
- With your consent, to send you marketing by email or SMS. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- To meet our legal obligations (tax, consumer protection).

## 3. Who we share it with

- Delivery and assembly subcontractors, so they can reach you.
- Our payment provider.
- Service providers that host or operate our systems on our behalf (for example our web store,
  helpdesk and email providers). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
- Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Where it is kept

Your information is kept in our systems in Israel and with our service providers, some of which
store information on servers outside Israel.

## 5. How long

We keep order and invoice information for seven years, as tax law requires. Other information is kept
for as long as we need it for the purposes above.

## 6. Your rights

Under the Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, you may ask to see the information we hold about you and
ask us to correct or delete it where it is inaccurate. Write to privacy@harborpine.co.il.

## 7. Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows the latest version.
