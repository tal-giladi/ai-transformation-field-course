# Where our data lives and how long we keep it

*Internal note by Avi Mizrahi (IT) for the COO, 22 October 2026. "Best of my knowledge — some of this
nobody has ever decided."*

| Data | Where | Kept for | Who decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders, invoices, customers | Priority ERP (cloud, Israel) | Forever (7 years minimum for tax) | Finance |
| Helpdesk tickets and emails | Deskly (cloud, US/EU) | Forever — no deletion rule set | Nobody |
| Web-store customer accounts | StoreHub (cloud, EU) | Until the customer deletes the account | Default setting |
| Call recordings | CloudVoice | 30 days, then deleted automatically | Samir, 2023 |
| Call log (no audio) | CloudVoice | 2 years | Default setting |
| Damage claims log | Google Sheet on the shared drive (Rina's) | Forever; also downloaded as CSV by Finance each quarter | Nobody |
| Container tracking sheet | Excel on the shared drive (Tamar's) | Forever | Nobody |
| Product catalogue copies | Shared drive, several old copies (2021, 2023) | Forever | Nobody |
| Web proxy logs | NetGuard (cloud) | 90 days | MSP default |
| Device and extension inventory | MSP's device management | Current state only (monthly export) | MSP |
| HR and candidate data | PeopleWise (cloud) | Employees: 7 years after leaving; candidates: not set | HR |
| Expense claims | Priority + scanned receipts in Google Drive | 7 years | Finance |
