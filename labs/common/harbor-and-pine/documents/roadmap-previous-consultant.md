# Harbor & Pine — AI Transformation Roadmap 2027

*Prepared for Yoav Ben-Ami by NextWave Digital Advisory (fictional), 15 October 2026. "Proposal —
confidential". Paid engagement: two workshops with the CEO and the marketing director. Left on the
shared drive under Management → AI.*

## Vision

By the end of 2027 Harbor & Pine will be an **AI-first retailer**: every customer question answered
instantly by AI, every department using AI daily, and customer service cost down by 40%.

## Guiding assumptions

1. Leading AI customer-service platforms resolve **70% of conversations without a human** (ChatNest
   benchmark, 2026).
2. Customer service handles about 9,000 contacts a year at about ₪380 per contact (₪3.4M ÷ 9,000).
3. Deflecting 70% of contacts removes 70% of the variable workload; allowing for a 3-person core team,
   we model a **40% cost reduction** (₪1.36M a year) from month 4.
4. Staff will adopt the tools once they see the benefit.
