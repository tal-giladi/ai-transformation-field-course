# Transcript — Olga Petrenko (Senior Support Agent)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 02.2. Wednesday 12 November 2026, 10:30, at Olga's desk and then the
small meeting room, 40 minutes. The interviewer is the new Head of AI and Operations Improvement. Read
it as if you were sitting in: note what the interviewer found out, what they missed, and which
questions worked.*

---

**Interviewer:** Hi Olga. Samir said I could sit with you for a bit. I'm trying to understand how the
work really gets done here — the shortcuts, the annoying parts. I'm not checking anyone's performance.

**Olga:** So you're not the robot man? *(laughs)* Sit. I'm in the Russian queue.

**Interviewer:** Can you just work as normal and talk me through what you do?

**Olga:** OK. This one: "Где мой заказ" — where is my order. Promised the 3rd, today the 12th. You see
the macro button? If I press it, the customer gets "your estimated delivery date is the 3rd". On the 12th.
So I don't press it.

**Interviewer:** What do you do instead?

**Olga:** I look at the order in Priority. If there's a container number, I look in Tamar's sheet —
if it's updated. If not, I email logistics@ and wait. Then I write to the customer myself.

**Interviewer:** How long does that take?

**Olga:** Finding the date — five minutes if the sheet is good, two hours if I wait for logistics. Writing,
that's quick for me.

**Interviewer:** How many of your tickets are like this one?

**Olga:** Today? Look. *(scrolls)* Seven out of the first ten.

**Interviewer:** And after you answer — does the customer come back?

**Olga:** If the date I gave is right, no. If somebody sent the macro, yes, a new ticket, and the old one
says "resolved". Resolved! Nothing is resolved.

**Interviewer:** Who checks damage claims?

**Olga:** Rina decides with Samir. The handbook says QA. There's no QA. There was a QA girl until 2023.

**Interviewer:** Good to know. Let's go to the meeting room for ten minutes?

**Olga:** OK.

**Interviewer:** *(in the meeting room)* I want to be clear about something. What I write up is about the
work, not about people. Nobody gets named and nobody gets punished for how they've been coping. I'm
trying to design something that helps the team.

**Olga:** People say that.

**Interviewer:** Fair. Then judge me later. You said writing is quick for you. A careful Russian reply
about a delay — how do you write it so fast?

**Olga:** *(long pause)* You really won't put my name?

**Interviewer:** I really won't.

**Olga:** ChatGPT. The free one, on my Google. I paste the customer's email and write "answer politely in
Russian, the new date is the 18th, apologise". It writes, I fix it. Fifteen, twenty minutes before. Now
five. Arabic too, for Lina, when she's drowning.

**Interviewer:** Thank you for telling me. What exactly goes into it?

**Olga:** The email. With everything. Name, phone, sometimes the address, the order. I know, I know. I
once forgot to delete "Certainly! Here is a polite reply" and the customer got it. *(laughs)* He wasn't
angry. He was surprised we were polite.

**Interviewer:** Why ChatGPT and not, say, the translation tools?

**Olga:** Translation gives you translation. I need a polite reply that sounds like a person. ChatGPT
does that.

**Interviewer:** Who else on the team does this?

**Olga:** Ask the team. Not me.

**Interviewer:** I just need to know how many, roughly. It stays between us.

**Olga:** No. I said what I do. I don't give names. If you want to know, ask everybody together and
promise nobody gets punished.

**Interviewer:** That's fair. I'll do it that way. If there were an approved tool that did what ChatGPT
does, without the risk, would the team use it?

**Olga:** Tomorrow. But not a bot that writes to customers alone. A tool for us. And not "from above" —
let us help choose.

**Interviewer:** Would you be willing to help design and test it?

**Olga:** If it's real, yes. If it's a show for the board, no.

**Interviewer:** It's real. What's the one thing you'd fix that has nothing to do with AI?

**Olga:** The date on the website. Fix that and I'll have time to drink coffee.

**Interviewer:** Thank you, Olga. That was very useful.

**Olga:** Don't make me regret it.

**Interviewer:** [end interview]

**Olga:** — end of interview —
