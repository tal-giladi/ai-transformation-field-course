# Transcript — Yoav Ben-Ami (CEO)

*Pre-run conversation for lessons 03.9 and 07.3. Tuesday 2 December 2026, 17:30, Yoav's office, just the
two of them, 25 minutes. The interviewer is the Head of AI and Operations Improvement, one month in,
bringing the discovery results before the alignment session. Read it as if you were sitting in: note
what the interviewer found out, what they missed, and which moves worked.*

---

**Yoav:** I have twenty minutes. Tell me when the bot goes live.

**Interviewer:** I'll give you the answer first. A chatbot is not the fastest way to cut support load
here. The biggest driver is something we can fix in weeks, without AI, and I'd like your decision on
which path to take to the board.

**Yoav:** That's not what I asked. But go on.

**Interviewer:** About 38% of all support tickets since May are "where is my order". Since July it's
closer to 45%. They happen because the website promises a delivery date from a fixed table, and since
June almost every container from Vietnam and China has been one to three weeks late. The median order
from those countries now arrives 11 days after the date we promised. Nobody tells the customer, so the
customer writes to us.

**Yoav:** So a bot answers them. That's exactly what bots are for.

**Interviewer:** The bot would answer with the same wrong date. ChatNest's own deck says that without an
integration to our order data it answers from the order confirmation. The agents' saved reply does the
same thing today, and those customers write again: about 23% of resolved tickets with an order number
come back within two weeks.

**Yoav:** *(pause)* And the integration?

**Interviewer:** Even with it, the bot only knows what's in Priority. The real dates are in Tamar's sheet
and the forwarder's emails. The fix is upstream: compare every open order with the latest container
ETA every day, and tell the customer before they ask. That's plain automation. No AI.

**Yoav:** I told the board AI.

**Interviewer:** I know. Can I ask what exactly you told them, and where the 40% came from? Just between
us.

**Yoav:** *(long pause)* The ChatNest slide said minus forty-five. I said forty, to be conservative. Orna
— one of the independent directors — asked if it was a target or a forecast. I said conservative. *(looks
away)* It wasn't based on our data. There was no data.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. That helps me help you. Here are three options for January. One: sign the bot
now, go live in February, and promise 40%. Two: fix the date problem first — live before the board — and
pilot AI where it does help: drafting replies for the agents in Hebrew, Arabic and Russian, with a human
sending every one. Three: do nothing new until the board and present a strategy only.

**Yoav:** Three is not an option. I'm not going to the board with a document.

**Interviewer:** Agreed. Between one and two?

**Yoav:** What do I show the board in option two?

**Interviewer:** That "where is my order" tickets are already falling, with numbers from January. That a
pilot of AI drafting is running with the team, in four languages. And a business case in Ronit's
template for what comes next, with gates.

**Yoav:** And the 40%?

**Interviewer:** I don't know the number yet. I'd rather give the board a number that holds. I'll have a
range by mid-January, signed by Ronit.

**Yoav:** The board will ask why I changed my mind.

**Interviewer:** You didn't change the goal. You found out what's actually driving the cost and went
after that first. That's a story a board likes.

**Yoav:** *(smiles slightly)* "We found the real problem in 30 days." I can say that.

**Interviewer:** You can, and it's true.

**Yoav:** OK. Option two. But I want the AI pilot visible. Not a lab experiment.

**Interviewer:** It will be with the support team, on real tickets. I'll bring the plan to the alignment
session on the 10th.

**Yoav:** Fine. Anything else?

**Interviewer:** No — thank you for being open about the 40%.

**Yoav:** Don't put that in a slide.

**Interviewer:** I won't.

**Interviewer:** [end interview]
