**Interviewer:** How many of the six who left mentioned notes?

**Shlomit:** So the evenings are in Nof HaGalil and Tiberias, not in Safed.

**Interviewer:** Mostly in the mixed clinics, yes. That's why the demos matter: ScribeWave's demo was in
English.

**Shlomit:** They support thirty languages.

**Interviewer:** Under the standard plan's data agreement, ScribeWave may use de-identified recordings and

**Shlomit:** Nobody told me that. Yoni certainly didn't. Has Hagit seen it?

**Interviewer:** Not yet. I'd like her to see every vendor's terms before anyone signs.

**Shlomit:** And the discount? Thirty-first of December.

**Interviewer:** I'd ask MedLink in writing whether it can move. If it can't, I'd still not sign before the
proof of concept and Hagit's review. I can give you a sentence for the board.

**Shlomit:** Go on.

**Interviewer:** "We are choosing the scribe on evidence from our own clinics, in Hebrew, Arabic and
Russian, and we will start with a pilot in two clinics in the first quarter."

**Shlomit:** The board will ask why we're behind.

**Interviewer:** You'd be behind by eight weeks and ahead on the thing the board member asked about,
Arabic and Russian.

**Shlomit:** ... I'll think about it. Bring me the proof of concept by the thirty-first and we'll see.
