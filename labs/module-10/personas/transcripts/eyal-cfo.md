**Interviewer:** I'd like to understand how you judge a case like this before I build one, so I don't

**Interviewer:** Where is the money today, then?

**Eyal:** Locums. Two point one million last year, one point seven one already this year to September.

**Interviewer:** So if a scribe kept one doctor from leaving, or filled one post without a locum...

**Eyal:** About three hundred thousand a year per post. That's a real line. But I need evidence it keeps
people, not a survey that says they like it.

**Interviewer:** What would you accept as evidence?

**Eyal:** A pilot with a comparison. After-hours work measured from the system, not asked. And whether the
doctors in the pilot keep using it after the novelty.

**Interviewer:** The ScribeWave quote: one point four six million in year one with the discount. Does it
fit?

**Eyal:** It fits the board line. Just. Year two is list price plus increases.

**Interviewer:** And the Enterprise plan, which the privacy terms may require, is eighteen percent more.

**Eyal:** Then it doesn't fit, for a hundred and twenty seats. For seventy-eight doctors it might.

**Interviewer:** How do you want options compared?

**Eyal:** Three years. Licences, integration, the Notara side, training time. Capacity on a separate line.
Payback under two years if you claim money.

**Interviewer:** And approvals?

**Eyal:** Above three hundred thousand over the project's life, the board. Three quotes above a hundred
thousand. We have four vendors, so that's covered.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. I'll show you the case before the readout.
