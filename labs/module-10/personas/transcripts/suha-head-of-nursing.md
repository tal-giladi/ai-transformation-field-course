# Transcript — Suha Nasser (Head of Nursing)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 10.2. Thursday 19 November 2026, 10:00, Majd al-Krum clinic, 40 minutes.
The interviewer had the survey results and the clinician list. Read it as if you were sitting in: note
what the interviewer found out, what they missed, and which questions worked.*

---

**Suha:** So now someone asks the nurses.

**Interviewer:** I should have come to you first. The quote is for a hundred and twenty seats, which
includes your forty-two nurses. Did anyone ask what nurses would use it for?

**Suha:** No. "Every doctor" was the slogan. The seats were the vendor's idea.

**Interviewer:** What does documentation look like for a nurse?

**Suha:** Shorter notes, many more of them. Diabetes and blood-pressure clinics, triage, vaccines. The
notes aren't the worst part of the day.

**Interviewer:** What is?

**Suha:** Being pulled out of my own clinic to interpret.

**Interviewer:** Tell me how that happens.

**Suha:** In Nof HaGalil, Tiberias, Nahariya, the women's health and the children's centres, many
doctors don't speak Arabic or Russian. If there's no relative, they call a nurse. Several times a day each
nurse. Her own patients wait. In the survey my nurses wrote about forty minutes a day.

**Interviewer:** Is it recorded anywhere?

**Suha:** Nowhere. Sometimes the doctor ticks "interpreter: nurse" in Notara. Mostly not.

**Interviewer:** If a scribe worked in Arabic and Russian, would that change?

**Suha:** For the doctors who don't speak the language, a scribe doesn't translate the conversation in the
room. But if the doctor writes in Hebrew from an Arabic conversation, a good scribe helps the Arabic-
speaking doctors. And the visits with relatives get shorter. That frees us indirectly.

**Interviewer:** Where would a scribe help nurses most directly?

**Suha:** Honestly, I'm not sure it's on our own visits. Measure it before you put it on us.

**Interviewer:** That's a fair test. Would nurses be willing to be in a pilot?

**Suha:** Some. They'd want to know who listens to the recordings.

**Interviewer:** I'll make sure the pilot plan answers that. Thank you.
