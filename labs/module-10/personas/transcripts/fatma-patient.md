# Transcript — Fatma Abu-Ras (patient, 64) with her daughter Hiba

*Pre-run interview for lesson 10.2. Tuesday 24 November 2026, 11:00, Majd al-Krum clinic, small meeting
room, 30 minutes. The CMO arranged it with Fatma's consent; Hiba translated from Arabic. The interviewer
had no access to Fatma's record. Read it as if you were sitting in: note what the interviewer found out,
what they missed, and which questions worked.*

---

**Interviewer:** Thank you both for coming. I'm not a doctor and I won't ask about your health. I'm trying
to understand what a visit to the doctor is like for patients, so that any new tool works for you. Mrs.
Abu-Ras, could you tell me about a visit, from when you arrive to when you leave?

**Fatma** *(through Hiba)*: I come with Hiba. We wait. The doctor is good, she speaks Arabic. She asks how
I am, she looks at the sugar numbers, she types.

**Interviewer:** While you talk?

**Fatma:** Yes. Sometimes she doesn't look up. She's busy, poor thing.

**Interviewer:** Was there a visit that went differently?

**Fatma:** In the summer my doctor was on holiday. There was another doctor, young, Hebrew only. Hiba came
to translate.

**Interviewer:** Please go on, in order, as it happened.

**Fatma:** I told Hiba to tell him I stopped the small pill for the pressure, because I was dizzy. She told
him. He nodded, he typed. Then the pharmacy kept giving me the small pill every month. In September my
doctor came back and asked why I'm not taking it. Nobody wrote it.

**Hiba:** He said "okay, okay" and wrote something. I don't know what.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. That's very helpful, and I'm sorry it happened. The clinic may start using a
tool that records the conversation to help the doctor write the note. How would you feel about that?

**Fatma:** Recording me?

**Interviewer:** The conversation with the doctor, so the note is written correctly.

**Fatma:** If the doctor explains to me in Arabic who hears it, and that it's only for writing, and they
delete it after... maybe yes.

**Hiba:** And she must be able to say no without the doctor being angry.

**Interviewer:** That's important. Who would you want to explain it?

**Fatma:** The doctor. Not a paper.

**Interviewer:** Would it help if the note were more correct, like the pill?

**Fatma:** Of course. That's why I come with Hiba.

**Interviewer:** Thank you both. I'll make sure what you said reaches the people writing the explanation.

*[end of transcript]*
