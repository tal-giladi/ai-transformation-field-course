# Engagement brief: Galil Health

All people, organizations and vendors are fictional.

## How you were brought in

On Monday 9 November 2026 Igor Shapiro, CIO of Galil Health, called you. A former colleague at Northwind
Outfitters had told him you "test the vendor's numbers before anyone signs". You met him on Wednesday 11
November at Galil's management offices in Karmiel. His words, from your notes:

> "Shlomit, our CEO, promised the board an ambient scribe for every doctor by Q2. We saw four demos in
> two weeks and she loved the American one. They're offering a discount if we sign by the end of
> December. I'll have to integrate whatever we buy with Notara, in five regions, two of them on an old
> version, and the medical director will hold me responsible if a note is wrong. I need someone from
> outside to run the selection properly and tell us which one, with evidence, before anyone signs."

You are an **independent consultant**, not an employee. You sent a proposal on Wednesday evening; Igor
signed it on Thursday 12 November.

## The stated ask

"An ambient scribe for every doctor by Q2 2027" (Shlomit Avraham, CEO, board meeting of 21 October 2026).
Igor's version in the signed proposal: "A recommendation, backed by evidence from Galil's own conditions,
on which ambient-scribe vendor Galil should contract with, on what terms, and how it should be piloted
with clinicians before any network-wide rollout."

## Who you report to

Igor Shapiro, CIO (the sponsor). A steering group meets three times (3 December, 31 December, 19
January): Shlomit Avraham (CEO, chair), Igor Shapiro, Dr. Wasim Haddad (Chief Medical Officer), Eyal
Dahan (CFO), Adv. Hagit Peretz (legal counsel and Privacy Protection Officer), Suha Nasser (Head of
Nursing).

## What you have on day one

- A signed mutual NDA and a data-processing agreement covering de-identified extracts (Hagit drafted
  it; data stays on a Galil laptop, which IT issues on Sunday). No access to patient records or audio.
- The data room (`data-room/`, start with `INDEX.md`): an 8% sample of doctor visits with Notara's
  documentation fields, doctor-day visit counts, the clinician list, the CMO's October survey, the KPI
  report, the board minute, the finance summary, the 2025 dictation trial's usage export, the incident
  log, the firewall's AI summary, the cloud committee's minutes, extracts of the health-fund agreements
  and other contracts, the patient privacy notice, and everything the four vendors left behind
  (decks, quotes, data-processing agreements, the demo-day scorecards, Igor's draft RFP).
- Meetings Igor has booked for week 1: Dr. Wasim Haddad (Monday), Adv. Hagit Peretz (Tuesday), half a
  day in the Karmiel clinic with Dr. Elena Volkova (Wednesday), Suha Nasser (Thursday).
- Not yet: time with Shlomit (her assistant offered 20 minutes "after the first readout"), Notara access,
  vendor contacts (all requests go through Igor), patients.

## Constraints you know about

- ScribeWave's quote (120 seats, 12% discount) is valid until Thursday 31 December 2026.
- Galil is paid per registered patient by two health funds; their service agreements govern what Galil may
  do with members' information.
- The board meets in late January 2027 and expects the CEO's update on the vendor.
- Hanukkah runs from the evening of 4 December to 12 December 2026; many clinicians take leave in the last
  week of December.
- Galil has a staff committee and a collective agreement for nursing and other non-medical staff; doctors
  are on personal contracts.
- This is a clinical setting: anything that touches patient safety is the CMO's to decide, and anything
  that touches patients' information is the DPO's and counsel's.
