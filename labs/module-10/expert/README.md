# Expert deliverables — Galil Health

What an experienced consultant produced in this engagement, one file per stage. Use them in lesson 10.6
to compare with your own work, after you have written yours: reading them first turns the engagement
into copying. They are one good answer, not the only one; the rubric in the module project says what any
strong answer needs. All people, organizations and vendors are fictional.

| File | Stage | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| [01-engagement-charter.md](01-engagement-charter.md) | Charter, entry role, decision rights, SOW with exits, first-read sheet, access request, first week | 10.1 |
| [02-findings-memo.md](02-findings-memo.md) | Findings memo with evidence labels | 10.2 |
| [03-baselines-and-case.md](03-baselines-and-case.md) | Baseline sheet, what the scribe must do, options, the one-page case for 3 December | 10.3 |
| [04-rfp-claims-and-poc.md](04-rfp-claims-and-poc.md) | The RFP as sent, claim read-backs, the proof-of-concept protocol and results, the 31 December note | 10.4 |
| [05-vendor-decision-and-pilot-plan.md](05-vendor-decision-and-pilot-plan.md) | Vendor scoring, contract terms, steps before signing, the clinician pilot plan | 10.5 |
| [06-steering-readout.md](06-steering-readout.md) | Steering pack front page, recommend-against, integration check, rehearsal, decision record, handover | 10.6 |

Numbers: everything computed from the data room and the proof-of-concept files is real and reproducible
with `discovery.py` and `poc_score.py`. Prices are the vendors' (fictional) quotes; costs over three years
use the stated assumptions. Nothing here is legal advice: every legal reading is attributed to Galil's
counsel and DPO.
