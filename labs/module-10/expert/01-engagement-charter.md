# 01 · Engagement charter — Galil Health

Version 1.0, Sunday 15 November 2026. Sponsor: Igor Shapiro, CIO. Consultant: independent.

## Objective (the sponsor's words, read back and agreed 15 November)

"A recommendation, backed by evidence from Galil's own conditions, on which ambient-scribe vendor Galil
should contract with, on what terms, and how it should be piloted with clinicians before any
network-wide rollout" — added at read-back: "that IT can actually integrate".

**The stated ask this engagement tests:** "An ambient scribe for every doctor by Q2 2027" (CEO, board, 21
October 2026), with ScribeWave preferred after its demo and a 12% discount offered until 31 December.

## What "done" means

1. A minuted decision of the steering group on Tuesday 19 January 2027 on vendor, terms and pilot.
2. Every piece of evidence labelled measured, reported or assumed.
3. A proof of concept on Galil's own synthetic visits in Hebrew, Arabic and Russian, with thresholds agreed
   before the run.
4. A named Galil owner (Igor) and clinical owner (Wasim) who accept the handover on 21 January.

demoed (others may answer the RFP); the PoC; contract terms; the pilot plan; how nurses are affected. Out,
