**Interviewer:** What do you answer?

**Nadia:** The macro says "within 14 business days of the item reaching the warehouse". MAC-060. But

**Interviewer:** Does that happen often?

**Nadia:** Every day. Arabic, Russian. Arabizi too, Arabic in English letters. The bot only does

**Interviewer:** I saw several returns macros in the export. Which do you use?

**Nadia:** *(laughs)* Pick one. MAC-031 says store credit, ten percent and thirty-nine shekels. MAC-047

**Interviewer:** Let me ask about a real case. A customer opened a tent, didn't use it, it's day ten,

**Nadia:** *(looks at the interviewer)* Officially, store credit minus ten percent and shipping. Really?

**Interviewer:** What does it depend on?

**Nadia:** If they push. *(pause)* You said no names?

**Nadia:** There's a doc. "Returns answers: what we really do." Fourteen rules. If they mention the

**Interviewer:** Who uses it?

**Nadia:** Most of the senior agents. The new ones don't know it exists.

**Interviewer:** What happens with defects today?

**Nadia:** The macro says send it to Modi'in. Most of us send a courier anyway; the doc says so. Some new
agents follow the macro and the customer pays postage for our broken tent.

**Interviewer:** And when a customer is refused?

**Nadia:** They come back. Phone, then WhatsApp, then email, until they hit someone who uses the doc.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. That explains a lot of what I see in the data. Can I see the doc?

**Nadia:** I'll show you on my screen. I won't send it.

**Interviewer:** That's fine. What would make your day easier?

**Nadia:** One policy I can say out loud without being ashamed. And refunds that arrive.

**Interviewer:** Have you had older customers with special situations? Pensioners, people who bought

**Nadia:** Once, a lady, sixty-eight. She bought in chat with Irina, then the bot told her store credit

**Interviewer:** If a tool drafted the reply for you, with the right policy and the amount already

**Nadia:** If it's right. If it writes Arabic like a person and not like Google. And if I can change it.
