# Transcript — Meseret Tadesse (Head of Customer Service)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 08.2. Monday 9 November 2026, 14:00, a small meeting room next to the
customer-service floor, 50 minutes. The interviewer is the outside consultant. Read it as if you were
sitting in: note what the interviewer found out, what they missed, and which questions worked.*

---

**Interviewer:** Thank you for the time, Meseret. I know the floor is busy. Before anything: I'm writing
a findings memo for Orly and the steering group. Nothing you tell me today will be attributed to you by
name unless you say it can be. If I need to quote you, I'll ask first.

**Meseret:** OK. Thank you.

**Interviewer:** Could you walk me through how a customer reaches your team today, channel by channel?

**Meseret:** Phone, email, the web form, WhatsApp, chat. WhatsApp has been inside Tikra since 2024. Chat
goes to Verbalo first since 15 June; if the customer asks for a person, it hands over to my chat desk.
Hours Sunday to Thursday eight to eight, Friday eight to one.

**Interviewer:** And the team?

**Meseret:** Thirty agents, six team leads. Three Hebrew desks, one Arabic, one Russian and English,
one WhatsApp and chat. In June I had forty-two.

**Interviewer:** What changed for the team when the twelve left?

**Meseret:** *(pause)* The queue doesn't empty. Written first response was about three hours in May; in
September it was eight. We had overtime in September.

**Interviewer:** The Q3 report says AHT went down thirty percent. How does that fit with a queue that
doesn't empty?

**Meseret:** The bot takes the easy chats. That's what the report says.

**Interviewer:** I compared the two reports this morning. The footnote for AHT in Q2 says it includes
after-contact work; the Q3 footnote doesn't mention it. Is it the same measure?

**Meseret:** *(long pause)* This stays between us?

**Interviewer:** Yes. Not attributed to you.

**Meseret:** It's not the same AHT. In June I asked Alexei to take after-contact work out: the return
forms, the notes. I said it's admin, not agent work. It started on the fourteenth, the day before the
bot. I knew it would look better at the launch. I should have said so in the report.

**Interviewer:** Thank you for telling me. If after-contact work were still counted, what do you think
handling time would show?

**Meseret:** Probably higher than before. The contacts we get now are harder. Returns, refunds, angry
people.

**Interviewer:** Tell me about returns.

**Meseret:** They're the longest and the worst. The website says one thing, the terms say another, the
club says thirty days. There are, I think, five or six returns macros. Every agent answers differently.
The customer calls back and gets a different answer.

**Interviewer:** Do you know how many customers come back about the same thing?

**Meseret:** Nobody measures it. The report says "not tracked". My team leads say they see the same
customers every day, especially about refunds.

**Interviewer:** When the Q3 report says a chat was "contained", what does that mean to you?

**Meseret:** That Verbalo handled it. *(pause)* Or that the customer didn't ask for an agent. It's
Verbalo's number. We don't check it.

**Interviewer:** And what does "resolved" mean in Tikra?

**Meseret:** The agent closes it when they've answered.

**Interviewer:** Which customers have the hardest time?

**Meseret:** Arabic and Russian speakers on chat; the bot answers them in Hebrew. And older customers
on the phone, they wait longest.

**Interviewer:** If you could change one thing before Black Friday, what would it be?

**Meseret:** One returns answer that everyone gives. And no more people leaving.

**Interviewer:** Can I sit on the floor with your WhatsApp desk tomorrow?

**Meseret:** Nadia's desk. Two hours. I'll tell her.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. I'll show you anything I write that touches your team before it goes to
the steering group.

*[end of transcript]*
