# Engagement charter and first-week plan — Northwind Outfitters

Expert deliverable for lesson 08.1. Written Monday 9 November 2026, signed by Orly Ben-David (COO) on
Tuesday 10 November. Template: [engagement charter](../../../templates/engagement-charter.md). All
people and companies are fictional.

## 1. Objective, in the sponsor's words

"A recommendation, backed by Northwind's own data, on how AI should be used in customer service in
2027, and a tested pilot if one is justified." (Orly Ben-David, signed proposal, 5 November 2026.)

Read back and agreed on 8 November with one addition: "something that survives the board and the fund
in late January".

**The stated ask behind it** (Gil Shachar, board, 21 September): "a chatbot that replaces half of
support by Q2 2027". This charter does not assume that ask is right or wrong; phase 1 tests it.

## 2. What "done" means

1. On Tuesday 12 January 2027 the steering group takes one minuted decision on AI in customer service
   for 2027 (scale, change, stop or start), with the evidence behind it labelled measured, directional
   or assumed.
2. On Thursday 14 January a named Northwind owner accepts the handover pack.
3. If a pilot is justified: it has been evaluated offline on Northwind's own tickets against thresholds
   agreed before the run.

## 4. Scope (summary; full scope boundary signed separately)

**In:** customer service (all channels), the Verbalo chatbot and its contract, the returns and refund
process as far as it drives contact, the 2027 customer-service plan's evidence. **Out:** warehouse
operations beyond returns, marketing automation, HR decisions about individuals, legal opinions (routed
to counsel), vendor negotiation on Northwind's behalf (I prepare, Northwind negotiates). Changes by
written change request signed by the COO.
