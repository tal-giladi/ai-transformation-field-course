# Engagement brief: Northwind Outfitters

All people and companies are fictional.

## How you were brought in

On Monday 2 November 2026 Orly Ben-David, COO of Northwind Outfitters, called you. A former colleague
had recommended you after your work at Harbor & Pine. You met her on Wednesday 4 November at the head
office in Petah Tikva. Her words, from your notes:

> "Gil, our CEO, wants a chatbot that replaces half of customer service by Q2. He told the board in
> September and the fund put it in the 2027 budget. The bot has been live on web chat since June and
> Gil says it works. I need someone from outside to tell us what to do next, with evidence, by the
> January board. I can't be the one who says it."

You are an **independent consultant**, not an employee. You sent a proposal the same evening; Orly
signed it on Thursday 5 November.

## The stated ask

"A chatbot that replaces half of support by Q2 2027" (Gil Shachar, CEO, board meeting of 21 September
2026). Orly's version in the signed proposal: "A recommendation, backed by Northwind's own data, on
how AI should be used in customer service in 2027, and a tested pilot if one is justified."

## Contract

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Duration | 10 weeks: Sunday 8 November 2026 – Thursday 14 January 2027 |
| Phase 1, Diagnose (weeks 1–3) | ₪72,000, fixed fee. Deliverables: engagement charter, findings memo, measured baselines and a shortlist. Readout to the COO and CEO on Thursday 26 November. **Exit point:** either side may stop here. |
| Phase 2, Decide and build (weeks 4–9) | ₪126,000, fixed fee. Deliverables: decision paper on the chatbot, a pilot evaluated offline on Northwind's tickets, a pilot charter. **Exit point** after the decision paper (Thursday 3 December). |
| Phase 3, Readout and handover (week 10) | ₪24,000, fixed fee. Steering committee on Tuesday 12 January 2027; handover pack to a named Northwind owner on Thursday 14 January. |
| Total | ₪222,000 + VAT, invoiced at the end of each phase, paid from the COO's operations budget |
| Pilot tooling budget | Up to ₪60,000 (LLM API, helpdesk sandbox, labelling hours), approved by the COO; anything above goes to the CEO (approval up to ₪250,000) |
| Change control | Any change in scope or dates by written change request signed by the COO |

## Who you report to

Orly Ben-David, COO (the sponsor). A steering group meets three times (26 November, 3 December, 12
January): Gil Shachar (CEO), Orly Ben-David, Boris Kaplan (CFO), Meseret Tadesse (Head of Customer
Service), Adv. Hila Rosen (legal counsel), Alexei Morozov (Head of IT and BI).

## What you have on day one

- A signed mutual NDA and a data-processing agreement covering pseudonymised extracts (Northwind's
  counsel drafted it; customer data stays on Northwind's laptop, which IT issues on Sunday).
- The data room (`data-room/`, start with `INDEX.md`): BI's standard 1% customer extract (orders,
  returns, helpdesk contacts, chatbot sessions), 2,000 written tickets from September–October, the KPI
  reports, the board minute, the finance summary, the returns policies, the Verbalo contract, demo and
  invoices, and the legal logs.
- Meetings Orly has already booked for week 1: Meseret Tadesse, Alexei Morozov, two hours on the floor
  with the WhatsApp desk (Nadia Khoury), Boris Kaplan on Thursday.
- Not yet: live helpdesk or Verbalo access, call recordings, payroll, legal memos, time with Gil (his
  assistant offered 30 minutes "after Black Friday").

## Constraints you know about

- Black Friday is Friday 27 November: the customer-service floor is at its busiest in weeks 3 and 4.
- The Verbalo contract renews automatically unless notice is given by 1 April 2027.
- The board meets in late January 2027 to approve the 2027 budget, which already assumes 21 agents
  from April 2027.
- Northwind has no works committee. Labour law still applies to any change in the team.
