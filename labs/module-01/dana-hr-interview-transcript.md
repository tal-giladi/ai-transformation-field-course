**Interviewer:** Thanks, Dana. I'm making a list of every system in the company that uses AI — what it
does, who owns it, what data it touches. HR systems are part of that. Can I ask about PeopleWise?

**Dana:** Of course. We use it for everything: employees, payroll, attendance, and recruiting.

**Interviewer:** How do candidates get screened in PeopleWise today? Walk me through the last hiring
round.

**Dana:** The autumn warehouse round — about fifteen seasonal workers. Candidates apply through the job
page, they land in the recruiting pipeline, Orly goes through them and calls the good ones. Why?

**Interviewer:** Does the pipeline show them in any particular order?

**Dana:** Now that you ask… Orly said in September that they arrive "sorted" now, and that some sit under
a "low match" heading. I assumed she'd set a filter. Is that something else?

**Interviewer:** These are the vendor's release notes from March. *(shows them)* They say an "AI
Candidate Match" feature was switched on by default for all jobs: a score from 0 to 100, candidates
sorted by it, and anyone under 40 hidden unless you click to show them.

**Dana:** *(reads)* On by default. Since March. *(pause)* So who decided which candidates we never saw?

**Interviewer:** As far as I can tell, nobody here did — it came with an update. Did anyone in HR or IT
get these notes?

**Dana:** They came to me by newsletter. I didn't read them. Avi wouldn't have seen them at all. Our
software list says PeopleWise has no AI.

**Interviewer:** Can it be switched off?

**Dana:** The notes say an admin can, under settings. I'm the admin. I'd like to switch it off today — but
I don't want to make it worse. Do we need to check the candidates from the autumn round first?

**Interviewer:** I'd suggest two things: export the list of everyone who applied this autumn with their
scores before you change anything, so nothing is lost, and ask the company's labour lawyer how to look
at who was hidden. I can't tell you what the law requires — that's their call.

**Dana:** I'll call him tomorrow. Discrimination in hiring is exactly what we must never get wrong here.
We hire Arabic, Russian, Amharic speakers — if a model scored them down because of how a CV is written…

**Interviewer:** That's the question to put to him. For my list: owner is you, data is candidates' CVs,
and it shapes who gets a call. Anything else in HR that might use AI — the attendance system, the
training system?

**Dana:** ClockIn just counts hours. LearnBox — nobody uses it. I don't think so, but I'm less sure than I
was an hour ago.

**Interviewer:** That's fair. Last thing: when we start piloting AI in Customer Service, what should I
know about the people side?

**Dana:** Not in December. Peak season — holidays are frozen. February or March. And tell them the truth
early, even if the truth is "we don't know yet". Two of Samir's agents already asked me whether they
should look for jobs.

**Interviewer:** Understood. I'll send you a short note today with what we found, and I'll put the
PeopleWise decision on Michal's agenda for Thursday.

**Dana:** Thank you. Really.
