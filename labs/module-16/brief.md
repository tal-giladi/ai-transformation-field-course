# Engagement brief: Open Hands

All people, families and organizations are fictional.

## How you were brought in

You signed up last year with *Mahut Pro Bono*, a (fictional) programme that matches experienced
professionals with Israeli nonprofits for short, unpaid projects. On Sunday 8 November 2026 Mahut sent you
a match: Open Hands, an amuta in Kiryat Ata that accompanies families raising children with disabilities.
On Tuesday 10 November you had a 40-minute video call with its executive director, Dr. Ronit Azoulay. Her
words, from your notes:

> "Our board chair came back from a conference saying every nonprofit uses AI and we are behind. Maybe he
> is right. My social workers spend their evenings on reports instead of with families, and Noa writes for
> fourteen funders in fourteen formats. We have no budget and no IT person — Yossi does computers on
> Tuesdays. I need someone to show us how to use the free tools, safely, and I have to tell the board
> something on the 9th of December. And please understand: our families trust us with everything."

You agreed on four weeks. Mahut sent its standard volunteer agreement the same evening; Ronit signed it on
Wednesday 11 November.

## The stated ask

"Help us use AI. We have no budget." (Board minutes, 27 October 2026, item 3.) Ronit's version on the call:
"Show us how to use the free tools for grants and paperwork, so my social workers spend time with families,
not with reports."
