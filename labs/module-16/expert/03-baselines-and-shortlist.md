## One-page case (hours)

**Ask:** let the funder report shrink, give people a safe tool for writing, and stop paying for what nobody
uses. **Cost:** ₪0 now; up to ₪602 a year from March (two nonprofit seats for development) only if the free
tools prove too limited, paid from the ₪4,497. **Freed (directional):** ≈ 300–350 coordinator hours a year
from the template (if agreed); grant drafting ≈ 20–30% of ≈ 790 writing hours (assumed until the December
test); case-note structuring unknown until tested. **What it does not do:** save money on salaries, or make
the reports "write themselves".

## The call with the funder (Wednesday 25 November, Ronit leads)

Prepared one page for Ronit: what the report is for (Tamar's own words, if she says them), the outcomes
framework with Q1–Q3 numbers, "up to six stories a quarter told with the family's approval of the text",
and a sentence on privacy ("we will no longer include family names, towns or diagnoses in reports").
Outcome: Tamar reads the first ten narratives and skims the rest; she agreed to the short format for Q4
and asked for the page in writing. She also said the foundation is drafting guidance asking grantees not
to put identifiable family information into AI without safeguards — which the boundary already does.
