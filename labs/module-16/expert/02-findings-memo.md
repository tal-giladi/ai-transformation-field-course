## One sentence

Open Hands is already using AI with family and donor information on personal accounts, its shared drive
is open far wider than its policy says, and most of the writing burden comes from one funder's template —
so the first step is closing access and giving people a safe alternative, and the biggest time saving is a
conversation with the funder, not a tool.

## What a parent told me (with her agreement, without her name)

She found her son's story on Facebook through a neighbour. Nobody had asked her. She is not against stories:
she would agree to one if she could read it first, in Arabic, and decide what stays out. She would like to
know who can see her son's file. She wants an apology, in Arabic, from someone senior.

## What I am not saying

That anyone broke the law: that is for counsel. That anyone should be blamed: people used what they had to
finish the work. That AI is the problem: the access problems predate it; AI made them travel further.
