## The charter (one page)

**Objective (Ronit's words, read back 15 Nov):** "By 10 December, Open Hands uses the tools it already has
for grant writing and paperwork, *safely first*, and the board has a clear plan."

**The stated ask this engagement tests:** "Help us use AI, we have no budget" (board, 27 Oct 2026).

**Done on 10 December means:**
1. Any family or donor information found where it should not be is closed, with Yossi, the same week.
2. Two workflows (grant drafting; case notes and letters) tried by the people who will use them, on Open
   Hands' own organization accounts, with their verdict written down.
3. One page of rules every staff member and volunteer can read in five minutes.
4. A board item on 9 December that asks for one decision.
5. Yossi and Ronit can keep it running in 15 minutes a month without me.

**Out of scope (and who owns it):** legal advice (Mahut's volunteer lawyer, booked by 25 Nov); buying any
tool (Ronit up to ₪3,000, Vova for anything recurring); live case files (Hana); talking to families or
funders without Ronit's introduction; volunteers' WhatsApp groups beyond advice (Amir).

**Urgent-finding rule:** anything that exposes family or donor information goes to Ronit **the same day, in
person or by phone**, not in a document. Yossi may fix it on the next Tuesday, or sooner if Ronit asks.

**Non-attribution:** what staff and volunteers tell me is written without names unless they agree.

**Hours (40):** week 1: 12 (read 3, interviews 6, staff meeting 1, notes 2); week 2: 10 (afternoon fix with
Yossi 4, diagnosis 3, funder call prep 1, counsel 2); week 3: 10 (build and test with Noa and Samah 7,
policy page 2, Vova 1); week 4: 8 (board pack 3, rehearsal 1, board 1, handover 3).
