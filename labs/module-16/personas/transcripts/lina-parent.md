# Transcript — Lina Mansour (mother of Adam, 9)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 16.2. Thursday 19 November 2026, 11:00, the Open Hands room in Shefa-'Amr,
40 minutes, in Hebrew (the interviewer does not speak Arabic). Samah introduced them and left. Read it as
if you were sitting in: note what the interviewer found out, what they missed, and which questions worked.*

---

**Interviewer:** Thank you for coming, Lina. I'm a volunteer helping Open Hands for a few weeks, not from
the welfare office or National Insurance. Nothing you say changes anything about your family's support.
Could you tell me about Adam?

**Lina:** Adam is nine. He doesn't speak, he walks with help. He laughs more than anyone you know.

**Interviewer:** How did you come to Open Hands?

**Lina:** 2021. The hospital told us. I had just left work. I was fighting everyone: National Insurance,
the school, the health fund. Samah came, and slowly things moved. The appeal, the aide at school. This
summer, the first weekend. We slept two nights. Two.

**Interviewer:** What has been hard with Open Hands, if anything?

**Lina:** *(pause)* In September my neighbour showed me Facebook. A story: "Adam, nine, from Shefa-'Amr",
his syndrome, his school. Everyone knew it was him. Nobody asked me.

**Interviewer:** I'm sorry. What happened after?

**Lina:** My husband was very angry. My daughter, the middle one, they teased her at school. "Your brother
is famous." I told Samah.

**Interviewer:** Did anyone from Open Hands' management talk to you about it?

**Lina:** No. I didn't go to them. I don't want trouble for Samah, or for us.

**Interviewer:** Do you remember what you signed when you started?

**Lina:** A form. In Hebrew. I don't remember.

**Interviewer:** How do letters from the committees reach you?

**Lina:** In Hebrew. Samah explains in Arabic, on WhatsApp, voice. The important ones I would like in
Arabic, written.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you'd like to know about how Open Hands keeps information about your
family?

**Lina:** Who can see Adam's file? Nobody ever told me.

**Interviewer:** That's a fair question, and I'll make sure it reaches Ronit. I can't promise what she'll
decide, but I can promise she'll hear it.

**Lina:** Good. Tell her I want her to say sorry. In Arabic.

**Interviewer:** I'll tell her exactly that.
