*Pre-run interview for lesson 16.1. Sunday 15 November 2026, 09:00, Ronit's office in Kiryat Ata, 30

**Interviewer:** Thank you for having me. I have about forty hours over four weeks, so I'd like to spend

tools for grants and paperwork, so the social workers spend time with families. Is that still it?

information is at risk. And the coordinators get some evenings back.

**Interviewer:** Can I write that down as the objective? "By 10 December, Open Hands staff use the tools it

**Interviewer:** Done. What I won't do: legal advice, buying anything, or touching live family files. If I

**Ronit:** Tell me the same day. Not in a report.

**Interviewer:** And if it needs Yossi to change something, can I ask him directly?

**Ronit:** On Tuesdays, yes. Tell me after.

**Interviewer:** The survey you sent — three people said they pasted family information "with details".
What did you make of it?

**Ronit:** It worried me. I didn't ask who. I didn't want it to become a hunt.

**Interviewer:** That's right, I think. I'd like to find out how people work, not who did what. Can I say at

**Ronit:** Yes. Please.

**Ronit:** Adler-Haddad. A narrative for every family, every quarter, in English. And Noa has fourteen

**Interviewer:** Is there anyone at the foundation I could talk to, later, with you?

**Ronit:** Tamar. She's good. Let's see first what you find.

**Interviewer:** And families — could I meet one? Only if they want to, through their coordinator.

**Ronit:** I'll ask Samah. There is a family that... maybe. I'll ask.

**Interviewer:** Last thing: who should own whatever we set up after I leave?

**Ronit:** Yossi, I suppose. There is nobody else. And me.

**Interviewer:** Then Yossi needs to be in it from week 1, not week 4. I'll book Tuesdays with him.
