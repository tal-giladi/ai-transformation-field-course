**Tsega:** I'm glad to listen. I won't be able to advise you on the design.

**Interviewer:** What's yours?

**Tsega:** A model, or an AI application that meets the bank's definition, that people rely on. Whether it is called a pilot doesn't change that.

**Interviewer:** Two owners on one row: would that be a finding?

**Interviewer:** If I find tools used outside the process, should I shut them down?

**Tsega:** I can't tell you what to do. I can tell you what I look for: whether management knew, what it decided, and whether the decision was recorded with its controls.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. Can I send you my inventory method before I use it?

**Tsega:** You can send it. I won't approve it. I'll read it.
