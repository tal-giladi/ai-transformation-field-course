# Transcript — Olga Petrenko (Branch manager, Haifa Hadar)

*Pre-run visit for lesson 15.2. Wednesday 20 January 2027, 08:00, Haifa Hadar branch, before opening, 30 minutes. The interviewer is the head of the AI CoE. Translated from Hebrew and Russian.*

---

**Olga:** Coffee? We have half an hour.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. Could you show me the FAQ on a real question?

**Olga:** Sit here. A customer wants to give her son power of attorney over her account. The teller asks, it shows the procedure section, the form number, the fee.

**Interviewer:** How often a day?

**Olga:** Fifteen, twenty-five questions in this branch.

**Interviewer:** Has it been wrong?

**Olga:** In November. The account-management fee changed on the first; the FAQ still had the old one until the twenty-sixth. A teller quoted the old fee. The customer complained, I refunded the difference.

**Interviewer:** Who told the people who update it?

**Olga:** I did, at the managers' forum. Before that nobody knew who updates it.

**Interviewer:** What about Tzofen? You got seats in December.

**Olga:** Seats, yes. Training, no. Most people haven't opened it.

**Interviewer:** Your customers: what languages?

**Olga:** About a third prefer Arabic or Russian. Some Amharic. The letters are only in Hebrew.

**Interviewer:** So when a customer needs a letter they can understand?

**Olga:** My deputy explains in Arabic, I explain in Russian. We do our best.

**Interviewer:** What would help most?

**Olga:** Training on Tzofen. The FAQ kept up to date. And something approved to write to customers in their language.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. I'll be back with a date for the training.

**Olga:** With a date. Good.
