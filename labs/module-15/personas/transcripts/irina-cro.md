# Transcript — Dr. Irina Kogan (CRO)

*Pre-run interview for lesson 15.2. Tuesday 12 January 2027, 10:00, the CRO's office, one hour. The interviewer is the head of the AI CoE. Translated from Hebrew.*

---

**Irina:** What do you need from Risk?

**Interviewer:** To understand the route an AI pilot takes to production today, and how much it can carry.

**Irina:** The AI policy says every AI system is a model. Every model is validated in full by Model Risk Management and approved by the Model Committee before use. That's the route.

**Interviewer:** How many people validate?

**Irina:** Yonatan Bar and two validators. About twelve review slots a quarter. A slot is roughly ten validator-days.

**Interviewer:** And how are the twelve used?

**Irina:** Six for the Directive 369 phase-in of existing non-material models. The deadline is 21 February; sixty-one are not yet compliant. Three for the annual reviews of material models. Three for AI.

**Interviewer:** And a full validation of one AI system?

**Irina:** Two slots.

**Interviewer:** So one and a half AI systems a quarter, with nineteen in the queue.

**Irina:** Two are in validation. Early-warning signals since September, the assistant since November.

**Interviewer:** After 21 February, do the phase-in slots move to AI?

**Irina:** To annual reviews. Unless I get the validator I asked for.

**Interviewer:** The minutes show five meetings in 2026. The policy says monthly.

**Irina:** There was nothing ready to decide. I don't hold a meeting to defer things.

**Interviewer:** Directive 369 talks about applying it according to the model's type, complexity and risk. Is there room for different depths of validation for, say, a letter draft that a person reads before it goes out, and a credit pre-approval model?

**Irina:** The directive allows proportionality. Our policy doesn't. I wrote the policy with the COO office in 2025. We didn't know what was coming.

**Interviewer:** Would you consider changing it?

**Irina:** It must not look like Risk lowering the bar.

**Interviewer:** If the tier rule were MRM's, high risk kept full validation, and nothing changed until the catalogue of every AI use was complete?

**Irina:** Then it is a policy proposal, and the committee can discuss it. Bring it in writing. Who owns each model?

**Interviewer:** One owner per model, in the catalogue. I've seen one pilot with two owners already.

**Irina:** That is not allowed under the directive. One owner.

**Interviewer:** Last question. Is there anything about Risk's own capacity that I should plan around?

**Irina:** The validator position. Without it, the AI slots stay at three.
