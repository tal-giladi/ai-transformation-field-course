**Interviewer:** The frame?

**Interviewer:** What does the report recommend on governance?

**Lina:** Board approval of AI use within risk appetite. An AI officer, who can be an existing officer. Validation before use and during use. The ability to stop a system. Arrangements with third parties.

**Interviewer:** The bank answered the supervisor's AI-governance survey in 2026. Can you tell me about it?

**Lina:** My unit prepared it with the COO office. The CRO and I approved it. In May the Bank of Israel published a summary: frameworks exist, gaps remain at the cross-cutting level.

**Interviewer:** If the CoE changes how AI is governed, how does that reach the supervisor?

**Lina:** Through regulatory liaison, Eitan's unit. Nothing goes to the supervisor except through us.

**Interviewer:** And the AI officer: do you see that as the CoE?

**Lina:** That is a board decision. I would want whoever it is to be someone the board can see and question.
