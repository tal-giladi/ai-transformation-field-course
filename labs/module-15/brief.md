# Engagement brief: Crescent Bank

All people, organizations and data are fictional.

## How you were brought in

You are the new **head of the AI Centre of Excellence** (ראש/ת מרכז המצוינות לבינה מלאכותית) at Crescent Bank (בנק הסהר), a mid-sized Israeli bank: about 4,000 people, 14 business units, 58 branches, about 1.1 million customers, supervised by the Bank of Israel's Banking Supervision Department. You were hired from a fintech in December and start on **Sunday 3 January 2027**. You report to **Dalit Ben-Ami, COO**, who proposed the CoE to the board.

On 24 November 2026 the CEO, Avner Golan, told the board (`data-room/company/board-minutes-2026-11-24-extract.md`):

> "We have 40 pilots and nothing in production. Fourteen units are each doing their own thing; the pilots are good, but none of them reaches the customer or the P&L."

The board approved a central CoE of 16 positions and a central AI programme of ₪11.0M for 2027. The CEO promised "at least ten in production by December 2027". On your first morning Dalit says:

> "You have the mandate and the money. I need a plan for the management committee on 15 March that gets these pilots into production, and something I can tell the board at the end of March."

## The stated ask

"We have 40 pilots and nothing in production." Dalit's version: "Get the pilots into production; tell us how AI should be run here." First recommendation to the **Management Committee on Monday 15 March 2027**; the board meets on **Tuesday 30 March** for the budget revision.

## Money and authority

| Item | What you have |
|---|---|

The COO approves spending; anything above ₪1M per commitment goes to the CEO; the Procurement Committee (CFO) for contracts |

## Who you report to

Dalit Ben-Ami (sponsor). The Management Committee decides on 15 March (CEO in the chair; COO, CFO, CRO, CIO, CCO, the general counsel and the unit heads concerned). The CRO chairs the Model Committee. The board's risk management committee expects the first quarterly AI report, including the inventory of AI in use, in Q2 2027.

## Constraints you know about

- Crescent is a banking corporation. Bank of Israel Proper Conduct of Banking Business Directives apply; among them Directive 369 on model risk management (August 2024, in force since 21 August 2025, with a phase-in for existing models), Directive 310 on risk management, 359A on outsourcing, 362 on cloud computing and 364 on IT, information security and cyber risk (in force since 18 May 2026). What each requires of a given AI use is for the CRO, the CCO and counsel to say; your job is to make sure they are asked.
- The bank told the supervisor in 2026 how it governs AI (`data-room/governance/bsd-ai-survey-response-2026-extract.md`).
- The Privacy Protection Law (חוק הגנת הפרטיות) as amended by Amendment 13 (in force since 14 August 2025) applies to customer and employee data; the DPO is Ruth Ben-David.
- Most branch, contact-centre and back-office staff are covered by collective agreements; changes in how work is done and any monitoring of employees go through HR and the employees' representatives.
- Customers and staff speak Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, English and Amharic.
