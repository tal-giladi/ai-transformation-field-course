# 01 · Mandate memo, first read and first week

*To: Dalit Ben-Ami, COO. From: head of the AI CoE. Sunday 3 January 2027, 21:00. Corrected by Dalit on Monday 4 January (her changes in italics).*

## Objective (read back)

By 15 March, bring the Management Committee one recommendation for how AI gets from pilot to production at Crescent — which pilots go, in what order, who decides, what it costs — and a 12-month plan the board can follow every quarter, *and something Avner can say to the board on 30 March*.

**The stated ask, recorded as the claim this work tests:** "We have 40 pilots and nothing in production" (CEO, board, 24 November 2026), with the remedy already chosen: a 16-person central CoE that industrialises the pilots.

## Done on 15 March

- A decision in the minutes with an owner and a date for every part.
- The 40 pilots counted, each with an action (scale, fix, merge, stop) and the evidence behind it.
- The route to production written as decision rights by risk, agreed with the CRO before it reaches the committee.
- Every number labelled measured, directional or assumed; the CFO's 15% answered with a list, not a protest.
- The board's Q2 report has an inventory behind it that internal audit could test.

## Scope

In: every AI use in the bank, not only the 43 rows (tools outside the register, AI inside vendor products, pilots in daily use); the route to production; the CoE's design and budget; the 2027 AI money. Out: model development itself, the core-banking roadmap, vendor selection for new tools (after 15 March), anything with customer data that the DPO has not approved.
