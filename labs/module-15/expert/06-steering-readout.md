# 06 · Readout, decision record and handover

*Management Committee, Monday 15 March 2027, 14:00–15:00. Chair: Avner Golan. Present: Dalit Ben-Ami, Moshe Dahan, Dr. Irina Kogan, Nir Ashkenazi, Adv. Lina Nassar, Adv. Gideon Shalev; Hagit Mor and Karim Haddad for item 3. Pre-read sent Thursday 11 March.*

## Front page

**One sentence.** Crescent's pilots stall at a gate that holds one and a half AI systems a quarter, and while they wait AI is already used without approval; so we change who decides by risk, build the catalogue and the paved roads first, run 26 of the 40 builds through the new route and stop or merge the rest — 14 in production through the approved route by March 2028, within the CFO's 15%, with the assistant kept.

**The recommendation, in parts (owner, date):**

1. AI policy v2 with decision rights and validation by risk tier; the tier rule owned by MRM (Irina; Board Risk Committee 20 April).
2. A hub-and-spoke CoE of 10 instead of a central team of 16; the head of the CoE proposed to the board as AI officer (Dalit; board 30 March).
3. Catalogue of every AI use, one owner each, by 31 May; the survey answer corrected through regulatory liaison in April (head of CoE; Lina).
4. Platform on IT's landing zone by September; evaluation kit and templates by June (Nir; head of CoE).
5. Portfolio: stop 10, merge 4, clean 3, route 26 (unit heads named in 05; head of CoE tracks).
6. Money: ₪16.57M of AI spend in 2027 against ₪20.13M; ₪3.57M saved against the CFO's ₪3.02M; the assistant kept with a usage condition (Moshe, Dalit).

**Recommend against, with the evidence:**

- **A central build team of 16.** It adds builders to the wrong side of a queue that validates 1.5 AI systems a quarter (M); the bank already has 46 people building AI in its units and IT (M); in the simulation the central design ends year one with 2 in production and year three with 23 shadow tools (educational model).
- **Cancelling the assistant.** "16% usage" is 250 of 1,600 seats activated in December; of the 380 people onboarded, 66% use it weekly, and the two pilot teams 74% and 68% (M). Two of the best-evidenced pilots run inside it (M).

**What "no" means:** the board's design stands; on 30 March the board hears "ten by December" again; the Q2 report to the board's risk committee lists the pilots and not what is used.

## Rehearsal of the hardest finding

The hardest finding is for Avner: **"nothing in production" was not true, the bank told the supervisor something that is not true, and the number he gave the board is the wrong measure.** Delivered to him alone on Thursday 11 March, with Dalit.

- First attempt (roleplay): opened with the survey answer. He heard "you misled the supervisor" and asked who wrote it.
- Second: opened with his own words from 4 February ("customers and employees using AI the bank stands behind"), then the Branch FAQ quoting an old fee to a customer in November, then what the bank needs to be able to stand behind it: a catalogue and an owner. Then the survey, framed as Lina's proposal to correct it proactively in April. He asked, "So what do I tell the board on the thirtieth?" That was the moment he owned it.
- What he will tell the board: "We counted. Twenty-six real candidates, fourteen in production by March next year through a route the CRO owns, and every AI use in the bank listed by May."
