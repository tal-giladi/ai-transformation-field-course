# Transcript — Yoav Ben-Ami, Sunday 17 January 2027

*Pre-run conversation for lesson 07.3, for learners who will not roleplay. Yoav's office, 13:00, twenty minutes, just the two of them. At 08:30 the Head of AI met Michal and agreed that the steering paper goes out with the pilot lead's numbers and her ₪500,000 as "an upside to be tested at the September gate". The paper goes to the committee at 15:00. Read it as if you were sitting in: what worked, what the interviewer missed, and what that will cost. All people are fictional.*

---

**Yoav:** Twenty minutes. Michal says the test came back at 82%. Tell me that's the headline.

**Interviewer:** I'll give you the answer first, then the numbers. The pilot works, and it will not get us to 40%. Not this year and not with this programme. What it can show the board on the 28th is about ₪113,000 a year in cash, and that number has one weak line I want you to hear from me today.

**Yoav:** That's three percent. Michal said half a million.

**Interviewer:** It's about three percent of support cost, yes. Michal's half million is what she hopes the programme could reach if drafts work on every channel. Nobody has measured that. I'd rather you hear the measured number from me now than from Ronit's questions in the meeting.

**Yoav:** And the 82%?

**Interviewer:** Real, and on our own emails: 82% of drafts on 400 messages from last summer were good enough to send with no or light edits. Two things it isn't. It isn't live traffic; the agents start on 1 February. And it isn't every language equally: Arabic came in at 72%, which passes, but only just.

**Yoav:** Fine. So what's the weak line?

**Interviewer:** The cash comes from two places: we stop paying for the translation service, about ₪17,000, which is certain, and we don't hire the two winter temps, ₪96,000. Today's evidence covers one temp, not two. If April shows the same, the case comes back to the committee with one temp before the rest of the money is spent.

**Yoav:** So the September story is… what? We spent a hundred and twenty thousand to save forty-eight?

**Interviewer:** Then the honest story is what the gates are for: we find out in April with ₪48,000 spent, not in December with everything spent. And the hours the drafts free are real. The team's volume doubled between May and October. That's capacity we don't have to hire, even when it isn't cash.

**Yoav:** The board doesn't vote on capacity.

**Interviewer:** No. Which is why I wouldn't put capacity in the money line. Can I give you three ways to tell the board?

**Yoav:** Go.

**Interviewer:** One: "We found the cause of the complaints in thirty days and fixed it without AI. The AI pilot starts next month with numbers we measured ourselves: about ₪113,000 a year in cash, with a gate in April. The 40% was a vendor's figure; we replaced it with ours." Two: keep 40% as a long-term ambition, clearly labelled as not a forecast, next to the measured case. Three: present only the date fix on the 28th and the AI case in April with live numbers.

**Yoav:** Two sounds like what I need.

**Interviewer:** It's the riskiest for you. Orna asked in September whether 40% was a target or a forecast. If it's on the slide next to ₪113,000, she'll ask again, and the answer will be "neither".

**Yoav:** She will. *(pause)* She'll ask exactly that. And three makes me look like I've got nothing.

**Interviewer:** Then one. And I'll help you write the slide so that every number on it is one Ronit has signed.

**Yoav:** "We replaced a vendor's number with our own." I can say that. Is Michal wrong, then?

**Interviewer:** Michal's number is an upside nobody has tested. We agreed this morning that it goes in the paper as exactly that: an assumption we test at the September gate. She's comfortable with it.

**Yoav:** Good. I don't want the two of you fighting in front of Ronit.

**Interviewer:** You won't see that. In the meeting I'll lead with the ask, the ₪113,000 and the weak line, and ask for the decision with conditions. If someone says a bigger number, I'll label it the same way.

**Yoav:** Send me the slide by Wednesday. And the paper goes out as you wrote it.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. 15:00, as written, with Michal's upside labelled. Slide on Wednesday.

**Yoav:** Done.

— *end of conversation* —

---

*Notes for the reader.* The interviewer gave the answer in the first two sentences, stated the finding once, kept measured and hoped-for apart, offered three options and argued against the one Yoav reached for, and left with a sentence Yoav owns and a date. What the interviewer never asked: who has already heard a number from Yoav since December. Asked privately, Yoav would have admitted that on 10 January he told his father, who chairs the board, that "the pilot already shows 82%" and that the 40% was "on track for 2028". The board slide is now fine; the chair still expects 40%. Lesson 07.3's reference answer shows the question that finds it, and what to agree about it.
