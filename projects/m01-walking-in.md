# Walking in — Harbor & Pine

The module 1 practical: the complete set of documents you produce in your first two weeks at Harbor & Pine, assembled into one entry pack your sponsor could approve. Most of the pieces start as your answers to lessons 01.1–01.7; the practical asks you to finish them, make them agree with each other, and hold them to the standard a COO, a CFO and a board would apply. Plan 5 hours if you completed the lessons' "Your turn" tasks, longer if you did not.

## The brief

It is Thursday 12 November 2026, the end of your second week as Head of AI and Operations Improvement at Harbor & Pine Ltd. (120 people, furniture and home goods, Rishon LeZion). Michal Peretz, the COO and your sponsor, meets the CEO, Yoav Ben-Ami, on Sunday to prepare the December review. She asks you for "everything you've set up, in one place, so I can show Yoav we have a grip on this — and tell me what needs a decision from him".

You have the Harbor & Pine data room, the persona pack and everything you wrote in this module. The board meets on 28 January 2027; the CEO has promised it an AI customer-service bot, an AI strategy and 40% lower support cost.

## Steps

1. **Collect** your lesson outputs for deliverables 1–6. Where you skipped a "Your turn", do it now; the lessons' reference answers show the standard but are not yours to copy.
2. **Make them agree.** Check that the same dates, numbers and names appear everywhere: the board date and pre-read, the budget and thresholds, the owners in the inventory and the census, the interim-rule decision date in the plan and in the census. List every inconsistency you fix.
3. **Trace every number to a source.** Each number in the pack names a file in the data room or the person who said it. Mark persona-only facts ("Michal said…") as such.
4. **Check the boundary against the findings.** Your census and inventory found things in marketing and HR. Does your scope boundary say how they are handled (in, out or read-only, with a reason)? Update it and log any change as a change request.
5. **Write the cover note** (one page): what is now in place; the three most important findings so far, each with its evidence; the three decisions you need from Yoav, each with options and your recommendation; what you will bring on 10 December.
6. **Self-assess** with the rubric below, then run the [coach prompt](../labs/common/coach-prompt.md) on the cover note with the rubric as the checklist.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| Objective and mandate | The objective is the sponsor's quotation with a read-back date; money, thresholds and authority each cite a source; the bot is recorded as a means | The objective is paraphrased into consultant language; budget or authority is assumed |
| Commercial shape | The SOW outline has phases with exits, acceptance criteria per deliverable, a priced Phase 0 in ₪ plus VAT with its approval band | One open-ended engagement; a price with no model or approval route |
| Scope boundary | Every line has a reason from the objective or a finding and a review date where "not now"; acknowledged by the sponsor and the owners of other areas | Lists of topics with no reasons; acknowledged by nobody |
| Change control | Every request is logged with refinement/expansion, decision, reason and date; nothing has disappeared | Requests accepted or refused in conversation with no record |
| 30-60-90 | Each block has a question and ends in a dated decision by a named person; peak season and the pre-read respected | Activities with no decisions; a solution named in week one |
| Data-room read | Every narrative number has actor, source, date and one of four statuses; contradictions recorded plainly | Narrative numbers quoted as facts; "verified" because two documents agree |
| Access requests | Minimal fields, purpose, approver, storage, deletion date and a legal-check line on each | "Send everything" or an admin login |
| Shadow-AI census | Four or more sources; a host list; reported by department; data-in and terms rated; the interim rule has an approved alternative and an amnesty | Category filter only; named individuals; a ban with no alternative |
| AI inventory | A counting rule; excluded systems with reasons; AI from feature updates found; each row owned by the using team; the decision each AI shapes | Only chatbots listed; "AI" labels accepted; IT owns everything |
| Routing | Legal, labour and privacy questions routed to named people with what happens meanwhile | Legal conclusions stated as fact, or questions left open with no owner |
| Cover note | One page; findings with evidence; decisions framed with options and a recommendation | A status report with no decision asked for |
